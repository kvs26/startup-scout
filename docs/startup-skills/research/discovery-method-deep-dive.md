# How to improve open startup discovery

Reviewed 2026-10-17. This note synthesizes original author material, YC video transcripts, twelve published agent skills, and first-party descriptions of research sources. The broader [40-source inspiration review](../../../IDEA-SCOUT-INSPIRATION.md) has the source-by-source inventory. Here the question is narrower: **why did our scout reject many ideas without finding a strong one, and what should it do differently on its next run?** Method advice below is not evidence that any particular startup idea will succeed.

## What “study a buyer's work” means

Take a historical example, not a current opportunity. [Intercom cofounder Des Traynor's account](https://www.intercom.com/blog/podcasts/intercom-co-founder-des-traynor-on-starting-up/) describes a repeated job at his earlier SaaS company: export paying subscriptions from PayPal, import them into an email service, ask users questions, then sort replies without knowing reliably whether each person was paying, trialing or gone. The founders first built small pieces to see and message users in context; that became Intercom. The useful unit of discovery was the **job and its failed handoffs**, not the broad category “customer messaging software.” The case came from founder experience and later real users; a scout can reconstruct part of a different buyer's job from public artifacts, but must say where direct observation is still missing. [Paul Graham](https://www.paulgraham.com/start.html) explicitly recommends watching how nontechnical firms use computers because many of the problems are invisible from a software category search.

For this scout, studying a buyer means identifying an organization type and role, the trigger for a recurring task, its inputs, sequence, people and systems, decision points, exceptions, output, cost of failure, and present workaround. An [O*NET task page](https://www.onetonline.org/) or job description can suggest tasks; it does not prove one is painful. [GOV.UK's discovery guidance](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/start-by-learning-user-needs) similarly asks who users are, what they are trying to do, how they do it now, and where they struggle. The scout chooses the group and does this legwork; the founder does not need to select the market first.

## Diagnosis of our actual rounds

The [India round](../../../scouting-runs/2026-10-17-msme-payables.md) and [global round](../../../scouting-runs/2026-10-17-global-search.md) touched 17 directions, but many passes relied on short searches, inaccessible Reddit bodies, vendor descriptions and snippets. They started largely from regulations, investor interests or product topics. A cheap existing service or built-in feature often ended the search before anyone had mapped what happened before and after that feature for one buyer. The negative conclusions were sometimes plausible, but the coverage did not support treating whole buyer groups as exhausted. A single original workflow within one group received less attention than the collection of market topics.

This matches a warning in [YC partner Jon Xu's video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R56RJFZBasQ) and [library page](https://www.ycombinator.com/library/Ri-how-to-pick-a-startup-idea): shallow sampling across several ideas produces weak data and can both reject a good direction and retain a bad one. His standard of knowing a customer's daily crises is intentionally demanding. For an agent scout, the practical version is a bounded, substantive pass on one buyer group's work before switching; it does not ask the founder to commit a company to that group at this stage.

The previous rubric also risked conflating three different findings: **this specific job is adequately served**, **the pain cannot be observed through available public sources**, and **a buyer's budget or switching choice is private**. These require different responses. The first can justify leaving an idea. The second calls for a different source or an authorized observation. The third can still leave a defensible candidate for deeper customer investigation when the problem and gap are well supported.

## What the primary methods add

| Method | Concrete lesson for the scout | Limit to respect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Paul Graham on startup ideas](https://www.paulgraham.com/startupideas.html) | Look for a narrow group that urgently needs something and a plausible route to adjacent groups. Competition can confirm a real need; inspect whether a specific group remains underserved. | A hypothetical growth route does not establish a large market or a reason to switch. |
| [YC on evaluating ideas and talking to users](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/startup-school-week-1-recap-kevin-hale-and-eric-migicovsky/) | Ask about the last incident, why it was hard, what the person did, and what they disliked about existing options. Urgency, expense, frequency, obligation and growth are signals; a candidate need not satisfy all five. | A user story supports that person's experience, not prevalence or willingness to buy. |
| [Rob Fitzpatrick's *Mom Test* teaching material](https://www.momtestbook.com/teachers) and [Steve Blank](https://steveblank.com/2009/11/30/customer-development-is-not-a-focus-group/) | Test beliefs about behavior and purchase instead of asking whether an imagined feature sounds appealing. | Public posts rarely contain the follow-up needed to test actual commitment. |
| [Bob Moesta's Jobs to Be Done guide](https://therewiredgroup.com/learn/complete-guide-jobs-to-be-done/) | Study a recent switch: what triggered it, what the buyer gave up, what held them back, and what progress they sought. | Existing spend alone does not imply a buyer will leave the incumbent. |
| [Teresa Torres on sourcing opportunities](https://www.producttalk.org/sourcing-opportunities/) and [solution testing](https://www.producttalk.org/discovering-solutions/) | Capture a specific customer's story and circumstances first; test a proposed solution's assumptions separately. | A complaint is an opportunity to investigate, not a product specification. |
| [Strategyn on customer needs](https://strategyn.com/outcome-driven-innovation/understanding-customer-needs/) | For each job step, name the measurable outcome the buyer wants and whether the current option satisfies it. | Its numeric opportunity score needs measured customer ratings, not search snippets. |
| [Strategyzer's evidence guide](https://www.strategyzer.com/library/business-project-succcess) | Judge evidence separately for customer problem, solution desirability, feasible delivery and payment. | More interviews do not turn stated opinions into observed purchasing behavior. |
| [MIT delta v customer evaluation material](https://entrepreneurship.mit.edu/wp-content/uploads/MIT-delta-v-2024-Info-Session-Presentation.pdf) | Separate end user, economic buyer, champion, influencers and veto holders. | A role map is a hypothesis until a real purchase process is examined. |

## A repeatable scout pass

1. **Select a group autonomously.** Screen possible role and company-size combinations for fit with the founder, accessible original material, a credible written contact route, and a plausible expansion path. Choose one for depth. A professional directory is a route to investigate, not an established relationship. [Product Talk's recruitment examples](https://www.producttalk.org/finding-interview-participants-before-customers/) show that generic messages can fail even when people are identifiable.
2. **Map the job.** Use occupational tasks, relevant company documents, templates and product help pages to reconstruct a recurring event from trigger to usable output. Mark gaps in the map; do not fill them with confident prose. Inspect several tasks within the same group to discover a more consequential one.
3. **Find original incidents.** Search full reviews, specialist communities, issue threads, public buyer documents and contractor requests around that event. Record role, date, exact failure, current response, consequence, replies and outcome. Follow duplicates to one underlying case. Original incident accounts are more informative than complaint counts.
4. **Challenge the gap at the exact step.** Inspect the incumbent's current function, plan tier, setup, integrations and exception handling, alongside internal staff, service providers and doing nothing. A [HubSpot user thread](https://community.hubspot.com/t/cross-object-workflow/22426) documents field-copy workarounds through February 2025, while [current HubSpot documentation](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/edit-records-using-workflows) describes some cross-object copying in Professional and Enterprise editions. Neither alone settles every use case; the scout must check the particular buyer and step before claiming an open gap or a solved one.
5. **Trace buyer value and access.** State who suffers the failure, who pays, what the present cost or spending is, why a switch might occur, and how to reach that role in writing. A posted job is intended spend; a public contract award is stronger evidence of a specific buyer's spending, not private-market demand. [UK Contracts Finder's documented notice stages](https://www.contractsfinder.service.gov.uk/apidocumentation) make that distinction visible.
6. **Test the strongest counterexplanation.** Perhaps an included feature solves it, a setup change fixes it, the failure is rare, the real cost is low, a service is preferred, the target buyer cannot buy, or this founder cannot deliver a credible first version. Seek sources that could overturn the emerging thesis before writing a product pitch.
7. **Make one calibrated decision.** Present one specific candidate when the problem, remaining gap, buyer and founder-deliverable first version are defensible. Show confidence claim by claim and state exactly what customer observation would test payment and switching. If none survives, report whether the cause is an adequately served job, weak original evidence, source access, unclear payer, delivery, or unfinished diligence; change the next search accordingly.

The scout should use agents for **distinct questions about the same workflow**, such as original incidents, current alternatives, and payer/access, then verify the decisive sources itself. Twelve inspected public agent skills support pieces of this process—for example [idea-finder](https://github.com/zhenya-vlasov/idea-finder/blob/main/skills/idea-finder/SKILL.md) starts with founder context, [first-customer-finder](https://github.com/carolinacherry/claude-first-customer-finder-skill/blob/main/skills/first-customer-finder/SKILL.md) checks original prospect signals, and [startup-idea](https://github.com/fabricioctelles/skills/blob/main/skills/startup-idea/SKILL.md) includes manual substitutes and switching. Their scores and fixed thresholds are not evidence that the methods work; three agents scoring the same snippet are still using one weak observation.

## What a recommendation can honestly mean

The scout's job is to find the **best publicly defensible opportunity for the founder to investigate**, not to promise that a business will succeed. Confidence can be high that a particular job and failure exist, moderate that an incumbent leaves a consequential gap, and unknown that this buyer will pay a new supplier. Those are separate statements. The founder's subsequent deep research should target the narrowest missing claim—often a recent-workflow walkthrough, comparison with the current tool, or a real buying step—rather than repeat the whole desk search. No outreach or purchase is implied by a research recommendation.

## Independent forward test of the revised instructions

An independent scout run applied the revised skill to the founder's global B2B brief without using this research note or the earlier scouting reports. It chose two buyer workflows. Small B2B software vendors described repeated security questionnaires, but [Knowally's advertised free tier](https://knowally.com/) already covers the proposed answer-drafting and spreadsheet-export job; the run found no original buyer account showing an important remaining failure after that substitute. US property managers described missed insurance-certificate expiry and spreadsheet work, but [TrustLayer Starter](https://www.trustlayer.io/pages/trustlayer-starter-free-vendor-compliance-tracking-ga) advertises free tracking and reminders for the small tier. The run therefore recommended no idea. That is a useful rejection of two narrow offers, not proof that either buyer group has no open problem.

The test also exposed a handoff failure: both directions had appeared in the prior global search, which the test deliberately did not read. The skill now requires consulting recent relevant scouting runs before selecting a segment, and recording the new buyer, task step or evidence that justifies any revisit. A validator confirms document structure; this behavioral test shows the revised instructions still need a real future discovery round to demonstrate that they can surface a strong candidate. We should judge that round by whether it finds an original consequential failure and a credible reason to switch, not by whether it is forced to output an idea.
