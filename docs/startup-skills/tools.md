# Research tools and access

Check available capabilities at the start of a run. The repository's [SETUP.md](../../SETUP.md) records configured status and dated checks; a selected service or an account is not proof of a working connection. The [setup ticket](../../.scratch/startup-skills/issues/13-guide-tool-setup.md) holds the setup history. Runtime research can use the fallbacks below without reading that history.

| Need | Route | Limits and fallback |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discover customer problems and alternatives | Available web search; Tavily search when configured | Use task/customer/local-language queries and sources beyond idea lists. Quota or tool failure is an access gap, not absent demand. |
| Read original sources and follow relevant links | Page reading; supported Tavily extraction/crawl when configured | Inspect relevant replies/resolutions. Partial extraction, login walls and failed links remain explicit. Use another accessible original source or interactive browsing when it can help. Selective reads do not establish comprehensive crawling. |
| Save and resume research | Local Markdown files using the shared record guide | Preserve original evidence and past reports; read current records before updating derived views. |
| Find organizations and business contact routes | Public company sites, relevant associations/events/directories | Match the market and customer. A listing is neither a named buyer nor proven demand. |
| Look up or verify professional contacts | Hunter/Apollo only when the actual free capability and remaining allowance are verified | Search, revealing contact details, enrichment and verification are different capabilities. Preserve public/vendor-supplied/inferred/unknown status and source/storage limits. Fall back to published company contact routes. |
| Human-assisted professional discovery | User's authorized LinkedIn/Sales Navigator access when relevant | No general people-search API or scraping dependency is assumed; a subscription does not establish integration access. |

Tavily is the selected supporting search/extraction/crawl integration. Its setup status is separate from whether built-in search works. If unavailable, disclose the gap and use available research routes, qualifying any affected coverage. Do not claim a Tavily check from a built-in web call. Enable only operations actually exposed and checked in the current environment.

Use verified included/free allowances for selected services. Do not infer endpoint access or free enrichment from a free website account; do not hard-code quotas as permanent guarantees. If allowance/access cannot be established or is exhausted, use public-source fallbacks and record the limit rather than enable paid overages, upgrade, or purchase credits. Account setup or credential changes follow the user's existing setup instructions, not an implicit scouting step. Keep secrets out of records and output.

Hunter and Apollo are conditional contact aids, not prerequisites for scouting. Additional crawlers, research agents, Places APIs, paid directories, CRM and sending tools have not been selected for this version. A demonstrated unmet need can justify proposing a tool later. No legacy scripts or integrations are required by these skills.

For access failure, record the source/tool attempted, check date, observed failure or partial result, affected claim, useful fallback and remaining gap. Distinguish unavailable, unattempted and searched-with-no-relevant-results. When all live research is unavailable, use dated saved/user-provided evidence, save an explicitly limited round and identify the live work still needed.

Contact data must preserve source-specific retention limits and uncertainty; consult current provider terms before adopting a new data route. A delivered email, a job title and a willingness to speak are different facts. Research and preparation do not authorize sending messages, campaigns, purchases or live customer experiments.
