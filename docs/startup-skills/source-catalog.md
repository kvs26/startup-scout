# Shared starting resources for startup scouting

Checked: **2026-10-03**. This is the shared runtime starting-source catalog, selected in [Decide how the scout discovers and compares open opportunities](../../.scratch/startup-skills/issues/07-decide-scout-workflow.md). It builds on [discovery-source research](../../.scratch/startup-skills/issues/03-research-discovery-coverage.md) and [reusable-toolkit research](../../.scratch/startup-skills/issues/12-research-reusable-startup-toolkit.md).

This is a starting menu, not an exhaustive catalog, approved vendor stack, or checklist every run must complete. The scope is software, AI, and similar technology serving any industry and geography. Choose sources around the customer and question; add useful sources found during research. The examples below demonstrate retrieval and source roles, not selected startup ideas.

**Independent discovery comes first:** search and read the wider web and relevant public communities using customer roles, tasks, frustrations, workarounds, and local terms. These searches must be able to find problems absent from curated lists. YC prompts and idea lists supplement that work; following only their links is insufficient.

## How to use and maintain this list

- Start with ordinary web search and selective page/link reads. The checks below used those tools without accounts or new installations. They establish access to sampled pages, not exhaustive crawling, whole-site coverage, API access, or continuing access guarantees. No integration is adopted by this file.
- Follow a result to its original account and read relevant replies, dates, corrections, workarounds, and linked resolutions. Search explicitly for satisfactory alternatives and reasons the proposed solution might be unnecessary. A first-person public account is a reported experience, not a verified identity or verified purchase.
- Distinguish method advice, expert prompts, customer/problem reports, advertised offers, official market context, and supplier inputs. Popularity, repeated complaints, a large population, and an investor's interest do not establish paid demand for the proposed opportunity.
- Record source URL, relevant statement, publication/event period, check date, market/population, and access state with the opportunity evidence. Deduplicate underlying incidents and sources, including several list entries or cross-posts derived from one discussion.
- Check geography, currency, billing period, tax, version, and eligibility before using prices or product claims. Vendor documentation establishes documented capability; runtime quality and actual customer costs need separate evidence.
- Use relevant regional languages and local names for occupations and workflows. English business/technology communities cannot represent all customers. For an unfamiliar market, find its own practitioner communities, statistical office, industry bodies, public-buyer records, and local alternatives. Check translation meaning when it changes the claim.
- Keep a compact record of searched directions and omitted or inaccessible populations. Distinguish no relevant results from a source failure, login wall, partial extraction, or an unattempted source. None means that demand is absent. Where public sources cannot answer the question, state the missing observation or customer test rather than inventing it.
- Add relevant resources as they are discovered, with the same role, limits, access state, and check date. Recheck resources when used; update broken links and changed access. Keep this menu small by linking opportunity-specific material from that opportunity's evidence instead of expanding the shared list indefinitely.

## Independent problem accounts and discussions

### Reddit: relevant practitioner and customer communities

**Job and use:** Search communities around the actual customer or activity, including communities outside startup-building. Read original incidents and replies to find manual work, unmet needs, existing solutions, and objections. Checked example: [a small auction business's inventory/invoicing discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/smallbusiness/comments/1mecoxb/small_business_needs_inventoryinvoicing/), found through independent problem searches.

**Limits:** This thread includes both product suggestions and a proposal to simplify the existing spreadsheet process. Posts and replies may be promotional; identities, locations, and spending claims were not verified. Accessible English posts are a selective sample, not a measure of prevalence.

**Access checked 2026-10-03:** Original post and replies readable through search and page opening without login. Deleted replies are mentioned within the conversation. Other communities, pagination, private groups, and API/bulk access were not tested.

### Frappe Forum: operational software and regional workflows

**Job and use:** Search [ERPNext discussions](https://discuss.frappe.io/c/erpnext/6) for accounting, inventory, operations, and regional needs. Checked [India Compliance discussion about branch expense allocation](https://discuss.frappe.io/t/input-service-distributor-anyone-implemented-working-on-it/161555) illustrates a reported missing workflow, an alternative suggested in replies, and a link to development work.

**Limits:** Represents people using or evaluating that product, including implementers. A feature request may be a configuration, training, or already-addressed issue rather than a separate business. The discussion's regulatory claims require official verification before use.

**Access checked 2026-10-03:** Category and complete short discussion readable without login. Its linked GitHub development issue failed to open in this check; current implementation status remains unverified. No forum/API integration tested.

### Hacker News: original discussions, with a technology bias

**Job and use:** [Ask HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/ask) and independently searched HN threads can reveal workflow accounts, technical constraints, and contradictory experiences. Checked [discussion about obtaining early user feedback](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=30186545) contains original accounts and replies. Use domain-targeted web search to reach relevant threads; [Algolia HN Search](https://hn.algolia.com/) is a possible interface to try when available.

**Limits:** Strong technology/founder and English-language skew. Advice, discussion attention, and self-reported outcomes are not verified customer demand. An old discussion needs a current check.

**Access checked 2026-10-03:** Ask page and sample thread readable. Algolia's page returned only a shell in text extraction, so interactive search and API capability were not tested. No installation needed for the readable pages.

### Stack Exchange: specific problems and attempted answers

**Job and use:** Choose a relevant specialist community rather than assuming Stack Overflow covers all work. [The Workplace questions](https://workplace.stackexchange.com/questions) and [sample knowledge-transfer question](https://workplace.stackexchange.com/questions/203604/making-knowledge-transfer-without-giving-your-personal-notes) provide concrete questions, context, and proposed responses. Follow tags and original questions to learn customer language and workarounds.

**Limits:** This example skews toward office work and English-speaking participants. Accepted answers and votes indicate community response, not verified effectiveness or buying intent. Many problems are organizational rather than software opportunities.

**Access checked 2026-10-03:** Question index and sample question/answers opened without login. Other communities, filter interactions, and bulk/API access were not tested.

### GitHub issues: product gaps and current resolution status

**Job and use:** Search repositories for products relevant to a workflow. [ERPNext issues](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/issues) and a checked [milestone-invoicing request](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/issues/59726) show the reported situation, requested behavior, and alternatives considered. Inspect linked issues, maintainer replies, fixes, and released versions before presenting a gap as current.

**Limits:** Repository participants are selective and often technical. Several issue links can describe one underlying problem. A requested feature is not a purchase commitment, and an open issue does not prove the capability is absent everywhere.

**Access checked 2026-10-03:** Public index and sample issue body/status readable. Reactions were unavailable in the extracted sample. Other links, full history, APIs, and authenticated access were not tested.

### Trustpilot: experiences with existing alternatives

**Job and use:** Use reviews to find specific friction, reasons to switch, and reasons customers remain satisfied. Checked [Zoho review page](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.zoho.com) exposes review text, dates, displayed country markers, ratings, and review labels. Follow a relevant individual review when recording evidence.

**Limits:** The checked page mixes products within a brand, so a complaint about one product must not be assigned to another. Reviewer identity, location, transaction, and truthfulness were not independently verified. Self-selection and potential promotion limit conclusions; ratings do not measure a proposed startup's demand.

**Access checked 2026-10-03:** Sample review page and displayed review text readable without login. Individual-review retrieval, filters, pagination, and bulk access were not tested. Other review sites are not assumed accessible.

## Supplementary prompts and startup-method guidance

### Y Combinator Requests for Startups

**Job and use:** [YC RFS](https://www.ycombinator.com/rfs) offers expert prompts and terminology to widen possible directions. The checked page displays Fall 2026 and previous editions. Follow promising prompts into independent customer research.

**Limits:** Expresses contributor/investor interests, with substantial AI emphasis and some US-specific and hardware proposals. Apply the agreed software/AI/similar-technology scope; do not inherit YC's investment preferences or treat its interest as evidence that customers will buy.

**Access checked 2026-10-03:** Current text readable without login. Previous-edition controls were visible but not exercised. No content reuse license was established; cite and summarize rather than assuming dataset reuse rights.

### Startup Ideas by fayerman-source

**Job and use:** [Repository and source-linked ideas](https://github.com/fayerman-source/startup-ideas) provide supplementary leads. The checked page identifies September 2026 as its current edition, links originating discussions, and retains earlier editions. Follow the original source before assessing a lead.

**Limits:** Editorial choices and Reddit sampling shape coverage. Entries are proposed solutions, not independently validated businesses; multiple ideas can reference the same thread. Do not inherit its solo-founder preferences, scores, or market assumptions.

**Access checked 2026-10-03:** Repository text, source links, edition links, and a JSON download link visible without login. This follow-up did not download/import the dataset or test the separate 1mil.app service. The repository labels its content MIT; that does not establish rights over linked third-party discussions.

### YC Startup School: how to learn from users

**Job and use:** The official [Startup School recap with Eric Migicovsky](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/startup-school-week-1-recap-kevin-hale-and-eric-migicovsky/) discusses listening, specific experiences, and existing attempts to solve a problem. Use it as method guidance for assessing the specificity of accounts and preparing later customer questions.

**Limits:** Advice is not opportunity-specific evidence. Startup guidance cannot establish the customer's problem, budget, purchasing authority, or willingness to participate. Reading it does not authorize outreach.

**Access checked 2026-10-03:** Official recap text opened. The separate [How to Talk to Users library page](https://www.ycombinator.com/library/6g-how-to-talk-to-users) returned an error; the readable official recap is the verified text route. No course enrollment or video playback tested.

## Geographic, industry, and buyer context

### India: MoSPI ASUSE, through the official release

**Job and use:** The [official ASUSE 2025 release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2258362&lang=1&reg=3), published 6 May 2026, describes establishments, employment, business activities, and technology use for the January–December 2025 reference period. Use this route to notice customer populations poorly represented in online founder communities, then seek relevant original workflow evidence.

**Limits:** This release covers unincorporated non-agricultural manufacturing, trade, and services excluding construction. It is not a complete business census, buyer directory, or software-demand estimate. Distinguish establishment counts from people and potential paying customers; check the selected survey's scope and reference period.

**Access checked 2026-10-03:** PIB release and endnote readable. MoSPI's current-surveys page appeared in search but direct opening returned only a shell. Detailed report, microdata, and interactive tables were linked/described, not downloaded or tested.

### World Bank Enterprise Surveys

**Job and use:** [Methodology and survey routes](https://www.enterprisesurveys.org/en/methodology) help choose country/industry context and interpret firm-reported operations and constraints. Formal, informal, and micro surveys provide different coverage; select the population matching the question.

**Limits:** Formal surveys generally cover eligible registered firms with at least five workers; micro surveys cover one to four. Informal surveys cover defined areas, typically urban centers. Country, sector, year, sample, and survey version matter. Reported obstacles are research leads, not proof of demand for a particular product.

**Access checked 2026-10-03:** Methods page readable. Questionnaires and a login-domain data portal are linked. Dataset access, account requirements in practice, and country-level downloads were not tested.

### EU and wider European public procurement: TED

**Job and use:** [TED](https://ted.europa.eu/en/about-ted) describes public procurement notices searchable by country, region, and business sector. Use buyer-authored requirements and award-stage records, when retrieved, to investigate concrete needs beyond online technology discussions.

**Limits:** Public buyers are a particular population. Notice publication is not an award or payment; terms, eligibility, contract scale, and incumbent arrangements need checking. TED describes procurement information in 24 official EU languages; do not assume every attachment is translated.

**Access checked 2026-10-03:** Official information page readable and describes free access. Notice search, actual notices/attachments, registration features, translations, and APIs were not tested in this pass.

### India public procurement: eProcurement

**Job and use:** [Government of India eProcurement](https://eprocure.gov.in/eprocure/app) exposes current notices and links to organization/location categories, corrigenda, and awards. Choose relevant software or operational requirements and inspect the original buyer documents.

**Limits:** It does not represent all Indian private demand or every government channel. Physical procurement can reveal context for supporting technology, but physical businesses themselves remain outside the agreed scope. Notice stage and supplier eligibility matter before inferring a reachable customer.

**Access checked 2026-10-03:** Landing-page listings and links readable; the page states schedules can be downloaded free. Detailed search, attachments, awards, and bidder access were not tested. A JavaScript-disabled warning appears in extraction.

## Alternatives, delivery constraints, and economic inputs

### First-party alternatives and prices: Zoho Books India example

**Job and use:** Find each relevant alternative's own product documentation, pricing, eligibility, and regional terms. Checked [Zoho Books India pricing](https://www.zoho.com/in/books/pricing/) supplies features, billing units, usage limits, and free/paid plans. This is an example of the route, not a required competitor for every opportunity.

**Limits:** Advertised capabilities and prices do not prove actual use, customer satisfaction, realized costs, or willingness to pay for a new offer. Include existing manual processes, services, and doing nothing among alternatives. Check the customer's own country and plan rather than generalizing Indian pricing worldwide.

**Access checked 2026-10-03:** Pricing and FAQ text readable without login, including monthly/annual billing, local-tax exclusion, and plan limits. No signup, feature execution, quote, or payment tested. Refresh numeric prices when used rather than freezing them in this menu.

### First-party technical and operating-cost inputs: AWS Lambda example

**Job and use:** Select supplier documentation for the proposed delivery approach. Checked [Lambda pricing](https://aws.amazon.com/lambda/pricing/) and [Lambda quotas](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/lambda/latest/dg/gettingstarted-limits.html) illustrate how to find charging dimensions and execution constraints. For another architecture or AI provider, find its own current pricing, limits, supported markets, and relevant technical documentation.

**Limits:** Supplier documentation is evidence of published terms and constraints, not benchmarked quality or a complete cost model. Usage, surrounding services, data movement, support, human operations, and customer acquisition require separate estimates with stated assumptions. This example does not select AWS or serverless delivery.

**Access checked 2026-10-03:** Official pricing and quota text readable without login. Region selectors, calculators, accounts, runtime behavior, and a workload-specific bill were not tested.

## Coverage and access cautions to carry forward

The menu combines original discussions, reviews, prompts, official market/buyer context, and first-party offers. Its directly checked discussion examples still skew toward English-speaking and digitally active people. India-specific discussion and statistics, international survey methods, and multilingual procurement routes help broaden research; they do not close all language, rural, informal-sector, consumer, or specialist-industry gaps. Discover the relevant local sources when a research direction requires them.

US statistics remain an adaptive route rather than a fixed current-data claim: the checked [Census County Business Patterns About page](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/cbp/about.html) explicitly warns that its information is no longer current while disclosure guidance is reconsidered. Before using CBP, inspect the chosen release and current guidance; employer-establishment data alone also omits nonemployers. This caution updates the earlier discovery research's access snapshot.

The available search/read tools were enough to independently find and read sampled problem accounts, replies, product information, and official context. Missing extraction from some interfaces and one failed development link remain real gaps. A new crawler or research service should be evaluated against a demonstrated retrieval need; this list neither installs one nor assumes it would unlock inaccessible content.
