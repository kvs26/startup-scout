Source: the user's answers to the eleven interview questions in this conversation. These are self-reports; friends' experiences are reported through the user, not separate interviews.

- **Main trigger and problem:** whenever the cook is absent, deciding what to order becomes time-consuming. Comparing unfamiliar meals creates doubts about taste, stomach comfort, calories and price. No diagnosis, allergy, calorie target or protein target was supplied. Do not infer that a dish will be medically safe or classify a sensitivity as an allergy.
- **Consequences and current behavior:** time, money and frustration. No numeric decision-time baseline or spending amount was given. The reported approach is scrolling Zomato/Swiggy; no other attempted workaround was reported. Avoid repeatedly asking the same broad question about alternatives.
- **Friends:** the user says friends describe the same frustration from daily delivery browsing, especially when they have no cook, and concerns that much restaurant food is unhealthy. Friend count, individual incidents and spending are unknown; the health characterization is their concern, not an audited statement about restaurants.
