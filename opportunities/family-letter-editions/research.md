# Family letter editions — research

Current claim assessment: 2026-10-11  
Scope: Owners of substantial inherited letter/diary collections seeking a readable edition. English family accounts and one French genealogy discussion; no representative country sample.

## C001

**Claim:** Some family archive owners find transcription, checking and preparing readable collections burdensome.

**Assessment and confidence:** Supported for the reported projects, with moderate confidence in the existence of the task. Prevalence, urgency and purchasing frequency remain unknown. [E001](#e001) includes a promotional original author, separate commenters and satisfied manual/tool users. [E002](#e002) corroborates difficult handwriting in a different community, but concerns historical acts rather than family editions.

**Contrary evidence:** Some owners enjoy transcription. Others successfully finish with existing tools ([E001](#e001), [E003](#e003), [E004](#e004)).

**Next useful check:** Examine a recent unfinished collection: what is scanned, the desired output, what stopped completion, and whether its owner wants help.

## C002

**Claim:** A finished, checked and selectively edited edition could be preferable to buying another transcription app.

**Assessment and confidence:** Specific but unresolved. One commenter wants selection help with six scanned volumes and mentions being able to afford the project ([E001](#e001)); this is interest without a quote, transaction or verified budget. Software and human transcription providers already exist ([E003](#e003)–[E005](#e005), [E007](#e007), [E008](#e008), [E009](#e009)).

**Proposed distinction, an inference:** Deliver a completed collection with source-page references, uncertain readings marked, an index and owner-approved selection. Keep selection separate from the full transcript to retain the underlying archive. Final checks found managed archive transcription, a family-book platform with human services, and professional memoir editing/publishing ([E007](#e007)–[E009](#e009)). Completion and source-linked editions are therefore not established missing features. A particular collection, relationship or delivery approach would need a concrete reason to win.

**Next useful check:** Compare the desired output with an existing provider's actual proposal. Establish what a new provider would do better and whether the owner values that difference.

## C003

**Claim:** A scoped project service is a plausible starting model, but human work could dominate cost.

**Assessment and confidence:** Plausible model, without demonstrated economics. Proposed revenue is a project fee after sampling; later volumes could bring repeat work, but subscription demand is not established. Published software and service prices are benchmarks, not accepted prices for this offer ([E003](#e003), [E005](#e005)).

**Illustrative calculation, not a measured workload or quote:** Assume 100 pages each require one handwriting-recognition pass and separate line detection. App credits = 100 × (1 + 0.25) = 125. Allocating a €59.50/250-credit pack proportionally gives €29.75 of processing. The upfront purchase still costs €59.50, with unused credits. Free allowances, reruns and eligibility change this. At an assumed ten minutes of human checking per page, checking alone takes 100 × 10 / 60 = 16.7 hours. Intake, cleanup, name research, selection, layout, corrections and acquisition are additional and unpriced. The labour assumption has not been tested.

**Initial delivery:** Use customer-supplied scans and a language/script a competent reviewer can read. Combine recognition, side-by-side proofing, indexing, uncertainty notes and portable exports with existing software. A custom app is unnecessary for learning about a small service. Fragile physical originals, unfamiliar scripts, translation and historical interpretation require additional specialist work. Commercial supplier terms/API eligibility, storage, printing, expert rates and actual founder time remain unverified or unpriced.

**Next useful check:** Time an authorized sample through the complete workflow and obtain terms/specialist quotes before proposing a full-project price. No sample or purchase was obtained.

## C004

**Claim:** Genealogy communities and professional referrals offer plausible customer-access routes.

**Assessment and confidence:** Relevant communities and a public professional directory exist ([E001](#e001), [E002](#e002), [E006](#e006)). Buyer access, partner willingness and acquisition costs remain unknown. A genealogist can be a competitor or intermediary.

**Next useful check:** Find a professional whose clients request readable family archives and clarify whether completion work falls outside their present service. Particular contacts/conversations belong in customer-connect after selection.
