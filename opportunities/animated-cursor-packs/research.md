# Animated cursor collections — research

Current claim assessment: **2026-10-29**. Stage: initially researched candidate, recommended for investigation. **User selection: none.**

Scope: a reusable $6 original Windows cursor collection for adult aesthetic-desktop buyers, with international customers eligible. Priority is earliest credible earnings under ₹5,000/month, 10–19 hours/week, AI-assisted production and willingness to support. The [complete scout campaign](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-29-viral-consumer-products.md#comparison) compared 50 distinct deep assessments and resolved independent challenges before promoting this one opportunity. No product, customer test, purchase or outreach occurred.

Evidence entries remain in the canonical campaign; this file records claim judgments and links to the observations rather than duplicating them. Read the [plain-language summary](summary.md) for the proposed product and founder work.

## C001 — People buy small original cursor designs for enjoyment

**Assessment:** moderately strong qualitative support for category spending. Recent item buyers describe original plant/flower collections; one reports two purchases to swap with desktop themes. This is closer evidence than generic viral-product popularity or a famous-character pack.

**Evidence:** [I017 E001](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-29-viral-consumer-products.md#i017-e001), [I017 E006](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-29-viral-consumer-products.md#i017-e006).

**Limits:** marketplace-selected buyer accounts, not independently audited receipts, sales velocity or market size. A repeat category purchase is not our retention. Brand/franchise-related evidence [E002](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-29-viral-consumer-products.md#i017-e002) supports the motive more weakly for an original character.

**Next check:** understand recent buyers' actual theme choices, what they paid for and how they discovered the specific pack. Avoid treating hypothetical enthusiasm as another purchase.

## C002 — A new original aesthetic could earn paid preference

**Assessment:** plausible but unproved. The reason to choose is the finished tiny artwork and animation. Technical completeness, no subscription and preview-before-buy are already available.

**Current baseline:** [free library](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-29-viral-consumer-products.md#i017-e004), [AI/image generator](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-29-viral-consumer-products.md#i017-e003), [similarly priced original creator pack with preview](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-29-viral-consumer-products.md#i017-e007), and an [exact free native animated pack](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-29-viral-consumer-products.md#i017-e008). The independent challenge found no reason to remove these from the comparison.

**Assumptions:** the founder can direct a coherent original style that works at pointer size; enough buyers prefer it to free alternatives. A large concept illustration cannot prove tiny-frame quality. Buyers can add a collection without abandoning their old one, but that does not make them pay.

**Next check:** compare clearly priced original-theme concepts against these real alternatives; a later production sample must show real-size states and actual installation. The exact theme remains undecided.

## C003 — AI-assisted production can fit, conditional on output quality

**Assessment:** feasible in principle, unmeasured for this founder. Free commercial-use editing software removes a compulsory editor charge, while AI can help concepts, frame cleanup and packaging. Correct hotspots, crisp outlines and calm animation require judgment and testing.

**Evidence:** [editor capability/license](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-29-viral-consumer-products.md#i017-e005), [bounded image cost reference](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-29-viral-consumer-products.md#shared-e005), [independent quality/hardware challenge](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-29-viral-consumer-products.md#challenge-play).

**Contrary:** one cursor buyer explicitly appreciates non-AI creation, and current creators use no-AI positioning. Exact paid AI-assisted art use in other formats shows that universal rejection is unsupported, but does not establish cursor-buyer acceptance. [Journal-art observation](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-29-viral-consumer-products.md#i030-e006) is adjacent only. Etsy currently allows seller-prompted AI work with disclosure [platform rule](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-29-viral-consumer-products.md#shared-e012); this is neither buyer approval nor clearance of a particular asset.

**Next check:** confirm target-device access, specify one representative original pointer/animation exercise for the separate production workflow and decide what quality the founder can independently inspect. Production method must be described accurately. A documented Mac workspace does not prove there is no Windows access elsewhere.

## C004 — Reusable files offer a bounded cost of starting

**Assessment:** favorable variable-cost structure, conditional on cheap delivery and manageable support. No generative model needs to run per purchase. Forty illustrative $0.08 generation attempts cost $3.20 upfront, with usable yield unknown.

**Scenario:** proposed $6 non-US card sale gives $5.11 after the [published checkout example](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-29-viral-consumer-products.md#shared-e001). Assume $0.10 delivery/support-system cash: $5.01 contribution before labor, acquisition, refunds, payout/FX and tax differences. Four orders recover a hypothetical $20 startup outlay; ten yield $30.10 after that outlay. These are calculations, not results or revenue forecasts. Recurring tool spend is additional if selected.

**Work:** 15–30 estimated production/learning hours plus six initial promotion hours, approximately 2–4 calendar weeks at the confirmed availability. This is readiness, not time to a sale. A ₹1,500 initial production/test cap is an allowance within the monthly budget, not an actual quote or conversion of the dollar scenario. A missing Windows device could break fit. Ten ten-minute support incidents add 100 minutes.

**Next check:** verify real tool/device access and time on the representative quality exercise before committing to a full collection or catalogue. Track support separately from cash costs.

## C005 — First buyers are reachable through visual desktop demonstrations

**Assessment:** credible proposed route, low confidence in acquisition. A real-screen before/after clip conveys the product quickly. An owned short-video channel, allowed creator showcases, free static preview and explicit store link provide a concrete buying path. No audience, permission to post or conversion is established.

**Evidence and limits:** [full acquisition hypothesis](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-29-viral-consumer-products.md#i017). Current listings establish where buyers have bought; they do not give the founder that traffic. One thousand relevant views × 2% visiting × 3–5% buying gives 0.6–1 expected orders and can produce zero actual sales. Prices and conversion are assumptions.

**Contrary:** attractive free clips can satisfy viewers without a purchase. Free packs are easy to find, and copied art can reduce exclusivity. [Original high-attention counterexample](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-29-viral-consumer-products.md#shared-e011) shows why activity cannot substitute for profit evidence; it is a different product, not a cursor revenue benchmark.

**Next check:** identify a specific permissible route to relevant adults and plan a small priced-preference test. No outreach is included in this handoff. An investigator should define the test; customer-connect would later prepare particular contacts if selected.

## C006 — A sale does not mean immediate usable cash

**Assessment:** provider terms support a material delay and minimum balance; founder eligibility remains unknown. Under the fee-only example, ten $6 Lemon sales cross $50; the provider then applies hold/scheduled payout/bank timing. Gumroad's direct case leaves $4.90 and needs 21 orders to cross $100.

**Evidence:** [Lemon schedule/eligibility](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-29-viral-consumer-products.md#shared-e002), [Gumroad fees](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-29-viral-consumer-products.md#shared-e003), [later Gumroad payout check](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-29-viral-consumer-products.md#shared-e009). If operating from India, [Etsy onboarding constraints](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-29-viral-consumer-products.md#shared-e010) and provider-specific routes matter. Founder residence/citizenship is unrecorded.

**Next check:** determine the actual available merchant/payout route and product approval before relying on checkout in a sales test. Do not open accounts or purchase subscriptions based solely on this recommendation.
