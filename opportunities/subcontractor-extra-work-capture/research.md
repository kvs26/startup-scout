# Subcontractor extra-work capture — research

Current claim assessment: 2026-10-10  
Scope: Electrical/specialty subcontractors capturing labor and materials for additional work; practitioner evidence mainly English-language, with the main author's geography unverified.

## C001

**Claim:** Some contractors lose billable extra-work records between the site and office.

**Assessment and confidence:** Moderate confidence in the reported workflow problem; low confidence in prevalence or verified lost revenue. [E001](#e001) gives a specific electrical-subcontractor account but the author later solicits beta users for their own tool. [E002](#e002) corroborates tracking difficulty in a different residential context. These contexts must not be treated as one segment.

**Contrary evidence:** E001 contains accounts of successful Clearstory/PeerAssist use and disciplined written authorization. E002 explains why minor extras are sometimes deliberately included for client relations. A paperwork tool does not resolve authorization, scope disagreement, or an unwilling payer.

## C002

**Claim:** Photos/voice capture is a sufficiently distinct reason to buy a new product.

**Assessment and confidence:** Challenged with high confidence about documented competitor capabilities. Clearstory's July 2026 release documents a voice/text T&M agent in open beta ([E004](#e004)); its Basic offer includes five T&M tags and five change-order requests per month ([E003](#e003)). SubTrack also targets field-to-billing work ([E005](#e005)). AI, digital signatures and smaller-company positioning do not establish a gap by themselves.

**Remaining hypothesis:** An installed workflow using an existing crew communication channel and the office's actual billing format might reduce more effort for a specific trade. No evidence yet demonstrates better adoption, lower total cost, or missing incumbent support. AI-beta eligibility and practical quality require checking; it was not confirmed as included in Clearstory Basic.

## C003

**Claim:** A solo founder could deliver a focused initial product.

**Assessment and confidence:** Plausible design inference, with substantial operational work. A crew forwards a photo or records work; the product prompts for job, requester, labor/materials and authorization; the office reviews a draft ticket and tracks acknowledgement/export. Capture and authorization remain distinct.

**Delivery/costs:** Project/rate setup, media handling, transcription/extraction, review, approvals, reliable delivery, duplicates and PDF/CSV export. Customer onboarding and field adoption may dominate founder effort. Messaging-number/carrier costs, transcription/OCR/model usage, storage, support, acquisition and trade-specific review are unpriced. SMS/MMS is not a verified universal voice-note transport; channel choice and provider behavior require testing. No build timeline or complete supplier-cost estimate exists.

## C004

**Claim:** A contractor owner or office team will pay for recurring use.

**Assessment and confidence:** Unresolved. Owner/operations manager is a proposed payer; foremen and office administrators are users. Monthly per-company pricing plus setup is a proposed model; no price accepted. A configured service using existing software could be preferable to a new subscription.

**Access:** NECA's search page exists ([E006](#e006)), but returned no listings in extraction. No warm access, purchasing authority or customer willingness is established.

## E001

**Origin:** [Lost ~$4k on unbilled T&M work](https://www.reddit.com/r/Construction/comments/1p3e7l4/lost_4k_this_month_on_unbilled_tm_work_how_do_you/), r/Construction; AssistPlus353 and replies.

**Report/context:** Self-described electrical subcontractor reports delayed vague texts/photos and a roughly $4,000 unbilled month. The update proposes an internal text-to-PDF tool and invites beta users. Replies report satisfactory incumbent tools and written-authorization procedures; some replies promote vendors.

**Dates:** Event month unknown; exact publication date unknown (index roughly late 2025); update says November 23 without a year; retrieved 2026-10-10.  
**Claims/limits:** C001–C004. Identity and loss unverified; promotional possibility lowers weight. Main post, update and relevant supporting/contrary replies read. Same-thread replies are not a population estimate.

## E002

**Report/context:** Author helping a family residential construction business describes accumulated small additions, informal Google Docs and poor tracking. Replies distinguish project size, scope, client relationships and documentation effort.

**Dates:** Event unknown; exact publication date unknown, search approximately early 2025; retrieved 2026-10-10.  
**Claims/limits:** C001–C002. Different customer segment; self-report, not purchasing authority or measured loss. Relevant replies and author's clarifications read.
