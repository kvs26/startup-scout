# Subcontractor extra-work capture

Folder identity: subcontractor-extra-work-capture  
Current findings assessed: 2026-10-10  
Stage: Initially researched candidate; distinct reason to buy unresolved

Electrical and other specialty subcontractors must record additional labor/materials and pass the records to the office for review and billing. The proposed product turns field photos and short descriptions into draft tickets, prompts for missing information, and tracks review, acknowledgement and export. Initial geography is unset; the main account's location is unverified.

**Illustration, not an observed use:** A crew installs an additional circuit. The foreman supplies a job photo, requester and labor details; the office reviews the generated ticket and sees that commercial authorization is still missing. A record of work and authorization to charge are separate facts.

**Proposed offer:** Per-company subscription plus setup for project rates and office formats; price unset. A service configuring existing software is a competing business approach.

## What we have learned

The main complaint describes delayed records and unbilled work, but the author later invites users to their own beta. Replies report satisfactory products and authorization procedures ([C001](../research.md#c001)). Clearstory already documents voice-based tickets in open beta, and its free tier has limited monthly tickets; SubTrack is another focused competitor ([C002](../research.md#c002)).

A possible distinction must be proven in crew adoption and office workflow. Field onboarding, rates, messaging, accurate drafts and reliable export create continuing founder work; costs are unpriced ([C003](../research.md#c003)). Potential owner/operations payers and NECA discovery routes do not establish actual access ([C004](../research.md#c004)).

## What happens next

**Recommendation:** Second investigation, or first if the founder has direct access to subcontractors. Examine an actual lost ticket and compare incumbent setup against the proposed approach.

**User selection:** None recorded.

**Next unresolved question:** Why would a crew and office adopt this instead of a satisfactory existing tool and better authorization procedures?
