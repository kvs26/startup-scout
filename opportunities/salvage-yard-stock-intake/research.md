# Salvage-yard stock intake — research

Current claim assessment: 2026-10-31. Initially researched candidate recommended by the scout after 54 distinct complete assessments. No user selection, outreach, customer test, build or payment. [Readable proposition](summary.md).

Scope: independent US architectural salvage yards with unique items and per-item digital stock records. Proposed first category: doors. Proposed model: yard-operated subscription, one existing stock connection; owner/manager pays. US entry is a testable choice, not validation of Australian evidence in the US. Wider categories/regions remain expansion hypotheses. The [founder profile](../../FOUNDER-PROFILE.md) supplies current starting resources; the user made them flexible by idea without approving spending.

All observations keep their canonical homes in the [campaign](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-31-new-discovery.md). This file assesses claims and carries the handoff; it does not duplicate source logs.

## C001 — The customer has recurring cataloguing work worth examining

**Assessment:** medium-high confidence in the activity, low confidence in a paid unmet need. [An Australian operator’s cataloguing limitation](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-31-new-discovery.md#i034-e001) and [Second Use’s US operating choices](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-31-new-discovery.md#i034-e002) establish the tension between useful detail and staff throughput. These undated current policies are not a measured backlog, lost-sales study or software request. Second Use’s existing catalogue and [controlled holds](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-31-new-discovery.md#i034-e003) are strong positive substitutes.

**Next check:** observe actual receiving and recording work at one willing US yard. Establish item volume, current staff time, why detail is omitted and whether individual stock records already exist. If measuring/handling dominates or the yard deliberately avoids low-value listings, restrict to a valuable category or stop.

## C002 — A focused capture/search product could earn an additional subscription

**Assessment:** concrete but low-confidence hypothesis. Proposed advantage is fewer taps and corrections from measured notes to approved fields and useful dimensional search, with the current stock system retained. It is not novel AI copy or a proven cheaper full-suite replacement. [ThriftCart’s relevant scope](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-31-new-discovery.md#i034-e004), [Square’s native AI/custom fields](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-31-new-discovery.md#challenge-ag-e001) and [Treecat’s fee/scope](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-31-new-discovery.md#challenge-ag-e002) substantially challenge the proposition. ThriftCart website integration is quote-gated; its starter price is not an equivalent comparison.

**Next check:** use the same 30 authorized items to compare the yard’s current method, configured native/available AI tools and an eventual separately produced demonstrator. Measure complete capture-plus-correction time, not draft generation speed. Record which public search questions matter and whether a current website configuration already supplies them.

## C003 — Reliable production is feasible only with staff ownership and a verified connection

**Assessment:** plausible documented route, no demonstrated quality. The [Square Inventory API](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-31-new-discovery.md#challenge-ag-e003) supplies a potential interface, not an existing integration or atomic reservation guarantee. A yard using generic department sales may have no item-level events. The first system is not selected and access rights are not confirmed.

AI could build parsers/screens and propose factual fields. Founder directs revisions, reviews software behavior, arranges hosting, monitors failures and handles onboarding/support. Staff measure, identify damage, set price and approve publication. Unknown facts remain unknown. Existing stock records control availability; failed or delayed updates must be visible. No scan, voice transcription, extraction benchmark, synchronization test or demonstrator exists.

**Next check:** inspect permitted exports/connection terms; define treatment of duplicates, out-of-order updates, holds, sales, returns and missing identifiers. Agree required fields and unacceptable factual errors with an experienced yard worker. No invented dimensions or condition claims; display original photos. Specialist development/security review, if needed, is unpriced. Separate production skills would create any demonstrator after the investigator defines the question; scouting did not authorize implementation.

## C004 — Price and support can produce viable economics

**Assessment:** scenario only. US$59/site/month and US$150 setup are hypotheses, not accepted prices. Proposed monthly coverage is drafts/approvals for one yard and one door-category stock connection; setup maps one existing file and configures the connection. Staff/item limits, support commitments and external-system charges remain undecided and must be priced. Unlimited manual cleanup is excluded.

[Full 30-yard scenario and per-item correction sensitivity](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-31-new-discovery.md#i034) show why cheap inference is insufficient. [Independent threshold analysis](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-31-new-discovery.md#challenge-ag-i034) gives the buyer-side test: at assumed US$20/hour, the fee alone requires about three staff hours saved monthly. This is opportunity cost, not assured cash savings. Setup, sales, taxes, refunds, specialist help and ongoing maintenance remain additional. No currency conversion or revenue forecast is used.

**Next check:** measure correction/support time, obtain actual connection/specialist costs and ask who owns the budget. Price acceptance needs to cover normal and failure weeks. Current ₹5,000/month does not establish an affordable delivery arrangement; no higher budget or spend is approved.
