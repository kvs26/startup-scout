# T002 — Will adults choose, pay for and return to this companion?

Prepared: 2026-10-18. Status: **proposed and conditional; no prototype, payment or customer usage exists**.

Offer: [current summary](../summary.md). Claims: [C002](../research.md#c002), [C003](../research.md#c003), [C005](../research.md#c005), [C006](../research.md#c006).

## Decision and prerequisites

Determine whether the actual experience earns a monthly payment and later renewal at an affordable cost. Start after [T001](T001-preference-and-current-use.md) identifies the relevant preferences and the separate production workflow supplies a working sample. This plan does not create or authorize that build, outreach, a purchase or a live customer experiment.

Required sample: one original character, mobile-friendly text chat, working memory approval/correction/deletion, accurate usage counter, export, cancellation and adequate account isolation. It must identify itself as AI. Check multi-day conversation quality, billable usage, response latency and critical failures before involving customers. Establish the launch geography, adult-access approach, supplier terms, data handling, payment eligibility, total price, refunds and real support capacity first. A generic disclaimer does not complete these tasks.

## Procedure

Use adult volunteers who fit the clarified audience, with their usual companion available as a comparison. Do not require abandoning a product or importing private histories. Begin with a small capacity batch, provisionally five people; that is a budget/workload constraint, not statistical proof.

Offer a non-renewing 100-reply trial and state the proposed $9/month, 1,000-reply offer before use. Make any compensation separate from a purchase decision. Observe the first session, then let participants return voluntarily. Do not have the character pressure, guilt or repeatedly prompt them into continuing.

After they have experienced a return visit and the memory behavior, offer the actual monthly product if delivery and the payment route are ready. Record completed payments, declines and reasons. Do not collect payment for an unavailable product or count a stated intention as a sale. Continue observation through one complete paid month and the first renewal opportunity, retaining all invited participants in the cohort record, including those who leave.

Record trial start, usage days, user-initiated returns, allowance exhaustion, what participants enjoyed, comparison preference, payment/refund/cancellation/renewal events, token and other costs, error reports and founder support minutes. For sensitive conversations, prefer event counts and participant-selected feedback over full content collection. Record reminder exposure so it is not mistaken for voluntary return.

## Interpretation

| Observation | Decision implication | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Users choose this character after experiencing continuity, pay, return voluntarily and renew with manageable support/cost | Supports another bounded cohort and more acquisition work | Does not establish large-market scale, long-term retention or every user segment |
| Enjoyable first session but little return or no payment | Character novelty may work while recurring value or price does not; revise within the selected business | Signups and compliments are not commercial validation |
| Payment followed by abandonment, refund or cancellation | Investigate whether expectations, limits, quality or price explain the result | A first payment alone cannot justify expansion |
| Repeat use with excessive context, expensive model upgrades or human correction | Revise delivery/allowance/price and recalculate | Engagement does not imply positive economics |
| Serious privacy/behavior failure or support beyond capacity | Pause this live test and repair/review the specific failure before another cohort | A strong demand signal cannot establish safe reliable delivery |

Use actual reasons and unit costs rather than inventing a universal conversion threshold for five people. The meaningful commercial observation is renewed use at the stated terms with acceptable quality and support. Comparison order, incentives, prior attachments and selective recruitment may bias the outcome; retain them in the record.

## Effort, cost and return

The [economics](../economics.md) contains provisional token/trial costs, not a test budget approval. Sample creation, payment setup, participant compensation and specialist review remain unpriced. Founder work continues for at least the trial plus one monthly billing cycle; do not promise a one-week validation of renewal.

The founder returns notes and transaction/usage summaries with this opportunity path. startup-opportunity-investigator records actual results and reassesses contribution, preference and renewal separately. Customer-connect prepares specific recruitment or purchase conversations only when those tasks are requested.
