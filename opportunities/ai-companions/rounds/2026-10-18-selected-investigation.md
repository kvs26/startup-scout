## Calculation snapshot

Use the current Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite Standard benchmark: $0.30/million input and $2.50/million output tokens, counting charged reasoning. The original scout's cheaper 2.5 benchmark is not assumed available to a new project; current lifecycle documentation restricts 2.5 access to prior users. This is a benchmark replacement, not a measured quality improvement.

Assume 600 replies/month, 6,000 input and 250 output tokens/reply, plus 25% additional model work. Generation is `600 × (6000×0.30 + 250×2.50)/1,000,000 × 1.25 = $1.81875`. At $9 revenue, hypothetical 5% fees/refunds and $0.75 other variable cash, contribution is `$9×0.95−$1.81875−$0.75 = $5.98125`. At 1,000 replies it is $4.76875. At 1,000 replies with 20,000 input/500 output tokens, generation is $9.0625 and contribution negative $1.2625. All exclude acquisition, fixed spend, tax and labor.

Assume fixed technical cash $10/month and production tools ₹2,000/month. Using ₹100/$ solely as a hypothetical planning allowance, five ordinary-use accounts including $0.75 variable allowance each cost approximately ₹4,284/month; ten ₹5,569; twenty ₹8,138. Acquisition, extra trials, taxes, review and founder work are additional. This does not establish that a commercial launch fits the budget.

A full 100-reply trial costs $0.303125 in modeled generation. Hypothetical conversion of 10% or 2% implies trial generation of $3.03 or $15.16 per payer. An assumed $12 acquisition cost needs 2.01 ordinary paid months to recover before fixed costs and labor. The inputs are assumptions, not observed performance.

## Proposed test snapshot

**T001:** Begin with six adult-user interviews as a workload batch, deliberately including payers, satisfied free users and cancellations. Ask about recent conversations, actual reported spending, reasons to return/leave, incumbent strengths, switching effort and the proposed allowance. Show the idea only after exploring past behavior. Record observed facts, reports and opinions separately. Concrete recurring preferences inform one sample; satisfied users with no reason to try ours challenge the offer. Compliments and future interest do not prove payment. Estimated conversation/synthesis work 4–6 hours, excluding recruitment; access/incentives unknown.

**T002:** Conditional on an actual reviewed sample and ready operating/payment arrangements. Start with a small five-adult capacity batch, 100-reply non-renewing trial and explicit $9 monthly offer. Observe voluntary return, then actual payment, a full paid month and renewal. Record costs, support, cancellations, refunds and failures; include everyone initially invited. Payment plus return/renewal at manageable costs supports another cohort; first-session enthusiasm without return, or high-cost ongoing use, calls for revision. A serious behavior/privacy failure suspends the live test pending repair. Neither the sample sizes nor any invented conversion cutoff constitutes validation.

These procedures are proposals, fully recorded in [T001](../tests/T001-preference-and-current-use.md) and [T002](../tests/T002-payment-and-return.md). No tests ran.
