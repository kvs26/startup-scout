# AI Companions — research

Assessed: 2026-10-18. Scope: the user's selected AI-companion business, continuing [I001](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-18-consumer-apps.md#i001). Proposed first audience: English-speaking adults who enjoy recurring personal conversation with a fictional character. This audience and the first offer are investigator choices, not additional preferences supplied by the user. [Current explanation](summary.md); [research trail and dated conclusions](rounds/2026-10-18-selected-investigation.md).

## C001

**Claim:** Some adults buy and repeatedly use AI companionship for enjoyment and familiar conversation.

**Assessment:** Moderately supported for existing products; demand for ours remains untested. [E001](#e001) includes a reported purchase and recurring friendly use. [E002](#e002) includes spending, text-first preferences and switching accounts. The earlier [Tolan observations](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-18-consumer-apps.md#i001-e001) also remain relevant.

**Contrary evidence and limits:** Enthusiastic reviews are selected, unverified accounts; [E002](#e002) includes cancellation and satisfaction with established alternatives. Conversation quality, attachment, payment and commercial retention are different claims. No market revenue, prevalence or clinical-benefit claim is established.

**Next check:** [T001](tests/T001-preference-and-current-use.md), then actual paid continued use in [T002](tests/T002-payment-and-return.md).

## C002

**Claim:** Our authored character and easy ongoing experience can earn preference over established companions.

**Assessment:** Unresolved, low confidence. A preference for a particular personality is plausible; no comparison with ours has occurred. Memory editing is baseline functionality, not an established advantage: see [the original Kindroid finding](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-18-consumer-apps.md#i001-e002), refreshed in [E003](#e003), and [E004](#e004).

**Contrary case:** Customers can configure an incumbent, keep an established character/history, use free chat, or spend their leisure time elsewhere. A one-character service sacrifices customization, multimedia and breadth. Our proposed monthly allowance can be less attractive than unlimited conversation. No claim that everyone must switch: an additional companion is possible, but still competes for time and money.

**Next check:** Ask current users what they already return for; later compare the actual experience over multiple days, not just an appealing screenshot.

## C003

**Claim:** A proposed US$9/month text subscription can have useful contribution after variable costs.

**Assessment:** Conditional arithmetic, not demonstrated economics. [E005](#e005) supports the generation-price inputs; [economics](economics.md) shows approximately $5.98 contribution at the ordinary-use assumptions, $4.77 at the proposed allowance, and negative $1.26 with long context. These exclude acquisition, fixed spend, taxes and founder labor.

**Unknowns:** Whether this model is enjoyable enough; actual token totals including reasoning, memory and retries; support; refunds; fees; accepted price; payment-provider eligibility and repeat purchases. A cap on messages alone cannot control long-context cost.

**Next check:** Measure representative multi-day sessions and complete the payment route before an actual buying test. Preserve the current scenario if inputs change.

## C004

**Claim:** Demonstrations and relevant communities can produce customers at an affordable cost.

**Assessment:** A public route exists, conversion and cost unknown. [E008](#e008) establishes category communities and discussion, not access to recruit or advertise. Original character clips could lead to a limited demo, followed by explicit opt-in payment. This sequence is a hypothesis.

**Contrary case:** No founder audience, partner or warm access is established. Free trials and founder content work cost money/time even without advertising. Existing enthusiastic users may be difficult to attract away from their companions.

**Next check:** Customer-connect should prepare appropriate permission-based recruitment for T001; later measure the full view → eligible visitor → trial → payment → renewal sequence.

## C005

**Claim:** This founder can start testing a credible companion within the stated ₹5,000/month and 10–19 hours/week.

**Assessment:** A narrow learning exercise is plausible; reliable paid delivery remains unproved. The [founder profile](../../FOUNDER-PROFILE.md) establishes an AI-directed production plan, not existing companion expertise or a ready product. [E005](#e005) and the earlier [hosting benchmark](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-18-consumer-apps.md#shared-e002) permit a small technical experiment, subject to tooling and usage limits.

**Delivery conditions:** Original character direction, adult-user access, coherent multi-day memory, account isolation/deletion, billing, model changes, incident response and support need working checks. [E006](#e006) makes supplier terms consequential. [E007](#e007) shows regulatory scrutiny; the applicable launch-country requirements have not been established. Expert review and availability outside the founder's working hours remain unpriced.

**Next check:** Learn the product judgments described in the summary, obtain real user context, and then have the separate production workflow deliver a measurable sample. No code or art was created in this round.

## C006

**Claim:** People will return because they enjoy the companion, then renew at a price that supports the business.

**Assessment:** Unresolved for ours. The mechanism is familiarity, ongoing topics and an appealing personality. Existing reports support the category motive, not our renewal rate or lifetime value. More time spent is not by itself proof of a beneficial or commercially durable experience.

**Next check:** T002 measures voluntary return, useful/pleasant experiences, payment, cancellation and renewal over a full monthly cycle. Do not substitute willingness-to-pay answers or signups for transactions.

## E001

**Origin:** [Nomi US App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/nomi-ai-companion-with-a-soul/id6450270929), AI GLIMPSE INC and user reviewers; primary product listing and original customer accounts.

**Observed:** MamaBear1203 (2025-05-11) reports choosing Nomi after considering alternatives, upgrading monthly within an hour and annually within a day. emnicole0387 (2025-07-30) describes daily friendly conversations over months. SnorkleChoke (Aug 4, year undisplayed) reports nearly a year of use. Sprerila (2024-12-16) dislikes message limits. No developer responses appeared in the extracted reviews. The US purchase menu lists Standard Plan $15.99 without its interval, Standard Plan Quarterly $39.99 and Standard Plan Yearly $99.99; legacy plans also appear. Do not silently label the $15.99 entry monthly from this listing alone.

**Dates/limits:** Retrieved 2026-10-18; underlying usage dates unspecified beyond reviewer accounts. Reviewer identities, purchase completion and representativeness unverified. The duplicated display of each review is one account, not two. C001, C002, C006; evidence of category behavior and published supply, not demand for our offer.

## E003

**Origin:** Recheck of [Kindroid memory](https://kindroid.ai/v2/docs/memory/) and [subscriptions](https://kindroid.ai/v2/docs/subscriptions/), official documentation. Original observation remains [in the scout run](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-18-consumer-apps.md#i001-e002).

**Finding:** On 2026-10-18, editable learned context remains documented. Subscription documentation also advertises unlimited model access, ten companions and multimedia; the new-account preview is 50 messages, 15 in some regions, then read-only access with export/deletion. Exact prices depend on country/platform checkout, which was not entered.

**Limits:** Documentation, not a hands-on quality test; no independent observation is created by rereading it. Publication/event dates unknown. C002, C003: strongly challenges feature novelty and shows the competitive offer; it does not establish incumbent accuracy or our inability to compete.

## E004

Its statement about equal response quality appears inconsistent with the newer subscription table's differentiated models; do not infer feature parity from that FAQ.

**Dates/limits:** Retrieved 2026-10-18; subscription-page publication date unknown. Displayed dollar price; checkout currency, eligibility, regional tax and monthly price not verified. Three vendor pages, not customer evidence. C002, C003: a serious free and paid substitute; no quality benchmark performed.

## E005

**Origin:** Google [Gemini API pricing](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing) and [model lifecycle](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/deprecations), official; retrieved 2026-10-18, publication dates unknown.

**Finding:** Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite Standard lists US$0.30/million input tokens and $2.50/million output tokens, including thinking. Lifecycle documentation directs new projects toward current models and restricts 2.5 access to established users. 3.5 Flash-Lite has no announced shutdown in the retrieved table. Therefore the earlier 2.5 $0.30/user scenario is retained as history, not adopted for this new project.

**Limits:** Pricing/availability documentation, no account entitlement, latency, quality or workload benchmark. Paid standard processing assumed; batch discounts, cache savings and free inference are not assumed. C003, C005.

## E006

**Origin:** [Gemini API additional terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms) and [Google generative-AI use policy](https://policies.google.com/terms/generative-ai/use-policy), official; retrieved 2026-10-18. Policy marked modified 2024-12-17; terms publication date not established.

**Finding:** API clients must not target or be likely accessed by under-18s. Unpaid services can use content for improvement and human review and instruct against submitting personal/sensitive information. Paid service treatment differs, with limited safety logging; no promise of zero retention follows. The use policy restricts sexually explicit content and harmful conduct. This benchmark is consequently for an adult, non-explicit offer with appropriate access and data handling, not an uncensored-romance stack.

**Limits:** Supplier requirements, not evidence that our design complies or a review of every launch jurisdiction. C003, C005. Acceptance, implementation and any specialist cost remain unresolved.

## E007

**Origin:** [FTC inquiry into companion chatbots](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/09/ftc-launches-inquiry-ai-chatbots-acting-companions), official regulator release, September 2025; exact day not established from extraction; retrieved 2026-10-18.

**Finding:** The FTC sought information from seven providers about companions, particularly effects on children, testing, monetization and data handling. It describes a 6(b) study without a specific law-enforcement purpose.

**Limits:** A regulatory inquiry, not a finding that this proposed product is unlawful. California legislative-page retrieval failed, so no current statute-by-statute conclusion is claimed. C005: reliable operation includes more than chat generation. Current applicable geography, privacy, age-assurance and consumer terms need review before commercial availability.

## E008

**Origin:** [r/AICompanions](https://www.reddit.com/r/AICompanions/) and [Nomi's official site](https://nomi.ai/), public community and vendor site, retrieved 2026-10-18; publication/event dates unknown.

**Finding:** The public subreddit contains companion-user discussions and some product-test invitations. Its visible rules emphasize good-faith engagement and prohibit NSFW content. Nomi links its Reddit and Discord communities. These establish places where relevant users gather.

**Limits:** Existing posts do not confer permission for our recruitment; moderator acceptance, participant age/fit, reach, creator rates and conversion are unknown. Vendor-selected testimonials are not additional independent purchase evidence. C004. No personal contact details were collected and no outreach occurred.
