# Priority ranking of the 13 saved opportunities

Updated: 2026-10-31, Asia/Kolkata.

All 13 current opportunity summaries were reviewed, together with relevant claim records and later corrections. Current buyer briefs, purchaser reviews and first-party alternatives were checked; the evidence and access limits are in the [dated research record](../scouting-runs/2026-10-31-saved-opportunity-ranking.md). No customer was contacted, product operated, payment accepted or prototype created. Social-media momentum was not freshly measured and does not determine this ranking.

This is a practical order for this founder to investigate next: credible earnings, reachable buyers, full delivery effort, repeat value and growth. The baseline is approximately ₹5,000/month, 10–19 hours/week, AI-led production and personal direction/customer conversations. These are starting resources, not a permanent ambition ceiling. First-revenue feasibility matters, but this is not a ranking of maximum possible market size or predicted profit. Adjacent positions are judgment calls under weak private evidence.

**Start with creator-community Discord setup as a focused income experiment.** It has the clearest buyer-authored requests and a concrete handoff. Prove the complete AI-assisted delivery method and permission checks first. **Shop makeover kits lead the owned-product options. Research-report number checking leads the software investigations.** None is a validated business.

## What customers would buy, and what you would do

### 1. Creator-community Discord setup

A creator-program owner pays once for agreed channels, access roles, welcome messages, private support tickets and staff handoff. In an imagined use, a member clicks Help and gets a ticket visible only to that member and staff; staff reply, close it and retain records under the chosen provider's limits. The owner learns to change a dummy channel and staff assignment independently.

The saved proposal is $150 with one correction round and a seven-day defect window. Recent Upwork briefs request these tasks, but some add custom approval/role automation outside this package. Advertised budgets are not founder earnings, and clients request experience. Free native Discord and ticket tools are genuine DIY alternatives. See [fresh buyer and supplier checks](../scouting-runs/2026-10-31-saved-opportunity-ranking.md#rank-e001).

AI can help configure tickets and draft the structure. Its documented ticket connector does not establish channel/role creation or native settings. You must learn effective permissions, direct the remaining configuration route, test separate member/staff views and teach the owner. The earlier six-to-twelve-hour delivery range is unmeasured and excludes initial learning and selling. Winning a project, marketplace fees and payout delays matter. This can become a repeatable service; it is initially client work, with revenue tied to delivery.

### 2. Shop makeover kits

A small seller pays once for a coordinated editable collection. Imagined use: a jewelry seller replaces the sample pictures and shop name, then exports a matching banner, announcement and thank-you card. Proposed prices are $19 internationally or ₹999 for a separately tested domestic offer.

Verified purchaser reviews support coordination and saved effort. Free Canva and kits below $10 challenge the proposed price; premium listings alone do not prove ours will sell. AI would generate original assets/layouts under your direction. You must judge typography and crops, direct revisions, verify allowed redistribution, and test editing with an ordinary buyer account. A polished output and effortless editing remain unproved. No need to build a custom editor.

Seller-focused demonstrations and a useful free matching asset are possible acquisition routes, with no audience or conversion established. Low repeated delivery cost helps only if support remains technical rather than custom design. This ranks above cursors because the proposed payment is larger and the buyer has a concrete shop-launch task; cursors could move above it if their art proves easier to make and sell. [Evidence](../scouting-runs/2026-10-31-saved-opportunity-ranking.md#rank-e004).

### 3. Research-report number checking

An agency pays for a capped report comparing an existing PowerPoint presentation with an approved workbook. Imagined use: a source cell is 47%, but the slide still says 42%; the output identifies both locations and flags the mismatch. The analyst approves source mappings and resolves findings. Unsupported/unmapped values must be disclosed as unchecked.

The first hypothesis is $99/report; the $199/month software offer requires much more automation. Existing linking tools prevent stale values and existing AI review overlaps detection. The remaining proposition is checking finished, externally supplied files without rebuilding the agency's reporting system. AI could help create parsers and reports, but you must understand source versions, percentages, rounding and unchecked cases, and obtain qualified review of representative files. A reviewer and file access are not secured.

Public research-agency pages identify possible buyers; no relationship exists. Local processing could reduce file-transfer objections while adding installation support. It ranks above salvage because a finite file comparison can be scoped without an operational inventory connection; neither has proved paid value. [Competition recheck](../scouting-runs/2026-10-31-saved-opportunity-ranking.md#rank-e007).

### 4. Animated cursor packs

An aesthetic-desktop buyer pays a proposed $6 for one original Windows collection and installation/restoration instructions. Imagined use: the ordinary pointer becomes a sleepy creature, while link and busy states remain readable. The download covers the necessary pointer roles.

A flower-pack buyer reports buying two themes; this is category behavior, not our conversion. Free packs and strong original artists are substantial alternatives. AI could generate/edit frames and software encode them. You must direct consistent tiny artwork, verify click hotspots, animation, scaling and installation on real Windows. Device access is unconfirmed. Distribution would require honest desktop demonstrations, not just listing a product. There is no required AI charge per sale, but a low ticket needs enough buyers and very light support. [Fresh purchaser recheck](../scouting-runs/2026-10-31-saved-opportunity-ranking.md#rank-e006).

### 5. Household-object blind dates

A host pays a proposed $7 for one private browser room for up to eight players including the host and up to three fresh rounds. Each person privately submits an object photo, name and quirk, then approves its generated dating biography. Everyone chooses one anonymous profile, excluding their own; several people may choose the same profile. After choices lock, the host reveals admirers and the corresponding object photo for every profile, including unchosen ones. Another round requires a fresh object/photo and review from each player.

Imagined example: a toaster that burns one corner gets “Warm, with a dark side.” A friend chooses that profile and then sees the toaster. The humor must work before and after the reveal. Adjacent object-romance and dating party purchases support the motive; paper or a group chat can recreate the sequence.

Players supply images. AI creates short text and helps build the room; you direct writing rules, check reconnections/photo deletion and observe whether ordinary groups enjoy continuing without help. The unverified 40–70-hour allowance is not a launch promise. A spectator choice/reveal is a concrete demo hook, but neither creator reach nor buying is measured. This ranks below reusable file products because room operations, per-play generation and group onboarding add work. [Current proposition and canonical findings](household-object-blind-dates/research.md).

### 6. Salvage-yard stock intake

A yard operator pays a proposed $59/month plus $150 setup for one category and one stock-system connection. Imagined use: staff enter “door, 32 × 80 inches, hinge damage, rack B2, $120,” approve factual fields and publish a listing; rack location stays private. Existing stock software remains the authority for holds, sales and returns.

Operator accounts support cataloguing work. ThriftCart, Square and AI listing tools weaken a generic drafting pitch. AI could implement parsing and drafts, while staff measure and approve physical facts. You must learn intake/hold workflows, direct a dependable integration, detect failed updates and support onboarding. The first system and access rights remain undecided. Yard websites and relevant agencies are routes to research, not a pipeline. Recurring revenue is attractive only if support and schemas become reusable. [Claim record](salvage-yard-stock-intake/research.md) and [fresh competition check](../scouting-runs/2026-10-31-saved-opportunity-ranking.md#rank-e008).

### 7. Agent-skill release review

A consultancy could pay monthly for local comparisons of a skill before and after an edit. Imagined use: one version attempts an unapproved file change; the report shows the trace so a lead can reject the release. Customers select representative requests and make the approval decision.

You would direct AI development, but must independently verify isolation, crashed-run handling and honest coverage. Existing free Promptfoo already compares versions and invocation/output behavior. A simpler reviewer handoff must earn payment beyond that baseline; skill familiarity alone is insufficient. Public maintaining teams are discoverable, but no payer is accessible yet. [Current alternative](../scouting-runs/2026-10-31-saved-opportunity-ranking.md#rank-e009).

### 8. Blind-presentation party packs

A host pays a proposed $7 once for twelve downloadable presentations and instructions. A volunteer speaks while the host shows unseen slides individually on a television. Imagined sequence: “My fridge runs a nightclub,” then milk cartons queuing behind a cucumber bouncer. The speaker invents an explanation for each surprise. After about three minutes, the host ends the turn and picks another speaker/presentation.

AI would produce writing, art and playable files under your direction. You must observe real improvisation and turn confusing or flat moments into specific revision requests. That production ability is untested; simple download delivery is not proof of quick readiness. Free slides and Jackbox Talking Points are close alternatives. Demonstrations need willing players, recording and relevant host traffic. [Current competition](../scouting-runs/2026-10-31-saved-opportunity-ranking.md#rank-e010).

### 9. Personal food assistant

A frequent delivery customer could pay a proposed ₹149 for 30 days and up to twenty decisions, with food separate. Imagined use: the user asks for dinner within budget, opens a specific suggested dish and later records whether to choose it again. Conversation and memory should reduce searching, not create another data-entry job.

You have firsthand problem knowledge and report friend access. Existing food-connected assistants are the most useful first comparison. AI would help build discovery/memory, while you check actual link destinations, available choices, price uncertainty and failures. Production platform access remains invite/contract dependent, with inconsistent Swiggy statements; this is neither an assumed open API nor proof of an absolute ban. Low subscription revenue must cover trials, calls, support and acquisition. Continue the existing Android benchmark as a cheap learning task, but do not treat its existence as a business lead over the top candidates. [Fresh access check](../scouting-runs/2026-10-31-saved-opportunity-ranking.md#rank-e011).

### 10. Walking-story expeditions

A walking-game user could pay a proposed $8 for an original illustrated journey. Imagined use: ordinary walking unlocks a lighthouse scene, then the next walk advances toward a garden. A new expedition could earn another purchase.

AI would create story, illustrations and software under your direction. You must judge a coherent six-landmark experience and separately verify phone step imports, saved progress and battery behavior. Older commissioned-art and 70–120-hour scenarios are superseded as baseline estimates by the opportunity's AI-production correction. Category purchases are credible, including a historical Fantasy Hike buyer who resisted subscription value. An original world, acquisition and paid continuation remain unproved. Native integration and content coherence make this a slower first undertaking than the top file products. ### 11. Familiar-meal rotation

A household could pay a proposed ₹499 once for a planner that organizes meals it already cooks. Imagined use: enter twenty dinners, mark Tuesday busy, replace one proposed meal and export the week. The customer supplies cooking knowledge and approves the plan.

AI could assist implementation; no runtime model or licensed recipe catalog is necessary. You must learn household effort/repetition rules and verify swaps, saved edits and exports. Direct free OnRotation and other planners already overlap; a paid version needs a measured setup/correction advantage. Cooking communities and demonstrations are possible routes without established access. One-time sales still need a continuing flow of households. [Current free baseline](../scouting-runs/2026-10-31-saved-opportunity-ranking.md#rank-e013).

### 12. AI companions

An adult could pay a proposed $9/month for a character and up to 1,000 text replies. Imagined use: approve a memory of an interview date, return afterward and discuss the outcome; deleting the memory should stop later retrieval.

Some customers pay and return in this category. Kindroid and other established products already offer sophisticated continuity, so another character must be preferred in practice. AI could generate conversations, assets and software. You must direct personality, judge multi-day behavior and manage memory corrections, deletion, usage costs and difficult user situations. Creator/community acquisition is unproved. This ranks low for a first launch because recurring generation and service obligations arrive before retention and acquisition are known. This is a fit/entry judgment, not a claim that the category is small. [Current memory baseline](../scouting-runs/2026-10-31-saved-opportunity-ranking.md#rank-e014).

### 13. Survey correction follow-up

A research team could pay per active study-month for shared resolution of flagged survey records. Imagined use: a supervisor explains that a duplicate ID is mistyped but a large fee is correct; a lead approves the one correction, then the team exports decisions. The software preserves that history on tomorrow's import.

AI could build the import/assignment/history workspace. You must understand record keys, waves and permissions and support fieldwork deadlines. Survey Solutions already offers comments, decisions and comment resolution; Kobo, DataSure and an organized spreadsheet also overlap. No present-day small-team unmet gap or payer has been established. Project-based revenue, confidential data and onboarding make this a weak first commitment without direct customer access. [Rechecked alternative](../scouting-runs/2026-10-31-saved-opportunity-ranking.md#rank-e015).

## What would change the order?

Discord loses first place if the AI configuration requires extensive manual work, permission checks cannot be demonstrated, or winning clients requires experience you cannot credibly show. It also loses its priority if you choose to focus only on owned products.

Shop kits move down if original design quality or buyer editing needs repeated personal rescue. Cursors move up with a preferred original theme, a Windows test device and reachable paying buyers. The object game moves up with evidence of unassisted play, voluntary fresh rounds and paid host preference.

Number checking or salvage can lead a longer-term software plan once an accessible customer supplies permitted examples, shows residual work after existing tools, and is willing to consider a scoped paid offer. Salvage especially improves with a reusable stock connection and yard partner. None of the other ideas is permanently rejected.

Existing selections of AI companions, food-assistant investigation and survey investigation remain historical user choices. This comparison neither cancels those choices nor selects a new venture for the user.
