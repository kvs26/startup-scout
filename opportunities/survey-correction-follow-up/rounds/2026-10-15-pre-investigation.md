# Survey correction follow-up — view before deeper investigation

Recorded: 2026-10-17, Asia/Kolkata, immediately before the 17 October investigation update.  
Findings then assessed: 2026-10-11; summary rewritten 2026-10-15 for clarity without new research.  
Proposition then: online issue-list follow-up workspace for smaller Indian social, education and development survey teams; proposed charge per active study-month plus possible separate setup.  
Round state then: initially researched candidate, waiting for customer evidence.

## Findings and recommendation at that time

The customer task was clear: a coordinator imports flags from an existing checking process, supervisors explain them, a research lead decides, and the team exports approved corrections. The summary illustrated a duplicate identifier, a permitted blank and a real but unusual fee amount. It explicitly identified that example as imagined.

[Indian practitioner and buyer sources](../research.md#c001) supported regular quality review and field follow-up. The strongest follow-up difficulty came from the [2019–2020 Madhya Pradesh study](../research.md#e001), whose apparent improvement after SMS feedback was confounded by later rollout and staff learning. The then-documented alternatives were [Kobo](../research.md#e009), [SurveyCTO](../research.md#e011), [DataSure](../research.md#e007), [World Bank iehfc](../research.md#e005), [DataQuad](../research.md#e018), [IPA's spreadsheet template](../research.md#e016) and a team-run shared sheet with messages. A separate shared supervisor workspace was a hypothesis, not an established missing feature. No customer interview, buyer approval, purchase, use or repeat revenue was observed.

The recommendation then was to investigate a current smaller team's handoff before building, and leave the idea if configuration or a better spreadsheet adequately resolved cases. User selection then: none recorded. The [scouting report](../../../scouting-runs/2026-10-11-researcher-workflows.md) preserves that assessment and search trail in detail. The 15 October edit improved explanation but did not revise evidence or recommendation.

## Earlier numerical and delivery assumptions

The supplier input was [US$7/month for a 1 GB Lightsail server and US$0.05/GB-month snapshots](../research.md#e017). With a hypothetical 40 GB of retained snapshots, the saved calculation was US$7 + (40 × US$0.05) = **US$9/month**. At an explicitly assumed ₹100/US$1, that was ₹900/month, or ₹4,100 below the founder's [₹5,000/month cash budget](../../../FOUNDER-PROFILE.md) before tax, domain, review, communication, monitoring and founder work. The rate was a planning assumption, not current FX or a production bill.

The support illustration was two simultaneous studies × two hours/study-week = **four hours/week**, about 21–40% of the founder's 10–19-hour week. Four hours/study-week would be **eight hours/week**, leaving 2–11 hours for all other work. Both were hypothetical. The original record did not have a customer-test procedure, price accepted by a buyer, payment lag, acquisition cost or professional review quote.
