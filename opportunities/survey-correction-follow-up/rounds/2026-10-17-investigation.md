# Survey correction follow-up — selected opportunity investigation

Recorded and assessed: 2026-10-17, Asia/Kolkata.  
Current proposition at this round: a cross-platform issue-list workspace for smaller Indian social, education and development survey teams. The team keeps its collection and checking tools; a coordinator imports flags, supervisors explain them, a research lead decides, and the team exports a decision log. Proposed charge per active study-month, with separately scoped setup; no price or terms agreed.  
Starting context: [11 October scouting report](../../../scouting-runs/2026-10-11-researcher-workflows.md), [15 October pre-investigation view](2026-10-15-pre-investigation.md), [current research](../research.md) and [founder profile](../../../FOUNDER-PROFILE.md).  
Round state: usable desk assessment; waiting for current customer and buying evidence.

## Findings and recommendation at this time

**Customer/problem:** It is well supported that Indian teams check survey answers and consult field staff. The [older Madhya Pradesh study](../research.md#e001) reported that error replies/corrections could be missed in its spreadsheet and message process; its before/after improvement cannot be cleanly attributed to SMS. A [2025-published Indian evaluation account](../research.md#e022) shows more recent checks, field monitoring and retraining at a much larger scale. Neither shows that the proposed smaller customers have a present-day unsolved handoff problem. Confidence in the general workflow is high; confidence in the target segment's pain is low.

**Alternatives and strongest contrary finding:** The proposed narrow advantage was shared follow-up, not check creation. [Survey Solutions](../research.md#e023) already documents interviewer assignment, supervisor and headquarters review, interview comments, correction requests and comment resolution. Its [software is free](../research.md#e024), with separate deployment/device costs. The 2026 release's unresolved-comment counter is for questionnaire design and was excluded from this case assessment. [SurveyCTO](../research.md#e011) also has integrated review; [Kobo](../research.md#e009) has validation, permissions and editing history; [DataSure](../research.md#e007) has checking and correction trails; [IPA's action spreadsheet](../research.md#e016) supports named responsibilities. No software was installed or observed in a customer's workflow. Survey Solutions would require a platform change for an existing study; that switching cost is unknown. The defensible question is whether a team that keeps its current platform has a specific cross-tool follow-up problem that these alternatives cannot solve at acceptable effort. Confidence that a generic checker or assignment workflow is missing is low.

**Income and sale:** The organization or survey firm is the proposed payer; coordinators, supervisors and leads are proposed users. A project manager, owner or institutional buyer may control approval, but no person with authority has been identified. The historical [IPA RFP](../research.md#e004) places quality work within a contracted study; it does not show a separate software budget or an award. Fee unit, setup, approval, invoice timing and repeat purchase are assumptions. No conversation, buying step, payment, use or renewal has occurred. Existing public organization pages are possible discovery routes, not access.

**First use and continuing use:** One study's flagged-case CSV, mapped to stable record IDs and question codes, could create an initial owner list and accepted export. That requires customer-provided mapping, role setup and agreement on what may be stored. Later batches would have to preserve cases without false matches; supervisors and leads would have to use it without constant founder help. A study ends, so another study may mean a new sale rather than continuous subscription. [C007](../research.md#c007) remains a low-confidence delivery/value hypothesis.

**Delivery and founder fit:** A synthetic prototype is within reach of the founder's software-oriented, AI-led workflow in principle; that does not prove reliable paid delivery. Correct identifiers, permitted missing answers, staff permissions, conflict handling, backups and exports need domain and technical review. Before confidential use, customer data permission and independent review are unpriced dependencies. The founder reports 10–19 hours/week for learning/building and about ₹5,000/month, with total sales/support time unrecorded, no recorded researcher buyer access and limited direct experience verifying multi-user confidential systems. An active fieldwork support obligation could consume a large share of the stated learning/building allocation if it must fit there.

**Recommendation:** Keep this as an investigation, and defer building a paid product until [T001](../tests/T001-workflow-and-alternatives.md) finds a recurring gap in a real smaller team's handoff after comparing realistic alternatives. If such a gap is visible, test a defined buying decision with an actual budget owner in [T002](../tests/T002-buying-decision.md); only then consider a safe limited pilot and later use/renewal test [T003](../tests/T003-continued-use.md). If the team can resolve cases adequately in native tools or an orderly sheet, leave this offer for now. The opportunity is more doubtful than on 11 October because a mature free platform already covers much of the proposed coordination value.

User selection at this time: the user explicitly selected this saved idea for **investigation** on 2026-10-17. No decision to build, buy, contact or sell was recorded.

## Test designs preserved at this round

- **T001 workflow and alternatives:** A coordinator, supervisor and research lead reconstruct recent closed/open flags from the actual file or platform, identify owners, replies, decisions and follow-up across a later batch, and compare with their native features and a disciplined sheet. Supporting result: repeated consequential handoff misses that remain after realistic alternatives. Contrary result: adequate native/sheet workflow. Ambiguous result: remembered annoyance without a recent case, or a staffing/training cause. No customer was recruited.
- **T002 buying decision, conditional on T001:** Trace a buyer's last fieldwork-tool purchase and approval path. After safe product preparation, present a fixed-scope pilot; a candidate *hypothetical* test price is ₹3,000 for one active study-month with one format, three roles and two setup hours. Support requires an actual paid order or binding approval, not a favorable conversation. A decline because existing tools suffice challenges the offer. No offer was made.
- **T003 continued use, conditional on safe paid trial:** Observe successive issue batches, staff use, exports, founder support and the later buyer decision for another eligible period/study. Initial payment alone cannot establish continuation. No trial occurred.

The current plan details and return paths live in [T001](../tests/T001-workflow-and-alternatives.md), [T002](../tests/T002-buying-decision.md) and [T003](../tests/T003-continued-use.md). These bullets preserve what was proposed at this round even if those mutable plans later change.
