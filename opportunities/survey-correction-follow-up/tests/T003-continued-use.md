# T003 — Does the follow-up aid earn use over a study cycle?

Prepared: 2026-10-17  
Opportunity/current offer: [summary](../summary.md), assessment 2026-10-17  
Status: Proposed and conditional on T001/T002; no product use or result.

## What this should teach us

**Claim and decision:** [C007](../research.md#c007) and the repeat-revenue part of [C004](../research.md#c004). A paid pilot would show a first buying decision, not whether a team keeps using the tool as new issue files arrive or buys for another study.

**Relevant participants:** The same pilot team's coordinator, supervisor and research lead during an active study; the budget owner at the end. A later distinct study or wave is needed to assess a new purchase. Several people on one team are still one organizational buyer.

## How to learn it

Only after permissions and reliable delivery are established, observe successive issue-file imports across a meaningful fieldwork cycle: first setup, next daily/weekly batches, reopened cases, approvals and final export. Compare each step with the team's previous method. Log actual active users by role, cases opened/resolved, cases abandoned or duplicated, elapsed time, exceptions, training requests, founder support time, infrastructure charges and data corrections verified by the customer. Ask why a person stopped using it, including cases where a spreadsheet or native review was preferred. At study end, ask the buyer to make a real renewal or next-study purchase decision under stated price and terms; record delays or a decline.

**Effort, cost and dependencies:** Requires a live study, approved minimal data scope, product reliability, independent review, user-authorized customer activity and support capacity. Duration follows that study's actual issue cycle; the 10–19-hour founder week and ₹5,000/month budget may constrain it. Costs and support are to be measured, not assumed.

## How to interpret the result

| Possible observation | Effect on next decision | What it cannot establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Relevant staff use it for successive batches without founder prompting, exports are accepted, support stays manageable and the buyer purchases the next eligible period/study. | Strengthens continued-value and repeat-sale claims for this setting; reassess economics with actual costs. | Broad market demand or low-cost acquisition. |
| Use stops after setup, decisions revert to existing tools, matching errors appear, support exceeds capacity or buyer declines another period. | Revise or leave the offer according to the specific cause. | That no other team could use a different offer. |
| The study ends before enough new issues arise, or renewal is delayed for unrelated funding reasons. | Keep continuing value unresolved and record the actual constraint. | Retention or churn rate. |
