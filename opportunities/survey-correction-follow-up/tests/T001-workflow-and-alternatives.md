# T001 — Does a current team lose track of flagged cases after using its existing tools?

Prepared: 2026-10-17  
Opportunity/current offer: [summary](../summary.md), assessment 2026-10-17  
Status: Proposed; no recruitment, conversation or result.

## What this should teach us

**Claim and decision:** [C002](../research.md#c002), [C003](../research.md#c003) and [C007](../research.md#c007). If the team's present-day handoff is reliable using its current platform or an orderly spreadsheet, stop this product direction. If a recurring, costly gap persists across daily quality files and supervisors, specify the smallest cross-platform aid worth testing.

**Relevant participants:** A coordinator/data manager who maintains the issue list, a field supervisor who investigates flags, and the research lead who approves a correction, ideally from one smaller Indian social, education or development survey team in active/recent fieldwork. The project manager or owner should explain who controls tool choice; that role may be separate from the daily users. A second distinct organization would help check that a single team's process is not mistaken for a segment pattern; no fixed sample size is a pass rule. Public organization pages in [E014](../research.md#e014) and [E015](../research.md#e015) are discovery routes, not willing participants.

## How to learn it

Ask the coordinator to choose a recent real flag that needed a human answer, preferably one closed and one still open. If any artifact is shown, use a permitted redacted/synthetic copy; do not take respondent identifiers, phone numbers or raw survey data into these records. Walk backward from the final dataset decision to the first flag: which file/platform produced it; how the case was identified; who received it; what the supervisor did; how replies or disagreements were recorded; who approved; how the correction was applied; and how an open case survives the next day's file. Ask what happened the last time a case was late or lost, including time spent chasing and consequences. Record whether that was a software limitation, unclear responsibility, staffing, training, connectivity or a one-off event.

Observe the actual current setup if permitted: Kobo, SurveyCTO, Survey Solutions, DataSure, scripts, spreadsheet and messaging. Ask the participant to show how they would resolve the same case with their current features and with a disciplined issue/action sheet. Show only a one-page sketch of the proposed import → assignment → supervisor explanation → lead decision → export flow **after** understanding the existing process. Ask which extra steps or risks the sketch would introduce. Do not treat politeness or feature requests as buying evidence.

Record the participant's role, organization/study context, date, platform, whether the file was direct or described, recent case counts if documented, unresolved ages, handoff steps, time estimates and their basis, exceptions, satisfaction and contrary cases. Preserve notes as one result per underlying conversation/event under `results/`; the same team's coordinator and supervisor accounts are not independent customers.

**Effort, cost and dependencies:** Founder time to learn role terminology, recruit through public routes and conduct/record a walkthrough; duration and acquisition cost unknown. Requires participant consent/permission for any screen or file and no outreach is authorized by this plan. A real-data comparison or installed product trial would need customer access and technical review beyond this first test.

## How to interpret the result

| Possible observation | Effect on next decision | What it cannot establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A current team repeatedly chases the same unresolved issues across files/messages, can show lost or late decisions, and cannot solve the specific handoff adequately in its existing licensed/free tools. | Specify the exact missing step and move to [T002](T002-buying-decision.md) with a narrower offer. | Prevalence across India, purchase, reliable product delivery or repeat use. |
| Native review or a clear shared sheet already lets relevant people see owners, explanations, approvals and open items with tolerable effort. | Leave or substantially revise the offer; a separate login is unlikely to help this team. | Whether another, meaningfully different segment has the problem. |
| The participant recalls annoyance but cannot show a recent case, or the difficulty was caused by staffing/training. | Keep the claim unresolved or test a process/configuration aid before software. | That a software gap exists. |

The decision turns on an observed recurring handoff failure **after comparing the realistic current alternative**, not on a preset count of interviews or flags.
