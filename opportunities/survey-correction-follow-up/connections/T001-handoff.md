## Current offer and limits

The strongest objection is [Survey Solutions' documented interviewer–supervisor–headquarters workflow](../research.md#e023), plus [Kobo/SurveyCTO/DataSure and an orderly spreadsheet](../research.md#c003). A conversation should reveal a **current, specific handoff gap after those alternatives**, not seek general praise for better survey data.
