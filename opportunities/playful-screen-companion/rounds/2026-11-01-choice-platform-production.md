## Findings and recommendation at this time

Free competitors already advertise funny page-aware reactions, custom imports/uploads and friend nudges. Squishpets also offers trying before installation. Therefore calmness, a name/color choice, an AI label, a sharing button or a free web try alone provide weak reasons to choose a new pet. Existing competitors remain demand and quality baselines, rather than automatic grounds for abandoning the category. New personal-character requests and a self-reported paid pet with setup trouble strengthen the importance of particular identity and usable delivery, without proving a broad missing feature.

**Recommended entry point to test:** a personal, playful gift from a friend, followed by optional everyday companion use. A sender chooses an original prepared character and message; the recipient immediately plays in a web page and can make a return gift. The proposed advantage is that complete personal journey. An ordinary GIF or free pet may already satisfy it. Gifting could earn an isolated smile without continuing use; no preference or referral outcome has been demonstrated. [C003 and evidence](../research.md#c003--a-reason-to-choose-us-must-survive-the-free-alternatives).

**Platform recommendation:** make the first encounter touch-friendly on the web; it only occupies its own page. Android is relevant to native social-media/game use but adds explicit overlay permission and publishing/testing work. A Chrome companion stays within supported computer webpages; a native desktop app is a different scope. No final persistent platform or iOS overlay is selected. Current Google Play facts: $25 registration, and new personal accounts face 12 continuously opted-in testers for 14 days before applying for production. Steam has a $100/app fee. These are supplier conditions, not a launch estimate. [C004](../research.md#c004--platform-recommendation-separates-trying-from-keeping).

**AI recommendation:** reuse a small set of approved appearances and reactions before considering generation for every visitor. Code-driven movement of approved art is an alternative, whose expressiveness is untested. Ludo's documentation supplies a possible 30-credit trial and $20/month, 250-credit web-only Indie option; the $15 annual equivalent requires $180 upfront. Current sprite-animation charges vary from 1.5–20 credits plus possible repairs. These prices make a bounded asset check researchable, not proof of our quality, hours or all-in fit within ₹5,000/month. No INR conversion, generation or cost forecast was made. [C005](../research.md#c005--ai-and-budget-reusable-assets-before-unlimited-custom-generation).
