# T002 — Does a personal pet gift earn a try and a desire to keep it?

Prepared: 2026-11-01. [Current proposition](../summary.md). **Status: proposed; not selected or executed.** The user asked for research, with why-choose-us first and platform/AI feasibility also covered. No production, spending, outreach or test is authorized by this plan.

## Claim and decision

[C003](../research.md#c003--a-reason-to-choose-us-must-survive-the-free-alternatives) proposes personal gifting as the first reason to choose the screen pet. Distinguish three decisions: a sender chooses to make/share one; a recipient voluntarily opens/plays; the recipient later wants the companion around. A one-off smile does not establish the third. The outcome could support this entry point, shift toward deeper own-pet customization, or weaken the gifting direction.

Relevant participants are people who actually exchange playful messages/gifts with a consenting friend. Include current pet users and people without an existing pet habit; record those groups separately. User and eventual payer may differ. Nobody has been recruited. Contact preparation belongs to startup-customer-connect if selected.

## Material needed from the separate production workflow

One original character, a calm idle/sleep state and one clearly readable comic response. Example: a snack-loving dragon hides fries when tapped. It should keep the same recognizable appearance, react to touch, stop/hide immediately and work inside a phone/computer webpage. A sender can add a name/message and preview it. A recipient sees the gift without an account/install requirement, plays, and can make a new return-gift copy. Optional persistence must be explained honestly as a later extension/app; a button label is not a working companion.

Start with reusable approved art and animations; full photo-to-pet generation is unnecessary for this claim. Record actual generation settings, credits, attempts, rejected outputs, any manual correction and founder hours. An appearance-consistency failure or unfunny reaction requires fixing the material before interpreting rejection as absence of market demand. No sample exists. Supplier allowance and price facts are in [E016](../research.md#e016--ai-animation-tooling-has-a-bounded-trial-not-proven-output-quality), not a production estimate or approval to subscribe.

## Later procedure

Ask the sender about a real recent playful message and why it was sent. Once usable material exists, let them compare an ordinary GIF, an existing free pet try and the proposed gift flow. Keep message relevance and visual quality reasonably comparable; vary presentation order and record material differences. They may choose an existing option or nothing. A prompted share is recorded as prompted, never organic acquisition.

If they choose to send to an agreed participant, let the recipient decide whether to open/play and whether to make one back. Record device, setup attempts, completed first play, sharing choice and reason. Ask about actual later use after several days, and desire for persistence separately from clicking an unimplemented button. That follow-up is an early novelty check, not established long-term retention. Record both people's accounts independently without treating the sender's report as the recipient's own experience.

| Outcome | Decision implication | Remaining limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sender prefers the personal pet; recipient voluntarily plays and creates a return gift. | Supports further testing of the gift journey. | Does not establish large-scale virality or payment. |
| Recipient enjoys once but does not want continued presence. | Gifting may work; persistent-companion demand remains weak for this person. | Does not prove a viable gift business either. |
| Ordinary GIF/free competitor is sufficient or preferred. | Reconsider what meaningful value our pet adds. | A poor sample or mismatched recipient can confound the result. |
| Own-pet likeness matters more than names/messages. | Reassess custom production and its costs before choosing it. | A request is not a paid order or quality benchmark. |
| People want persistence mainly on one device/context. | Informs Android versus computer scope. | Stated preference is weaker than actual installation/continued use. |

No universal sample size, pass percentage or acquisition forecast is imposed. Costs and participant access remain unpriced; the founder's available time/budget are constraints to measure against.
