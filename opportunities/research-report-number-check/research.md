# Research-report number checking — research

Current claim assessment: 2026-10-18. Global B2B, initially smaller market-research/specialist consulting agencies delivering editable PPTX reports from approved Excel results. [Self-contained explanation](summary.md). Scout recommendation only; no user selection or investigator run.

Observations remain canonical in the [fifty-idea scouting run](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-18-scope-selection.md#i017). This record links them rather than copying evidence entries.

## C001

**Claim:** Some commercial research/consulting teams still need repetitive final numerical review of slide reports.

**Assessment:** Moderately supported for existence of the workflow, weak for prevalence or monetizable cost. Original commercial accounts describe manual report preparation and mistakes; a current agency page confirms slide-report delivery.

**Evidence:** [Analyst account](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-18-scope-selection.md#i017-e001), [small research-firm account](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-18-scope-selection.md#i017-e002), [agency service pages](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-18-scope-selection.md#i017-e010).

**Contrary/limits:** Self-reports are selected and from 2024; not a current representative sample. Better training, peer review or source linking may prevent the mistakes. No monetary loss measured.

**Next check:** Reconstruct recent report preparation/review with relevant agencies and identify residual work after their current process.

## C002

**Claim:** Explicit source-workbook comparison of finished reports can offer enough extra value to be chosen over current tools.

**Assessment:** Unresolved, low confidence. It is a testable entry hypothesis, not demonstrated differentiation.

**Contrary evidence:** [Native Excel links](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-18-scope-selection.md#i017-e006), [thinkcell](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-18-scope-selection.md#i017-e003), [Displayr](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-18-scope-selection.md#i017-e005), [Anthropic checking workflow](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-18-scope-selection.md#i017-e008) and [FlexTabs generation](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-18-scope-selection.md#i017-e009). These challenge a generic AI-deck-checking product.

**Limits:** No product trials or comparison on real files. More precise coverage may add setup work. Unsupported images/prose must not silently pass.

**Next check:** Compare agreed supported objects against a senior review and existing tools on permitted past files; measure total effort, false positives and missed discrepancies.

## C003

**Claim:** A director will pay $99/report or later $199/month for ten reports, with recurring use.

**Assessment:** Unvalidated. Prices, caps and renewal are assumptions. Existing software list prices establish alternatives, not acceptance of ours.

**Economic links:** [Offer and contribution calculations](summary.md#building-and-operating-requirements), [shared supplier evidence](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-18-scope-selection.md#shared-e006). Labor sensitivity is more consequential than base hosting.

**Next check:** Identify report frequency, budget owner, current review cost, acceptable cap and whether a paid comparison is possible. Do not count interest or a free-file submission as a sale.

## C004

**Claim:** Relevant first customers can be found through public research-agency pages.

**Assessment:** Public discovery is supported; access, response and conversion remain low-confidence.

**Evidence:** [DJS workflow example](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-18-scope-selection.md#i017-e010), [ResearchOps/B2B International routes](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-18-scope-selection.md#shared-e007).

**Limits:** DJS is not qualified as a small-firm target or confirmed buyer. Communities are not advertising permission. No contact or warm relationship exists.

**Next check:** Qualify a small set of actual agencies by report format, volume, size, tools and decision role before preparing a customer-connect handoff.

## C005

**Claim:** A solo founder with ten to nineteen hours/week and approximately ₹5,000/month can deliver a dependable initial offer.

**Assessment:** Conditional and unproven. A bounded local comparison is more manageable than an entire reporting platform, but this is engineering judgment. Founder file-format and research-method competence is unassessed.

**Dependencies:** Explicit mappings, supported formats, reviewer examples, technical quality checks, file permissions, onboarding, exception labor and acquisition. Specialist costs and payment-provider eligibility are unknown. The $5/$20 variable estimates are unbenchmarked; the 5% allowance is hypothetical.

**Next check:** Obtain a practitioner walkthrough and reviewed example corpus; estimate actual implementation/review effort and supplier costs. Reassess if paid review or production operations exceed the budget.
