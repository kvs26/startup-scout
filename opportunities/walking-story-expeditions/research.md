# Walking-story expeditions — claim register

Current assessment: 2026-10-18. Scope: an original, finite walking adventure for English-reading adults, sold by expedition. This records claim judgments; original observations remain in [scouting assessment I033](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-18-consumer-apps.md#i033). Promotion creates no new customer evidence.

## C001

**Claim:** some adults repeatedly enjoy and spend on games in which walking advances an illustrated world or narrative.

**Assessment:** moderately supported. Original accounts describe purchases and substantial repeated use; multiple current suppliers offer paid content. The sources are selected storefront users, not representative demand estimates or verified receipts.

**Evidence:** [Fantasy Hike purchase/discovery accounts E001](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-18-consumer-apps.md#i033-e001), [Walkr accounts E003](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-18-consumer-apps.md#i033-e003), [The Walk E004](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-18-consumer-apps.md#i033-e004).

**Limits and next check:** category spending does not establish our story's appeal. Observe people choosing among actual samples and returning over time; distinguish liking the art from paid use.

## C002

**Claim:** a finite original expedition can earn an additional purchase alongside strong incumbents.

**Assessment:** plausible, low-to-moderate confidence; not validated. Requests for more themed content support the mechanism. Familiar-fiction attachment and substantial free alternatives challenge transfer to an unknown original world.

**Evidence:** [E001](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-18-consumer-apps.md#i033-e001) and [E004](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-18-consumer-apps.md#i033-e004). Contrary supply: [Fantasy Hike E002](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-18-consumer-apps.md#i033-e002), [Walkr E003](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-18-consumer-apps.md#i033-e003), [WalkScape E005](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-18-consumer-apps.md#i033-e005).

**Next check:** preference for a complete original sample and actual acceptance of a stated continuation price. An $8 price and a four-to-eight-week journey remain design assumptions, not source facts.

## C003

**Claim:** short demonstrations can acquire enough buyers economically.

**Assessment:** channel mechanism moderately supported; founder access and economics unknown. One reported video-to-purchase path verifies that the sequence can occur. It establishes neither a repeatable campaign nor low acquisition cost.

**Evidence:** [E001](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-18-consumer-apps.md#i033-e001). No creator agreement, audience, conversion or advertising result exists.

**Next check:** assess a concrete sample and suitable creator/content route, including preparation time and partner terms, before treating views as customers. No outreach was sent.

## C004

**Claim:** a solo founder can deliver an enjoyable, reliable first expedition within available time and cash.

**Assessment:** conditional, low-to-moderate confidence. Authored local content and a single platform can keep scope bounded. Art, native data handling and consumer support are unassessed skills in the [founder profile](../../FOUNDER-PROFILE.md). An AI-coding workflow is not proof of independent debugging competence.

**Evidence:** operating requirements and explicit assumptions in [I033 delivery/economics](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-18-consumer-apps.md#i033-delivery-founder-fit-and-growth); [shared supplier terms](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-18-consumer-apps.md#shared). Existing games prove the category is technically possible, not that this founder can reproduce their quality cheaply.

**Next check:** six-landmark pacing exercise, a real-device step-sync check and actual art/editing quotes. A manual prototype can test taste but cannot establish final mobile convenience.

## C005

**Claim:** repeat expedition purchases can finance a growing content catalog.

**Assessment:** unproved, low confidence. The model has a natural repeat unit and low necessary runtime AI cost, but no observed repurchase or costs for our content. Revenue is not profit; fees, refunds, acquisition, content production, compatibility and labor remain material.

**Evidence:** [I033 scenario](../../scouting-runs/2026-10-18-consumer-apps.md#i033-reaching-customers-and-making-money). The $8 price, 20% reserve, $0.20 variable allocation, $400 content expense and support sensitivity are assumptions. They are not supplier quotes or a prediction.

**Next check:** production time and cost for one acceptable sample, then the paid continuation/repurchase behavior. Do not infer repeat revenue from free users completing a story.

## Production clarification — 2026-10-18

The user clarified that all digital production will use AI, while they direct the work and handle people-facing activities; this is recorded in the [founder profile](../../FOUNDER-PROFILE.md). This updates delivery assumptions, not customer evidence. The initial claim assessments above remain as dated history; this section supersedes their manual-craft and commissioned-production assumptions.

**C004 reassessment:** delivery remains conditional and unmeasured. Lack of independent illustration, writing or coding skill alone does not rule out this workflow. The relevant check is whether AI-generated code, scenes and artwork can be revised into a coherent, enjoyable sample and reliable app within the actual budget and time. Create six landmarks with a consistent visual and narrative brief, record generation/revision costs and founder hours, and separately check step import, deduplication and saved progress on a real device. Customer feedback helps judge appeal. A specialist is not an assumed production requirement; any dependency discovered during testing must have a concrete reason.

**C005 reassessment:** repeat-purchase economics remain unproved. The earlier $400 commissioned-content scenario is an optional comparison, not the baseline. Estimate AI tool subscriptions/credits, retries, integration, testing and founder direction for one acceptable sample before extrapolating catalog costs. No actual production-cost reduction, new price acceptance or repeat purchase has been observed.

**Recommendation:** investigate through a small AI-produced sample. Claims C001–C003 retain their existing limits. This clarification does not establish a stronger commercial ranking or imply that the other 49 ideas were reassessed. No prototype was built or customer contacted in this update.
