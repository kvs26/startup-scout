# Wholesale message orders

Folder identity: wholesale-message-orders  
Current findings assessed: 2026-10-10  
Stage: Initially researched candidate; target segment and purchase reason unresolved

Wholesale order-desk staff receive messages, photos and calls, identify the buyer and products, resolve quantities/pack sizes, then enter orders into their business system. The proposed product converts forwarded messages into catalog-matched drafts for staff review and export. A small food/beverage segment is a proposed starting point, not the original author's verified category. Country and language remain open.

**Illustration, not an observed use:** A shop messages “same as Tuesday, but two fewer cases of cola.” Staff forward it, select the buyer and review the proposed repeat order. Ambiguous pack sizes remain flagged until resolved.

**Proposed offer:** Monthly per-branch service plus catalog/import setup; price unset.

## What we have learned

A practitioner thread describes notebook capture, evening entry and searching chats. Identities, geography and representativeness are uncertain; discovery also surfaced many developer pitches and cross-posts ([C001](../research.md#c001)). Both Orderlion and Choco already advertise messaging-to-order automation, including preserving the customer's channel ([C002](../research.md#c002)).

The remaining proposition requires a specific segment poorly served by incumbents. Catalog aliases, packs, customer pricing, amendments and imports create substantial onboarding/support work; provider and integration costs remain unpriced ([C003](../research.md#c003)). IFDA lists real companies, but small-company fit and access to buyers are unverified ([C004](../research.md#c004)).
