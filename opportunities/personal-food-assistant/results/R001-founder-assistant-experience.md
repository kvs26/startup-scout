## Interpretation

The experience gives a more concrete unmet outcome: an actionable suggestion for a specific food item with a usable link, instead of advice that leaves the user searching again. A direct dish link is an inferred acceptance criterion from the phrase “link of food”; whether a restaurant menu link with the item clearly identified is sufficient still needs observing. It is not established that any particular competitor or properly connected assistant cannot provide this.

Possible explanations remain product quality, missing live-data access, an unconnected assistant, inadequate location/context, or limitations of dish-link support. A clarification was requested: which assistant, and whether it was connected to Swiggy/Zomato versus answering from chat/web search. No answer at initial save. No causal explanation selected yet.

Friend recruitment is now reported feasible for the first trial. Their willingness, count, ordering frequency and representativeness are not established. Friend feedback can reveal usability failures but does not establish independent demand, economical acquisition or willingness to pay.

## Effect on work

T001 now checks the exact dish/outlet/variant, usable destination, current availability, remaining manual search and total choosing time. Record a link opening the app homepage, wrong outlet, restaurant menu or exact dish separately. Never manufacture a URL or count an untested link as actionable. Start with friends only, in whatever small number actually agrees; wider recruitment is a later unresolved question.

The recommendation remains compare before building; the target improvement is sharper. Economics and price confidence are unchanged. See the [follow-up round](../rounds/2026-10-27-actionable-food-links.md).
