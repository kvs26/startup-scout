## Revised T001 design preserved here

Retain past-choice walkthroughs, ordinary-app baseline, fair connected-assistant setup, later-session preference recall, optional after-meal feedback and voluntary return. First cohort is friends only; use the number who actually agree, not a required five-to-eight target. Track friendship/researcher help and refrain from generalizing purchase intent or referral-driven acquisition costs.

For every recommendation record specificity (general advice / dish+outlet / exact variant), source and time, whether a link was provided, where it actually opens (broken / app home / restaurant menu / intended dish), stock/serviceability for that participant, price certainty, remaining search/taps and time to an acceptable order-ready choice. Check before ordering; checkout remains optional. A menu link that still requires hunting is not equal to an exact dish destination. If exact linking is unsupported, report that openly and measure the fallback's usefulness. Links must come from supported sources and be checked; never invent them.

Interpretation: repeated generic answers or missing/broken destinations support an actionable-choice gap for the tested configuration. If proper connection/configuration resolves it, distinguish setup assistance from a missing product capability. If exact dish links are impossible through available authorized access, assess a measured fallback rather than promise one-tap ordering. Positive friend comments without actual use do not establish value. Unprompted repeated use informs convenience but does not establish paid demand.
