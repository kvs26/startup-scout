# T002 — Will someone pay, return and renew for the demonstrated improvement?

Prepared 2026-10-27. Status: **conditional proposed test; not executed**. Claims [C004–C007](../research.md#c004). Depends on [T001](T001-existing-alternatives.md) identifying a specific advantage and on lawful, workable delivery access. [Economics](../economics.md) supplies assumptions to replace with observations.

## Decision, participants and material

Decide whether the improvement supports a consumer business. Invite relevant adults who encountered the problem in T001, including people who declined the assistant. Separate friends, incentivized participants and independently recruited prospects. The individual uses, pays and approves; a gym/creator introducing them is not the buyer.

Required material for the user's separate production workflow: a limited but usable delivery-choice experience that demonstrates the selected improvement, displays current information with sources/uncertainty, lets users edit/delete preferences, and measures assistance and cost. No product is built by this investigation. If a human delivers recommendations during the trial, disclose that and record every minute. Do not sell a hypothetical integration as working software.

## Buying decision and subsequent use

Proposed first offer: ₹149 prepaid for 30 days, up to 20 complete decision sessions, a saved preference profile and optional meal feedback. Food is purchased separately. Define delivery coverage, session counting, service hours if human-assisted, cancellation/refund handling and any taxes before offering it. A session covers finding and refining a shortlist; unsuccessful delivery of the promised recommendation should not silently consume the allowance. Price and terms are proposals, not accepted commitments.

Show the demonstrated experience and the strongest available free/existing alternative. Make a real, optional purchase offer only when delivery and payment handling are ready and the user authorizes execution. Record offered terms, actual payment, refusal, reason, refund and source of recruitment. Statements of interest and refundable deposits are not completed purchase-plus-use evidence.

For buyers, observe a full paid month, including self-initiated use, skipped opportunities, feedback burden, suggestion acceptance, after-meal satisfaction, complaints, support minutes and tool/model costs. Record every eligible occasion where the person chose another method if they volunteer that information; do not infer use from absence. At the end, offer a second paid period and observe use after renewal. A renewal charge without subsequent use is weaker than active continued value. No automatic charge should be mistaken for a deliberate choice.

Keep offer terms stable within the cohort. If ₹49/five sessions or another price is tested later, record a new version and participant context; small nonrandom cohorts cannot establish price elasticity. Incentives, discounts or paid meals must be recorded and not netted invisibly against purchases.

## Interpretation and costs

| Observation | Meaning and next step |
| --- | --- |
| Actual payment followed by voluntary repeated use, a reason to prefer it, and intentional renewal/use | Supports continuing with that segment; inspect contribution and labor before expanding |
| People like it but choose a free alternative at the real price | Supports usefulness but challenges paid offer; examine specific value before cutting price |
| Payment occurs but use fades after novelty | Acquisition is possible; recurring value remains weak |
| Users return only with human curation or heavy reminders | Assess assisted-service economics separately; not proof of software retention |
| Quality is good but provider, acquisition or support costs consume receipts | Change economics/distribution or stop that version |
| Access outages or unavailable data dominate | Delivery test unresolved; do not interpret this as absent customer need |

Do not declare success from a preset universal customer count. Continue only if observed buying reasons, actual repeated value and a plausible path to covering costs agree; seek contrary cases before generalizing. The first small cohort can disprove easy assumptions but cannot establish market-wide retention.

Effort: at least one paid month plus a later renewal/use period, after setup. Costs include the required sample, approved supplier access, gateway fees, support and recruitment; quotes remain unknown. At 20 sessions × five human minutes, even one manually served buyer costs 100 minutes/month before support. The founder's time/budget constrains cohort size. No spending, outreach, charging or live test is authorized by this plan.

Next owner after T001: **startup-opportunity-investigator** revises the offer and confirms readiness; the **user's separate production workflow** supplies the sample if selected; **startup-customer-connect** prepares an honest purchase conversation. Actual results return to the investigator with this opportunity path, event dates, terms and artifacts. Neither this plan nor its handoff is a completed test.
