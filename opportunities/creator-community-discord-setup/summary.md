# Creator-community setup and private support in Discord

We would sell small creator-program operators a working Discord community setup. Discord is an online space divided into discussion channels and access roles. The client creates and owns a new server, approves who should see what, and pays us to configure the welcome flow, agreed channels/roles, one private support-ticket panel and basic agreed moderation, then teach their team to maintain it. The starting model is a fixed-scope service; repeat setups and separately contracted updates could follow.

**Imagined example:** a creator joins, reads the rules and chooses their campaign role. A “Start here” message says, “Choose your campaign; read its brief; post your introduction.” Pressing Help opens a private conversation with staff. We personally check that another ordinary member cannot read it. At handoff, the owner uses the instructions to add a dummy campaign channel and change a staff assignment without our intervention. Contractor access is removed. This illustrates the proposed job; no server or customer result exists.
