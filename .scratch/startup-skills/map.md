# Design the startup skill suite and ship the first scout

Labels: wayfinder:map
Status: resolved

## Destination

A research-backed blueprint for a startup skill suite covering discovery, validation, customers, business model, costs, marketing, and sales, with working and behaviorally checked skills for startup idea scouting, deeper opportunity investigation, and customer-connection preparation delivered before this map closes.
