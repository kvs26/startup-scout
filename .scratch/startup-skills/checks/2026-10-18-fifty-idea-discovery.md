# Fifty-idea discovery revision — 2026-10-18

## Requested behavior

For open discovery, deeply research at least 50 distinct ideas, compare the full pool and recommend the strongest. If none merits recommendation, explain the top 3–5 researched discussion options so the founder can choose despite the scout's reservations. Existing competition is not a rejection reason. Preserve commercial quality, meaningful upside, diversity and founder fit.

## Changes

- The scout instructions, discovery guide and commercial guide now require 50 completed deep assessments before a final open-discovery result. A request for one recommendation does not reduce the research minimum. A specific supplied idea or explicit smaller research request remains scoped to that request.
- Eight depth requirements cover the customer, demand, alternatives, buying reason, acquisition, economics, delivery/fit/growth and a challenged verdict. Research completion is separate from a positive business verdict. Duplicate concepts, brief screens and source-only unfinished research do not count.
- Added the individual idea-assessment template and expanded the campaign report into an index, full comparison, completion audit and resumable progress record. Incomplete research must show the actual count and precise remaining tasks.
- The founder profile, shared record/research pointers and saved-opportunity comparison template were aligned with the new scout-owned workflow. Evidence retains one canonical home; promotion and later corrections preserve history.
