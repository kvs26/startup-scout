## Question

What decision-ready outputs should the suite provide for customers, alternatives and positioning, revenue model and pricing, delivery costs and cash needs, marketing channels, sales, onboarding, retention, and operating feasibility?

Agree the minimum useful depth and which topics are conditional on the idea. Keep customer, user, economic buyer, and purchasing authority distinguishable where relevant. Decide how assumptions, ranges, and scenario arithmetic may be used when observations are unavailable; identify the stage at which each question matters without forcing every scout idea through a full business plan.

Preserve the solo starting situation as context while keeping opportunities open within the agreed SaaS, software, and AI product scope. State the preparation and unresolved evidence passed to future infrastructure and first-customer maps. Define the commercial depth needed by the requested deeper investigation skill. Determine whether customer experiment design belongs in validation while actual experiment execution remains a separate authorized activity. Consult grilling and domain-modeling; do not predetermine one skill per topic.
