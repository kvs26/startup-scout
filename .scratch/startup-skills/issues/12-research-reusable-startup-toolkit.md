## Question

Which existing open-source projects, maintained discovery resources, APIs, and MCP servers could usefully support this startup skill suite rather than rebuilding capabilities that already exist?

The user requested this research during [Decide shared opportunity records and skill handoffs](06-decide-shared-records.md), noting that different tasks may need different tools and suggesting maintained idea lists as one possible resource. Investigate both integrated startup research projects and focused resources for discovery, source retrieval, deeper investigation, and customer-connection preparation. Do not assume that an MCP server, idea list, or complete startup agent is automatically useful.

Use current primary sources: official repositories, maintainer documentation, first-party data/API descriptions, licenses, and release or commit evidence. Produce a bounded, useful shortlist rather than an exhaustive catalog. For each candidate state the job it serves, fit and limits for the agreed suite, maintenance evidence with dates where available, license or reuse terms, hosted versus local operation, cost/access/credential requirements when verifiable, and whether ordinary browsing is sufficient. Distinguish open-source code from freely accessible content and paid hosted services. Report inaccessible or unverified details honestly; no installation or runtime testing is required to describe documented capabilities.

Specifically examine whether useful maintained idea/problem lists exist and whether their entries link to customer evidence. Ideas, popularity, repositories, and startup lists supply leads rather than proof of demand. Account for market/geographic coverage and commercial reuse limitations. Compare complementary tools and note where no good fit was established.

Write the findings and source links directly under `## Answer` in this ticket, following the map's no-Git research exception. Do not initialize Git or create an extra report. Research may recommend candidates for evaluation but does not adopt tools, install servers, sign up for accounts, send outreach, or settle the human decisions in the workflow or blueprint tickets.

## Answer

### 2026-10-03 — Research resolution

**Useful pieces already exist. The strongest starting point is a small collection of discovery sources plus optional research/retrieval tools, with our skills owning the opportunity records and decisions.** No reviewed project established a complete replacement for the agreed scout, repeat investigation, customer-results intake, and shared-record workflow. That is a bounded research finding, not a claim that no such project exists.

This was documentation research using primary repositories, maintainer documentation, and a sample source discussion. Nothing was installed or executed; quality, reliability, exact running cost, and compatibility with this workspace remain untested. Dates below are observed publication/commit dates, not search-engine crawl dates. Some GitHub views were cached or incomplete, so the dated activity establishes activity by that date, not the last-ever update. Recommendations are inputs to the blueprint decision, not adopted integrations.

### Discovery resources

**Startup Ideas by fayerman-source — strongest idea-list candidate for a small trial.** The September 2026 edition contains 371 hypotheses derived from August discussions in 26 practitioner communities, with source-thread links and downloadable JSON. Earlier editions are retained. The repository identifies its content as MIT-licensed and publicly readable without an account; the linked 1mil.app service is separate and its full commercial terms were not evaluated. A browser is sufficient; importing a whole dataset is unnecessary for the first scout. Its English Reddit sampling and examples of US-specific workflows should not determine our geographic coverage. Its own positioning/scoring also emphasizes solo-founder suitability, which we must not inherit as an exclusion rule. [Repository, editions, data and license](https://github.com/fayerman-source/startup-ideas).

The links are useful but still need checking: one medical-billing idea links to a person reporting a friend's insurance-tier problem, with conflicting explanations in the replies. That establishes a reported problem worth investigating, not a verified buyer interview or willingness to pay. Two proposed products in the list link to that same thread, so they are not independent demand observations. The sample shows why we should preserve source identity and distinguish an author's proposed solution from the underlying account. [Sample source discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/CodingandBilling/comments/1vfaugc/tier_1_doctors_biller_is_tier_2/), [idea list](https://github.com/fayerman-source/startup-ideas).

**Y Combinator Requests for Startups — complementary expert prompts.** The official page has a Fall 2026 edition and previous seasonal editions. It names problems that YC partners and contributors want founders to tackle. Useful for discovering overlooked directions and terminology; it represents investor/contributor interests and includes US-focused and hardware topics outside our software-only boundary. It does not supply customer evidence for every proposal. Freely readable hosted content with no account or setup needed; no open-content reuse license was established in the inspected page. Link and summarize selected prompts rather than treating it as an openly licensed dataset. Ordinary browsing is sufficient. [Official RFS](https://www.ycombinator.com/rfs).

**Coverage gap:** these two sources are not a worldwide problem census. In particular, no equally suitable India-focused maintained, evidence-linked idea dataset was established in this bounded pass. The official Smart India Hackathon problem-statement page failed to load through the research tool; that is an access failure, not evidence that it is absent or unmaintained. Keep the broader sources already researched in [Research discovery sources across markets and business types](03-research-discovery-coverage.md) and add geography/industry-specific sources when an opportunity calls for them. [Attempted official SIH page](https://www.sih.gov.in/sih2025PS).

An open-source wrapper does not grant new rights over the pages it retrieves or make its hosted provider free. For every retrieval option, country/language coverage, inaccessible sources and missing dates still need recording. The tools do not independently establish demand or remove source bias.

### More integrated projects

**GPT Researcher — worth evaluating if delegated desk research becomes a bottleneck.** It documents research over web sources and local documents, source tracking and report generation. The main project is Apache-2.0; its separate `gptr-mcp` server is MIT. Local Python or Docker operation is documented, with model/search credentials required for the default setup; model/search service costs remain separate. Main-project commits show retrieval improvements and fixes on **2026-08-23**. The separate MCP wrapper's latest maintenance date was not established. [Main project](https://github.com/assafelovic/gpt-researcher), [commit history](https://github.com/assafelovic/gpt-researcher/commits/main/), [MCP wrapper](https://github.com/assafelovic/gptr-mcp).

Its documented report workflow could help gather source material, but the inspected material does not establish our exact opportunity identity, evidence deduplication, pasted-result intake, or comparison-summary contract. Our investigator would still reconcile its output into the opportunity folder and check its citations. A readable report is not proof of validation. Existing browsing plus a research subagent is the simpler baseline; do not add a second research orchestrator unless a comparison shows a benefit.

**VIVPM Market Research Crew — relevant reference, weak adoption candidate.** This is an integrated idea-to-report project covering market, competitors, customer insights, product strategy and business analysis. It documents local CrewAI/Streamlit operation and Gemini/Tavily keys; hosted deployment is also described. API use can carry provider charges. It illustrates possible task decomposition, but generated personas, projections and go/no-go output cannot replace our human-owned selection and observed customer evidence. The README claims MIT licensing, yet its LICENSE link failed and the root listing did not display that file. Eight commits were visible, but dated maintenance could not be verified. Therefore code reuse and operational adoption remain unestablished. Its self-reported evaluation score is not evidence of quality for our work. Browse it for design comparison only. [Repository and documented workflow](https://github.com/VIVPM/market-research-crew).

**LangChain Open Deep Research — reference only.** MIT code with configurable models, search tools and MCP connections; documented local LangGraph operation needs model/search configuration, with separate provider costs and optional hosted deployment. It is a useful research-system/evaluation example, but GitHub explicitly says it was **archived on 2026-08-21**. Do not recommend it as an actively maintained dependency. Ordinary browsing or the current research-subagent workflow avoids taking ownership of an archived runtime. [Official repository and archive notice](https://github.com/langchain-ai/open_deep_research).

### What this suggests for the suite

These are recommendations for [Agree the startup skill suite blueprint](08-decide-suite-blueprint.md), not settled tooling choices:

1. **Keep one record guide and allow different tools per task.** The scout, investigator and future customer-connection skill share saved facts, source identities and handoff rules; they need not share an identical tool list.
2. **Trial the evidence-linked idea list and YC RFS through ordinary browsing.** Compare selected leads with independently gathered sources; preserve the originating discussion rather than copying an idea list's score into our conclusions.
3. **Use existing web/browser/file tools as the initial baseline.** If repeated retrieval becomes a demonstrated problem, compare Tavily and Firecrawl on that task and choose one. Consider Crawl4AI only when local operation justifies its upkeep; Playwright MCP only when the available browser cannot do the required interaction.
4. **Evaluate GPT Researcher separately from retrieval tools.** It adds another research process, so judge citation accuracy, source diversity, cost and ease of reconciliation before deciding it saves work.
5. **Customer-connection preparation does not currently establish a need for a CRM or outreach MCP.** The useful first capability is to find relevant communities/organizations, identify a plausible route to conversations, draft questions and accept the user's results. Source browsing and the opportunity files can support that. No examined tool established a shortcut to trustworthy customer access; sending messages, collecting leads at scale or running campaigns is outside this research authorization.

Before adopting a candidate, a small authorized trial should use one real opportunity and check original-source accessibility, country relevance, repeated evidence, dated pricing, contradictions and saved-output compatibility against the existing workflow. A tool should earn its place by improving that work. This research adds no installation, purchase, account or outreach requirement to closing the shared-records decision.
