# Startup skill suite intake

The user wants a new set of skills and supporting resources for finding and assessing startup opportunities, including business model, costs, customers, marketing, and sales. This records the request and open scope questions before charting the wayfinder map; it is not an agreed specification or a completed map.

## Stated direction

- Design new skills for startups. The copied passive-site materials are motivation and reference, not a specification to port or lightly rename.
- Carefully review the skills and scripts in `my_old_resoures/`. The spelling is the actual folder name.
- Keep geography open: India, the US, and other markets are eligible according to the idea. Do not inherit the old US default.
- Cover scouting and validation as well as business model, costing, customers, marketing, sales, and other necessary startup considerations. The final skill boundaries remain undecided.
- Use wayfinder and this repository's local Markdown issue tracker to chart the work.

## Future maps to remember

1. After the startup skill suite effort, explore automation that builds the infrastructure appropriate to a selected idea.
2. In a later effort, work on acquiring the first customer.

Those future execution efforts should remain separate from this map. The current map still needs to decide what customer research, validation experiment design, marketing strategy, and sales planning the skill suite should support; postponing customer acquisition does not answer those design questions.

## Questions pending with the user

- Should this map end with an agreed blueprint for the skills and resources, or explicitly include implementing and testing them? Wayfinder defaults to planning. The recommendation presented was a blueprint with implementation as a separate step; this has not been accepted.
- Should eligible business types stay open and be assessed against the founder's circumstances, focus on software and digital services, or explicitly include physical products and substantial operations from the start? The recommendation presented was to keep them open; this has not been accepted.
- What is the starting founder situation: solo or team, available time, money at risk before revenue, and preference for a profitable independent business versus raising investment? These are unknown, not inherited from the passive-site project.
