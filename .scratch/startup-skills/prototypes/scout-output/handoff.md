# What a chosen idea passes to the investigator

**FICTIONAL PROTOTYPE. This shows a hypothetical user choice, not the current user's selection or real customer evidence.**

Example user instruction: “Investigate invoice follow-up for small US agencies, especially disputed invoices.”

Example next invocation: `Use startup-opportunity-investigator for opportunities/agency-invoice-follow-up/ and investigate whether dispute follow-up leaves a useful gap.` This illustrates the future skill contract; it does not imply that skill is already installed.

## What goes with it

| Information | Saved record or question |
| --- | --- |
| Identity and current proposition | [Current summary](opportunities/agency-invoice-follow-up/summary.md): same small-agency customer/problem, possible dispute-focused version. |
| Evidence and opposing findings | [E1–E4 and claim assessments](opportunities/agency-invoice-follow-up/research.md): reported work, a feature that already exists, and a newer same-customer account. No purchase evidence. |
| Search context and unfinished work | [Original run](scouting-runs/01-open-discovery.md) and [continuation](scouting-runs/02-continuation.md): current feature check and alternative-source follow-up incomplete. |
| Commercial questions | Who uses it, who pays and approves buying, what subscription or other offer could fit, reasons to switch, and how customers could be reached. Model and access are assumptions. |
| Delivery questions | Integration permissions, access to billing data, human review, provider/hosting charges, support burden and practical founder effort. No sourced budget or timeline. |
| Highest-impact next work | Recheck current alternatives; identify unresolved dispute work; prepare a focused customer-test plan for any remaining gap. |
| Decision authority | Record the user's choice as selection history. It does not increase confidence in demand or remove conflicting evidence. |

The investigator reads current records, follows evidence links, and saves deeper findings and proposed tests in the same opportunity folder. It does not need the previous chat. Detailed customer-test design belongs to that investigation, not to this scout report.

No messages, customer tests or purchases are executed. This file is a handoff example, not a completed investigation or a new source of facts.
