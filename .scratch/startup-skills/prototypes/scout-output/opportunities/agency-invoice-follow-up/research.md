# Agency invoice follow-up — example evidence

**FICTIONAL FIXTURE. All entries and source names are invented. No external citations are claimed.** Currency context for any future pricing is USD; there are no observed prices here. Observation/publication/retrieval dates are unknown because these are fixtures, not retrieved sources. Scenario days denote only ordering.

## E1 — Manual chasing

Mock original account from a small US agency owner: overdue invoices require repeated emails. Scope: one reported workflow; amount lost, purchasing authority and representativeness unverified. Supports a manual-work claim; does not establish willingness to pay. Introduced on scenario day 1.

## E2 — Disputed invoices

A second, separately invented US agency account attributes delays to disputed work and client approval, rather than forgotten reminders. Supports a possible different problem; challenges the assumption that more reminders solve late payment. Introduced on day 1. Independence is stipulated for the fixture only.

## E3 — Incumbent reminder capability

Mock feature page for fictional billing product LedgerNest describes automatic reminders. Supports advertised capability, not successful customer use. Introduced on day 1. Current feature limits and price require rechecking; no real product is being described.

## E4 — Newer account of existing reminders

Mock follow-up to the E1 account says the agency enabled LedgerNest reminders and now sends fewer manual messages; disputes remain. Received on scenario day 2 by a hypothetical later investigation. This is the same agency, not another independent customer. No payment, time-saving measurement or actual adoption has been observed outside the fictional scenario.
