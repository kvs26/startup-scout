# Maintenance handovers for small Indian factories

**FICTIONAL PROTOTYPE.** Scenario day-1 findings; not reassessed during the day-3 continuation.

Customer/problem: supervisors passing maintenance information between shifts. A plant owner is the possible payer; setup plus ongoing support is an untested model. One mock account reports missing notes while another finds paper adequate. [Evidence, disagreement and limits](research.md).

Searchable handovers might improve retrieval; paper logs, messages and existing maintenance software remain alternatives. Site work and technical/operating dependencies could demand substantial solo effort. All costs are unpriced assumptions. No idea is removed solely because it is demanding.

**User selection:** none. **Next investigation:** understand a real handover, check existing alternatives, determine purchasing authority and whether a small deployment and customer access are practical.
