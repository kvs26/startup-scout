These directions originate from customer work, not an idea list. In this fictional example, a supplementary idea-list pass adds no independent evidence. No exhaustive crawl or global market coverage is claimed. The queries and abbreviated trail illustrate what to save; they do not demonstrate real research depth.

## Why this round stopped

**Incomplete in this scenario.** Agency/factory follow-ups are mostly repeating known themes, but museum-specific evidence could still change the comparison. A blocked attachment and the scenario's available research time prevent completing that follow-up. Repetition in the agency sources is not a reason to claim all markets have been covered.

Next unfinished work: recheck incumbent feature scope; find museum operator accounts through accessible alternatives; check factory access and offline requirements. No customer contact has occurred.

Unexplored: other countries and industries, local-language sources, offline customer settings and price/cost verification. This is a selective run, not evidence that the best worldwide opportunities were found.
