## Outcome and practical limits

The agreed implementation and bounded checks are complete. No unresolved design decision was exposed, so no new decision ticket is needed. The old multi-selection opening survives only in dated historical records; active instructions use single-choice prompts and explicit broad/delegated choices. Existing glossary terms suffice.

These checks do not demonstrate live worker scheduling, actual source retrieval, full-depth research of fifty ideas, reliable behavior across repeated model runs or production promotion of a real opportunity. The assessment fixtures stipulate prior depth/acceptance; they test how supplied states are interpreted. Source-access facts remain the earlier dated connection results, including unconfigured YouTube/SaaSHub routes and failed browser bootstrap. No new subscription, outreach, product or real campaign was created. The supplied-input budget inconsistency was corrected in test artifacts; no remaining implementation defect was demonstrated.
