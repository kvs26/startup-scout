## Question

How should the existing explanation workflow, summary template and production assessment change so an unfamiliar reader can picture the actual experience, understand the offer and founder work, and evaluate the recommendation without decoding jargon or a research ledger? Apply the correction to blind-presentation-party while preserving the dated evidence and unverified status of AI production.

## Comments

- 2026-10-30: User reports that earlier clarity instructions did not prevent assuming knowledge of the party-game format, unexplained “decks,” “offline player” and “bounded delivery,” a vague fridge example, excessive qualifications/calculations, and ranking by short build estimates without examining the intended AI production workflow. Instruction repair and a concrete explanation correction are authorized. Supadata/Apify work is deferred; do not resume it here.

## Answer

Resolved 2026-10-30. The [explanation guide](../../../docs/startup-skills/explaining-ideas.md) now requires reading before drafting, establishing an unfamiliar activity's rules, literal example contents and participant actions, and a draft-only retelling before finalizing. A missing actor/step, unexplained term or vague central example fails the draft. Unfamiliar or previously confusing explanations receive a separate reader check when available; the actual retelling and inferred gaps matter, not a bare clarity verdict.

The [summary template](../../../docs/startup-skills/templates/opportunity-summary.md) now groups the decision into fewer non-repeating sections. Detailed source inventories, repeated qualifications and fee scenarios stay in research; the summary retains the strongest evidence, alternative, decisive uncertainty, founder work, cost/cash implication and next step. Scout/investigator entrypoints, shared record guidance and scout assessment/report templates all use this contract.

The shared [production assessment](../../../docs/startup-skills/research-guide.md#assess-the-founders-production-workflow) now requires a concrete AI input/task/output, founder direction and correction, a quality check and evidence status, and full costs/dependencies for material sellable outputs. It separates requesting AI revisions from manually doing the creative work. The scout must examine the hardest content/quality step before treating fast software construction as a ranking advantage; unresolved production keeps that advantage conditional.

Applied to [blind-presentation-party](../../../opportunities/blind-presentation-party/summary.md): the opening explains the host/player roles and unseen slides, the fridge example shows actual content and responses, and voting purpose/turn transition are explicit proposals. The summary explains how AI would create the material and what the founder must judge; it no longer treats the old preparation estimate as an established schedule. Detailed calculations moved into [research](../../../opportunities/blind-presentation-party/research.md#c005). The complete previous summary/research wording is preserved in its [dated round](../../../opportunities/blind-presentation-party/rounds/2026-10-30-explanation-correction.md), with a [history entry](../../../opportunities/blind-presentation-party/history.md). No historical campaign was rewritten or new business research claimed.

[Verification](../checks/readability-2026-10-30.md): an independent reader reconstructed the experience and production split, identified two remaining gaps, then confirmed the targeted fixes. A separate writer successfully applied the new instructions to an unrelated bakery-service fixture, including concrete example output and an unresolved manual-reconciliation dependency. Both edited skills, local links/anchors and whitespace passed validation. These are bounded behavioral checks, not a guarantee of all future outputs or proof of customer comprehension.

Next step: use the corrected explanation and production checks on future scout/investigator outputs. Supadata/Apify connection work remains deferred at the user's direction and can resume when they return to it; no source connection work occurred in this correction. The party-game's actual AI quality, customer preference and production costs still require investigation if the user chooses that opportunity.
