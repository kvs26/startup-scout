## Answer

Resolved 2026-10-29 through the user's source-selection answers.

- Adopt the six-role starting portfolio in the preceding proposal: customer situations; products/overseas models; economics/failures; creator ideas; changes/context; target-market fit/alternatives. Select relevant sources after business areas are chosen, preserve substantial breadth, and follow useful source leads. No numerical source quota or 10–14-source cap was adopted. Retain original customer evidence, current alternatives and contrary checks under the existing assessment-depth contract. Deduplicate the underlying observation, including cross-posted creator content.
- India adaptation remains contextual to the brief. Existing technology eligibility and the fifty-deep-assessment rule remain in force; the user's emphasis on earning money strengthens the commercial objective without silently admitting ordinary offline businesses.
- Expand the proposed creator pool through [Research more YouTube creators for small opportunities and emerging demand](10-research-more-creators.md). Favor YouTube for gigs, small technology businesses and emerging ideas; Instagram is useful supplementary coverage, not a mandatory completion gate. Preserve conditional/access-limited status for uninspected creators. Use original transcripts, show notes, companion material and relevant audiovisual portions where accessible. If unavailable, give the user the original link and precise missing material; the user may supply transcripts, screenshots or copied text. Record supplied material's provenance and limits.
- Assess early attention signals and plausible distribution routes alongside monetization. Explain how a particular idea might spread, with concrete audience/channel/content or sharing experiments, effort/cost, assumptions and checks connecting attention to paying customers. Future virality is an uncertain hypothesis, not a prediction or a substitute for commercial evidence. Illustrate this in the report discussion; researching promotion does not authorize publishing, outreach or building a product.
- **2026-10-29 clarification:** earlier references to the user's desired “gigs” are superseded by the [corrected business-label decision](01-decide-business-labels.md#answer): seek small owned products, including novelty/viral-app hypotheses, with speed to revenue foremost when that track is selected. Creator accounts of services/freelancing can provide context but do not redefine the user's desired track.
- Initial verification priorities are HN, existing Tavily allowance, YouTube metadata and SaaSHub subject to terms, plus creator browser/transcript routes. They are priorities, not the entire access inventory. [Audit free and low-cost access across the selected source portfolio](11-audit-selected-source-access.md) must account for every selected named source, including sources without APIs and shared YouTube/Instagram routes. Preserve the aggregate ₹1,000/month paid-API planning cap; identify free allowances, low-cost paid options, useful content, restrictions and unknowns before any concrete paid choice. No subscription is selected by this decision.
- Missing one source does not invalidate a campaign or candidate. Try substitutes, preserve the limitation, and offer links with precise requests for user help where worthwhile. Keep claims or recommendations provisional if the missing evidence is essential to their commercial case; do not mistake access failure for negative demand evidence.
- At implementation/closeout, maintain the existing `docs/startup-skills/source-catalog.md` as the single canonical resource list, including the selected resources, roles, usable routes, costs/access states and checked dates. This extends an existing file instead of creating a competing catalog. Run records still cite material actually used; dated research artifacts remain historical evidence, not additional maintained resource lists. User feedback after completed skill testing can drive later additions.
