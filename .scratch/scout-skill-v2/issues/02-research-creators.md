## Question

Which specific YouTube and Instagram creators or series provide useful business-idea leads for this scout, and what reliable access and verification routes exist?

Research a representative mix of India and international creators, gigs/services, small software businesses and ambitious startup models. Prefer original channel/profile/video material and platform-owned API documentation. Provide roughly 8–12 specific candidates with representative content links where accessible, relevance, incentives, provenance, date/access state and a recommendation to consider or defer. Do not assume that a creator's earnings claim is verified or that every video needs to be watched in full.

Distinguish metadata, captions/transcripts and audiovisual evidence; public viewing, official APIs, account/permission requirements and access failures. Explain how to follow an idea back to the actual business/customer and detect repeated or affiliate-driven claims. Determine a workable free/public-first route without creating accounts, buying access, contacting creators or bypassing restrictions. Save one cited research artifact and a branch/commit context pointer. Source selection remains with the user.

## Answer

Resolved 2026-10-28 by Codex / creator-research. [Creator-source research](../creator-source-research.md) records ten candidates spanning Indian/global content, gigs/services, small software and larger business-model inspiration. Source-owned articles and show notes provide a usable public-first route. YouTube metadata has an official API; its caption-download endpoint requires edit permission on the video, while public Show transcript is a separate UI route. Meta confirms an authenticated professional-account API, but current Business Discovery permissions and arbitrary-creator access remain unverified.

Limitations: no full original video transcript or audiovisual segment inspected, no authenticated APIs tested, sampled Instagram profiles failed (including 429), and some named candidates are explicitly deferred pending original-content access. Creator earnings claims are not independently verified. The artifact includes provenance/deduplication and India-adaptation checks. Source adoption, scale labels and opportunity eligibility remain human decisions.
