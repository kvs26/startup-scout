## Why this round stopped

**State:** No suitable researched idea found.

Across two rounds and 17 directions, public search keeps finding either a free or cheap incumbent, or only vendor-written pain. The method is the limit: forums rarely open, practitioners rarely post, and an ambitious idea needs evidence that someone will pay, which only conversations or a small paid test can show. What public research cannot show: whether a particular buyer has an unsolved, paid problem.

## Resume or investigate

1. Change the method. Options: (a) authorize a few written learning conversations with practitioners (for example accessibility consultants or small SaaS vendors) before any build; (b) pick a customer segment the founder can reach and study, then research its work in depth; (c) widen the source mix to review sites (G2/Capterra one-star reviews of named incumbents) and Reddit through Arctic Shift.
2. If continuing with a lead, the two with the least visible cheap competitor are customs duty-drawback prep for small US/Canadian importers (one thread only) and ACR-as-code (no demand evidence).
3. Nothing in this round authorizes outreach, purchases or tests.
