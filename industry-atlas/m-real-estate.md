## Scope

Property ownership, brokerage and fee-based management are different businesses. Large portfolios have recurring data; a single landlord may use only spreadsheets and messaging.

**Evidence scope in the atlas:** RICS commercial-property respondents and Eurostat enterprises provide limited measured anchors; small residential operators may look very different. [S14](references.md#s14) [S04](references.md#s04) [S07](references.md#s07)

The D/A ranges and class-level AI examples below are analyst estimates or candidate applications. They are not measured adoption rates or proof of buyer demand.

### Division 68 — Real estate

#### Group 681 — Owned or leased real estate

<a id="class-6810"></a>
##### 6810 — Owned or leased real estate

**Maturity estimate:** D1-D3 / A0-A2; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** residential landlords; commercial property owners; self-owned property lessors; real-estate development for sale.
- **Software:** Lease or tenancy records, property accounting, work orders, portfolios and document management.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Lease extraction, maintenance triage and pricing support.
- **Adoption context:** Large portfolios integrate building and tenant data; small landlords use basic tools.
- **Watchpoint:** Review legal terms and do not automate discriminatory decisions.

#### Group 682 — Fee-based or contract real-estate services

<a id="class-6821"></a>
##### 6821 — Real-estate intermediation

**Maturity estimate:** D1-D3 / A0-A2; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** estate agents; property brokers; real-estate marketplaces; property referral agencies.
- **Software:** Listings, customer records, lead management, appointments, contracts and transactions.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Listing drafts, lead matching and valuation support.
- **Adoption context:** Larger brokers use more analytics; small agents often rely on portals and messaging.
- **Watchpoint:** Verify listings and avoid discriminatory matching.

<a id="class-6829"></a>
##### 6829 — Other fee-based real-estate activities

**Maturity estimate:** D1-D3 / A0-A2; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** property managers; real-estate appraisers; building management firms; real-estate service contractors.
- **Software:** Lease or tenancy records, property accounting, work orders, portfolios and document management.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Lease abstraction, work-order triage and valuation support.
- **Adoption context:** Large portfolios integrate building and tenant data; small landlords use basic tools.
- **Watchpoint:** Review legal terms and do not automate discriminatory decisions.
