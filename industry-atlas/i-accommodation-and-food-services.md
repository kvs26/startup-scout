# I — Accommodation and food services

[All sectors](index.md) · [Cross-sector ecosystems](ecosystems.md) · [Source PDF](Global_Industry_Business_Software_AI_Atlas_2026.pdf#page=79)

**Coverage:** 2 divisions / 9 groups / 10 classes / 37 business examples. Source pp. 79–81.

## Scope

Hotels, holiday properties, restaurants, caterers and booking platforms differ in scale and data quality. Reservation systems and checkout tools are the foundation, not evidence of autonomous operation.

**Evidence scope in the atlas:** Tourism sources provide digital context. Most detailed maturity bands here are low- or medium-confidence analytical estimates. [S17](references.md#s17) [S05](references.md#s05) [S07](references.md#s07)

The D/A ranges and class-level AI examples below are analyst estimates or candidate applications. They are not measured adoption rates or proof of buyer demand.

<a id="division-55"></a>
### Division 55 — Accommodation

#### Group 551 — Hotels and similar accommodation

<a id="class-5510"></a>
##### 5510 — Hotels and similar accommodation

**Maturity estimate:** D1-D4 / A0-A3; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** hotels; motels; resort hotels; hotel-style guesthouses.
- **Software:** Property management, reservations, channel management, housekeeping, billing and guest records.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Demand forecasts, pricing support and guest assistance.
- **Adoption context:** Chains use integrated revenue tools; small independent properties are less connected.
- **Watchpoint:** Guest privacy and accurate availability are essential.

#### Group 552 — Other short-term accommodation

<a id="class-5520"></a>
##### 5520 — Other short-term accommodation

**Maturity estimate:** D0-D3 / A0-A2; confidence: low.

- **Businesses:** holiday homes; vacation apartments; hostels; short-stay guest accommodation.
- **Software:** Booking calendars, listing channels, payments, guest messages and basic property records.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Guest-message drafting and booking forecasts.
- **Adoption context:** Platforms supply much of the technology; operator-owned AI capability is often limited.
- **Watchpoint:** Confirm booking terms and protect guest access information.

#### Group 553 — Campgrounds and recreational vehicle parks

- **AI applications / candidates:** Occupancy forecasts and guest enquiry assistance.
- **Adoption context:** Platforms supply much of the technology; operator-owned AI capability is often limited.

#### Group 554 — Accommodation intermediation

<a id="class-5540"></a>
##### 5540 — Accommodation intermediation

**Maturity estimate:** D3-D4 / A1-A3; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** online accommodation marketplaces; lodging reservation agents; accommodation booking platforms.
- **Software:** Search, listings, matching, booking, payments, customer support and seller-management systems.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Property matching, search ranking and fraud detection.
- **Adoption context:** Digital platforms may embed ranking and fraud models; offline agents can be much less digital.
- **Watchpoint:** Explain rankings; protect payments, identity and sensitive data.

#### Group 559 — Other accommodation

<a id="class-5590"></a>
##### 5590 — Other accommodation

**Maturity estimate:** D1-D3 / A0-A2; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** student residences; workers' hostels; boarding houses; other longer-stay lodging.
- **Software:** Lease or tenancy records, property accounting, work orders, portfolios and document management.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Occupancy planning and maintenance triage.
- **Adoption context:** Large portfolios integrate building and tenant data; small landlords use basic tools.
- **Watchpoint:** Review legal terms and do not automate discriminatory decisions.

<a id="division-56"></a>
### Division 56 — Food and beverage services

#### Group 561 — Restaurants and mobile food services

<a id="class-5610"></a>
##### 5610 — Restaurants and mobile food services

**Maturity estimate:** D0-D3 / A0-A2; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** restaurants; takeaway shops; street-food vendors; food trucks.
- **Software:** Checkout, ordering, reservations, kitchen displays, purchasing, inventory and staff schedules.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Demand forecasts, order assistance and waste reduction.
- **Adoption context:** Chains have richer data; stalls and small cafes may rely on messaging and payments.
- **Watchpoint:** Food safety and allergen information need verified sources.

#### Group 562 — Event catering and other food services

<a id="class-5621"></a>
##### 5621 — Event catering

**Maturity estimate:** D1-D3 / A0-A2; confidence: low.

- **Businesses:** wedding caterers; corporate event caterers; private-party caterers.
- **Software:** Event orders, menu costing, purchasing, production plans, staffing and invoicing.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Headcount-based purchasing and quote assistance.
- **Adoption context:** Larger contract caterers can forecast repeat demand; one-off events remain harder.
- **Watchpoint:** Check allergies, quantities and event-specific requirements.
