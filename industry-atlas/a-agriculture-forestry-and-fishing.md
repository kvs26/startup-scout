# A — Agriculture, forestry and fishing

[All sectors](index.md) · [Cross-sector ecosystems](ecosystems.md) · [Source PDF](Global_Industry_Business_Software_AI_Atlas_2026.pdf#page=11)

**Coverage:** 3 divisions / 14 groups / 39 classes / 144 business examples. Source pp. 11–19.

## Scope

Physical production dominates, but farm records, connected machinery and remote monitoring create very different operating models. Smallholders and industrial farms should never receive one unquestioned adoption score.

**Evidence scope in the atlas:** Farm connectivity is measurable in some countries; class-level global AI use is not. FAO provides application context, not a census of adoption. [S08](references.md#s08) [S09](references.md#s09) [S06](references.md#s06) [S07](references.md#s07)

The D/A ranges and class-level AI examples below are analyst estimates or candidate applications. They are not measured adoption rates or proof of buyer demand.

**Maturity estimate:** D0-D3 / A0-A2; confidence: medium.

- **AI applications / candidates:** Crop scouting and yield estimates.
