# H — Transportation and storage

[All sectors](index.md) · [Cross-sector ecosystems](ecosystems.md) · [Source PDF](Global_Industry_Business_Software_AI_Atlas_2026.pdf#page=73)

**Coverage:** 5 divisions / 13 groups / 23 classes / 82 business examples. Source pp. 73–78.

## Scope

Separate the carrier, terminal, warehouse, forwarder and booking intermediary. A large logistics network can run extensive AI while an owner-driver relies on navigation and a phone.

**Evidence scope in the atlas:** DHL supplies concrete operator examples. Estimates for rail, aviation, ports and small carriers are workflow-based transfers, not direct class-level measurements. [S16](references.md#s16) [S12](references.md#s12) [S07](references.md#s07)

The D/A ranges and class-level AI examples below are analyst estimates or candidate applications. They are not measured adoption rates or proof of buyer demand.

### Division 49 — Land and pipeline transport

#### Group 491 — Rail transport

<a id="class-4911"></a>
##### 4911 — Interurban passenger rail

**Maturity estimate:** D3-D4 / A1-A3; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** long-distance train operators; high-speed passenger rail operators; intercity rail services.
- **Software:** Train planning, signaling interfaces, asset maintenance, ticketing or freight and control systems.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Demand forecasts and asset-condition monitoring.
- **Adoption context:** AI is selectively added to established operational software and sensor streams.
- **Watchpoint:** Safety-critical railway decisions require certified controls.

<a id="class-4912"></a>
##### 4912 — Freight rail

- **Businesses:** rail freight carriers; bulk freight rail operators; intermodal rail operators.
- **Software:** Train planning, signaling interfaces, asset maintenance, ticketing or freight and control systems.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Wagon maintenance and train planning support.
- **Adoption context:** AI is selectively added to established operational software and sensor streams.
- **Watchpoint:** Safety-critical railway decisions require certified controls.

#### Group 492 — Other land transport

<a id="class-4921"></a>
##### 4921 — Urban and suburban passenger land transport

**Maturity estimate:** D2-D4 / A0-A3; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** city bus operators; metro operators; tram operators; suburban transit services.
- **Software:** Scheduling, ticketing, vehicle tracking, passenger information and maintenance systems.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Ridership forecasts and service disruption detection.
- **Adoption context:** Large networks are more integrated than small operators; AI focuses on prediction.
- **Watchpoint:** Preserve accessibility and service reliability.

<a id="class-4922"></a>
##### 4922 — Other passenger land transport

**Maturity estimate:** D1-D3 / A0-A2; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** taxi businesses; coach operators; school bus contractors; chauffeur services.
- **Software:** Booking, dispatch, vehicle tracking, payments, driver schedules and maintenance.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Dispatch assistance and demand forecasting.
- **Adoption context:** Platforms and fleets are more digital than independent drivers or boat operators.
- **Watchpoint:** Operational automation is not proof of safe autonomy.

<a id="class-4923"></a>
##### 4923 — Road freight

**Maturity estimate:** D1-D3 / A0-A2; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** trucking companies; owner-driver hauliers; refrigerated transport firms; household moving companies.
- **Software:** Transport management, dispatch, telematics, proof of delivery, fuel and maintenance tools.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Route planning, load matching and maintenance alerts.
- **Adoption context:** Large fleets combine datasets; owner-drivers may mainly use broker and navigation apps.
- **Watchpoint:** Respect driving limits, loading constraints and human oversight.

#### Group 493 — Pipeline transport

<a id="class-4930"></a>
##### 4930 — Pipeline transport

**Maturity estimate:** D3-D4 / A1-A3; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** oil pipeline operators; gas transmission pipelines; other bulk pipeline carriers.
- **Software:** Pipeline controls, network maps, integrity inspection, leak monitoring and maintenance systems.

- **Adoption context:** Connected networks can add anomaly detection to existing controls.
- **Watchpoint:** Maintain independent safety and incident-response systems.

### Division 52 — Warehousing and transport support

#### Group 521 — Warehousing and storage

<a id="class-5210"></a>
##### 5210 — Warehousing and storage

**Maturity estimate:** D1-D4 / A0-A3; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** general warehouses; cold stores; grain storage businesses; self-storage facilities.
- **Software:** Warehouse management, inventory, barcodes, labor plans, automation and customer billing.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Slotting, demand forecasts and visual inventory checks.
- **Adoption context:** Large fulfillment centres can use vision and robotics; small stores may use spreadsheets.
- **Watchpoint:** Check inventory accuracy and worker safety.
