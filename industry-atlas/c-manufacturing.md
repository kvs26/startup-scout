# C — Manufacturing

[All sectors](index.md) · [Cross-sector ecosystems](ecosystems.md) · [Source PDF](Global_Industry_Business_Software_AI_Atlas_2026.pdf#page=24)

**Coverage:** 24 divisions / 71 group / 141 class / 494 business examples. Source pp. 24–55.

## Scope

The catalogue covers every manufacturing division, from food and textiles to semiconductors, aircraft and repair. Software used to design and run a factory is different from software or AI embedded in its products.

**Evidence scope in the atlas:** NIST supplies manufacturing research context; Eurostat supplies selected regional software and AI benchmarks. Individual product-class ratings are estimates, not survey results. [S11](references.md#s11) [S04](references.md#s04) [S05](references.md#s05) [S30](references.md#s30)

The D/A ranges and class-level AI examples below are analyst estimates or candidate applications. They are not measured adoption rates or proof of buyer demand.

##### 1010 — Meat processing and preservation

**Maturity estimate:** D1-D3 / A0-A2; confidence: medium.

- **Software:** Batch production, recipes, inventory, quality checks, expiry tracking and food traceability.
