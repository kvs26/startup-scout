# T — Membership, repair and personal services

[All sectors](index.md) · [Cross-sector ecosystems](ecosystems.md) · [Source PDF](Global_Industry_Business_Software_AI_Atlas_2026.pdf#page=121)

**Coverage:** 3 divisions / 12 groups / 22 classes / 77 business examples. Source pp. 121–125.

## Scope

This sector includes associations, device and vehicle repair, laundry, hair and beauty, funerals and other personal services. Many are microbusinesses where simple scheduling matters more than sophisticated AI.

**Evidence scope in the atlas:** SME research supports the scale caveat; detailed personal-service bands rely mainly on transparent workflow inference, not global survey evidence. [S07](references.md#s07) [S30](references.md#s30)

The D/A ranges and class-level AI examples below are analyst estimates or candidate applications. They are not measured adoption rates or proof of buyer demand.
