# N — Professional, scientific and technical services

[All sectors](index.md) · [Cross-sector ecosystems](ecosystems.md) · [Source PDF](Global_Industry_Business_Software_AI_Atlas_2026.pdf#page=96)

**Coverage:** 7 divisions / 16 groups / 17 classes / 64 business examples. Source pp. 96–100.

## Scope

Law, accounting, consulting, engineering, research, advertising and veterinary care share knowledge work but not the same obligations. Technical expertise remains necessary even when documents become easier to produce.

**Evidence scope in the atlas:** Professional-survey results cover selected occupations. Broader engineering, creative and scientific applications use additional sector evidence and explicit analyst judgment. ## Activity catalogue

<a id="division-69"></a>
### Division 69 — Legal and accounting services

#### Group 691 — Legal services

<a id="class-6910"></a>
##### 6910 — Legal services

**Maturity estimate:** D2-D3 / A1-A3; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** law firms; solo lawyers; notaries; legal-aid practices.
- **Software:** Practice management, legal research, document systems, matter billing and e-signatures.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Research assistance, document review and drafting.
- **Adoption context:** AI adoption differs by firm and task; research and document review are frequent entry points.
- **Watchpoint:** Check every legal authority and preserve confidentiality.

#### Group 692 — Accounting, bookkeeping, audit and tax

<a id="class-6920"></a>
##### 6920 — Accounting, bookkeeping, audit and tax

**Maturity estimate:** D2-D3 / A1-A3; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** accounting firms; bookkeeping bureaus; audit practices; tax consultancies.
- **Software:** Accounting ledgers, tax, audit, practice management, documents and client portals.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Transaction classification and anomaly-led review.
- **Adoption context:** Structured records support assisted classification; judgment-heavy audit work needs review.
- **Watchpoint:** Reconcile outputs to evidence and applicable rules.
