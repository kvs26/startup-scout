**Coverage:** 4 divisions / 6 groups / 8 classes / 27 business examples. Source pp. 58–60.

## Scope

This sector spans sensor-rich treatment facilities and very small waste collectors. Keep waste collection, disposal, recovery and environmental remediation separate.

**Evidence scope in the atlas:** EPA provides connected-sewer examples; manufacturing and infrastructure research inform related operational analogies. Waste-class AI estimates are especially uncertain. [S13](references.md#s13) [S11](references.md#s11) [S06](references.md#s06)

The D/A ranges and class-level AI examples below are analyst estimates or candidate applications. They are not measured adoption rates or proof of buyer demand.

### Division 36 — Water collection, treatment and supply

#### Group 360 — Water collection, treatment and supply

<a id="class-3600"></a>
##### 3600 — Water collection, treatment and supply

**Maturity estimate:** D1-D4 / A0-A2; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** municipal water suppliers; private water utilities; desalination operators; bulk water suppliers.
- **Software:** Network maps, treatment controls, sensors, work orders, billing and water-quality records.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Leak alerts and treatment-process anomalies.
- **Adoption context:** Large networks can use smart monitoring; smaller operators may lack reliable instrumentation.
- **Watchpoint:** Protect public health and verify sensor data.

### Division 38 — Waste collection, treatment, disposal and recovery

#### Group 381 — Waste collection

<a id="class-3811"></a>
##### 3811 — Non-hazardous waste collection

**Maturity estimate:** D1-D3 / A0-A2; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** municipal waste collectors; commercial refuse firms; recycling collection businesses.
- **Software:** Fleet tracking, route plans, customer accounts, bin records and weighing systems.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Route planning and contamination-image checks.
- **Adoption context:** AI is more feasible in fleet-scale operations than informal collection.
- **Watchpoint:** Routes and image tools must respect privacy and safety.

<a id="class-3812"></a>
##### 3812 — Hazardous waste collection

**Maturity estimate:** D2-D3 / A0-A2; confidence: low.

- **Businesses:** licensed medical-waste collectors; hazardous industrial-waste collectors; hazardous-material collection firms.
- **Software:** Waste manifests, permits, chain-of-custody records, transport tracking and asset systems.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Manifest checking and collection planning.
- **Adoption context:** AI is mainly supervised document and inspection support, not autonomous handling.
- **Watchpoint:** Licensed procedures and traceability remain essential.

#### Group 382 — Waste treatment and disposal

<a id="class-3821"></a>
##### 3821 — Non-hazardous waste treatment and disposal

**Maturity estimate:** D2-D3 / A0-A2; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** landfill operators; non-hazardous incinerators; organic-waste treatment firms.
- **Software:** Weighbridge systems, plant controls, maintenance, environmental monitoring and compliance records.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Waste-stream monitoring and asset reliability.
- **Adoption context:** Connected facilities can add analytics; deployment is uneven across sites.
- **Watchpoint:** Maintain independent environmental and safety monitoring.

<a id="class-3822"></a>
##### 3822 — Hazardous waste treatment and disposal

**Maturity estimate:** D2-D3 / A0-A2; confidence: low.

- **Businesses:** licensed hazardous-waste treatment plants; medical-waste disposal firms; hazardous-waste landfill operators.
- **Software:** Waste manifests, permits, chain-of-custody records, transport tracking and asset systems.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Compliance-record review and asset monitoring.
- **Adoption context:** AI is mainly supervised document and inspection support, not autonomous handling.
- **Watchpoint:** Licensed procedures and traceability remain essential.

#### Group 383 — Materials and other waste recovery

<a id="class-3830"></a>
##### 3830 — Materials and other waste recovery

**Maturity estimate:** D1-D4 / A0-A3; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** scrap-metal recyclers; plastics recyclers; electronic-waste recovery plants; materials recovery facilities.
- **Software:** Material inventories, grading, weighing, production controls, traceability and sales systems.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Material recognition and automated sorting support.
- **Adoption context:** Industrial sorting lines can use vision; informal collectors often use basic mobile tools.
- **Watchpoint:** Distinguish model confidence from actual material purity.

<a id="division-39"></a>
### Division 39 — Remediation and other waste services

#### Group 390 — Remediation and other waste services

<a id="class-3900"></a>
##### 3900 — Remediation and other waste services

**Maturity estimate:** D1-D3 / A0-A2; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** contaminated-land remediators; spill clean-up contractors; asbestos-remediation firms; environmental cleanup businesses.
- **Software:** Site maps, laboratory results, sampling records, projects and compliance documentation.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Site-data interpretation and remediation reporting.
- **Adoption context:** AI supports interpretation and reporting where consistent site data exist.
- **Watchpoint:** Professionals must validate site-specific risks.
