# J — Publishing, broadcasting and content

[All sectors](index.md) · [Cross-sector ecosystems](ecosystems.md) · [Source PDF](Global_Industry_Business_Software_AI_Atlas_2026.pdf#page=82)

**Coverage:** 3 divisions / 7 groups / 15 classes / 56 business examples. Source pp. 82–85.

## Scope

This includes software publishing as well as books, games, music, film and distribution. Digital content production can be highly software-dependent while AI policies differ by creator, rights holder and audience.

**Evidence scope in the atlas:** Creative-sector research informs rights and workflow issues. Developer survey evidence is relevant to software work, not all media businesses. [S18](references.md#s18) [S20](references.md#s20) [S30](references.md#s30)

The D/A ranges and class-level AI examples below are analyst estimates or candidate applications. They are not measured adoption rates or proof of buyer demand.

<a id="division-58"></a>
### Division 58 — Publishing

#### Group 581 — Books, newspapers, periodicals and other publishing

<a id="class-5811"></a>
##### 5811 — Book publishing

**Maturity estimate:** D2-D4 / A1-A3; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** trade publishers; educational publishers; academic book publishers; self-publishing businesses.
- **Software:** Editorial workflow, content management, page layout, rights, distribution and subscription systems.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Metadata, editing assistance and accessible formats.
- **Adoption context:** AI can assist production, but maturity varies greatly by publisher and genre.
- **Watchpoint:** Verify facts, licenses and author agreements.

<a id="class-5812"></a>
##### 5812 — Newspaper publishing

**Maturity estimate:** D2-D4 / A1-A3; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** daily newspaper publishers; local news publishers; digital newspaper businesses.
- **Software:** Newsroom production, content management, archives, subscriptions and distribution tools.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Transcription, archive search and copy assistance.
- **Adoption context:** Text-heavy work supports AI assistance, but trustworthy reporting needs editorial control.
- **Watchpoint:** Never publish unverified generated facts or fabricated sources.

<a id="class-5813"></a>
##### 5813 — Journal and periodical publishing

**Maturity estimate:** D2-D4 / A1-A3; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** magazine publishers; scholarly journal publishers; professional periodical publishers.
- **Software:** Editorial workflow, content management, page layout, rights, distribution and subscription systems.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Metadata extraction and editorial workflow assistance.
- **Adoption context:** AI can assist production, but maturity varies greatly by publisher and genre.
- **Watchpoint:** Verify facts, licenses and author agreements.

<a id="class-5819"></a>
##### 5819 — Other publishing

**Maturity estimate:** D2-D4 / A1-A3; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** directory publishers; calendar publishers; greeting-card publishers; other information publishers.
- **Software:** Editorial workflow, content management, page layout, rights, distribution and subscription systems.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Content classification and layout assistance.
- **Adoption context:** AI can assist production, but maturity varies greatly by publisher and genre.
- **Watchpoint:** Verify facts, licenses and author agreements.

#### Group 582 — Software publishing

<a id="class-5821"></a>
##### 5821 — Video-game publishing

**Maturity estimate:** D3-D4 / A1-A3; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** console game publishers; mobile game publishers; PC game publishers; independent game publishers.
- **Software:** Release management, storefronts, analytics, customer support, rights and marketing tools.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Localization, support and player-behavior analysis.
- **Adoption context:** AI can enter support and content workflows; core game production may be handled separately.
- **Watchpoint:** Respect creator rights, age protections and player privacy.

<a id="class-5829"></a>
##### 5829 — Other software publishing

**Maturity estimate:** D4 / A1-A4; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** business SaaS publishers; cybersecurity software publishers; operating-system vendors; consumer app publishers.
- **Software:** Source control, testing, cloud infrastructure, product analytics, subscriptions and support tools.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Coding, support, analytics and embedded AI features.
- **Adoption context:** AI is central for some vendors and optional for others; ordinary software is not inherently AI.
- **Watchpoint:** Evaluate generated code, data access and model behavior.

<a id="division-59"></a>
### Division 59 — Film, video, television and sound production

#### Group 591 — Film, video and television activities

<a id="class-5911"></a>
##### 5911 — Film, video and television production

**Maturity estimate:** D2-D4 / A0-A3; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** film studios; TV production companies; video production agencies; independent video producers.
- **Software:** Production planning, scripts, editing, asset storage, rights and collaboration systems.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Transcription, previsualization and production planning.
- **Adoption context:** AI-assisted production is growing but uneven across budgets and content types.
- **Watchpoint:** Obtain likeness, voice, performer and copyright permissions.

<a id="class-5912"></a>
##### 5912 — Film, video and television post-production

**Maturity estimate:** D3-D4 / A1-A3; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** editing studios; visual-effects houses; dubbing studios; color-grading businesses.
- **Software:** Editing, visual effects, compositing, audio, asset management and rendering systems.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Rotoscoping, captioning, cleanup and localization.
- **Adoption context:** AI features can be embedded in specialist tools without replacing the whole workflow.
- **Watchpoint:** Check artifacts, provenance and contractual permissions.

<a id="class-5913"></a>
##### 5913 — Film, video and television distribution

**Maturity estimate:** D2-D3 / A0-A2; confidence: low.

- **Businesses:** film distributors; video licensing businesses; television content distributors.
- **Software:** Rights catalogues, licensing contracts, royalties, sales and digital delivery systems.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Catalogue tagging and rights-document search.
- **Adoption context:** Search and document tools are easier to adopt than fully automated rights decisions.
- **Watchpoint:** Rights conflicts require authoritative contract review.

<a id="class-5914"></a>
##### 5914 — Film projection

**Maturity estimate:** D1-D3 / A0-A2; confidence: low.

- **Businesses:** cinema chains; independent cinemas; mobile cinemas; film-screening venues.
- **Software:** Ticketing or reservations, payments, membership, staffing and event schedules.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Programming forecasts and customer enquiry assistance.
- **Adoption context:** Chains and online-first venues integrate more data than independent sites.
- **Watchpoint:** Keep customer and event information accurate.

#### Group 592 — Sound recording and music publishing

<a id="class-5920"></a>
##### 5920 — Sound recording and music publishing

**Maturity estimate:** D2-D4 / A0-A3; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** recording studios; record labels; music publishers; audio production businesses.
- **Software:** Digital audio production, editing, rights, royalties and catalogue management.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Audio restoration, transcription and catalogue tagging.
- **Adoption context:** AI features vary from restoration to generation; studios retain substantial human craft.
- **Watchpoint:** Obtain voice, music and recording permissions.

<a id="class-6031"></a>
##### 6031 — News agencies

**Maturity estimate:** D2-D4 / A1-A3; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** wire services; photo news agencies; news syndication businesses.
- **Software:** Newsroom production, content management, archives, subscriptions and distribution tools.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Archive search, translation and verification support.
- **Adoption context:** Text-heavy work supports AI assistance, but trustworthy reporting needs editorial control.
- **Watchpoint:** Never publish unverified generated facts or fabricated sources.

<a id="class-6039"></a>
##### 6039 — Social networks and other content distribution

**Maturity estimate:** D4 / A2-A4; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** social networks; creator-content distribution platforms; media sharing platforms; other content distribution services.
- **Software:** Content delivery, social graphs, recommendations, advertising, moderation and identity systems.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Recommendations, spam detection and moderation triage.
- **Adoption context:** AI can be part of the core service, but smaller communities may use simpler tools.
- **Watchpoint:** Address bias, manipulation, illegal content and user appeals.

## Focused business subtypes

These 72 PDF appendix profiles are editorial refinements of a parent class, not extra official classes. All are labeled low confidence in the source.

<a id="subtype-01"></a>
### 01 — Accounting and ERP software publishers

**Parent:** [5829](#class-5829) · D4 / A1-A3 / Estimate confidence: low · source p. 132.

- **Software:** Ledgers, purchasing, inventory, reporting, implementation and subscription operations.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Invoice extraction, reconciliation suggestions and business-document assistants.
- **Boundary / watchpoint:** Software publishing is distinct from operating an accounting practice.

<a id="subtype-02"></a>
### 02 — Human-resources and payroll software publishers

**Parent:** [5829](#class-5829) · D4 / A1-A3 / Estimate confidence: low · source p. 132.

- **Software:** Employee records, payroll rules, attendance, identity controls and application operations.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Employee support, document processing and workforce analysis.
- **Boundary / watchpoint:** Do not treat model output as authoritative payroll or employment law.

<a id="subtype-03"></a>
### 03 — Customer-service and CRM software publishers

**Parent:** [5829](#class-5829) · D4 / A1-A4 / Estimate confidence: low · source p. 132.

- **Software:** Customer databases, communication channels, workflow rules and support operations.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Conversation assistance, ticket routing and controlled task completion.
- **Boundary / watchpoint:** A vendor may offer AI even when customers do not enable it.

<a id="subtype-04"></a>
### 04 — Cybersecurity software publishers

**Parent:** [5829](#class-5829) · D4 / A1-A4 / Estimate confidence: low · source p. 132.

- **Software:** Security telemetry, detection rules, threat analysis, cloud delivery and incident tools.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Alert correlation, anomaly detection and analyst assistance.
- **Boundary / watchpoint:** False alerts and privileged automated actions require controls.

<a id="subtype-05"></a>
### 05 — Education software publishers

**Parent:** [5829](#class-5829) · D4 / A1-A4 / Estimate confidence: low · source p. 132.

- **Software:** Learning content, assessments, student integrations, subscriptions and product operations.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Tutoring support, lesson generation and reviewed feedback.
- **Boundary / watchpoint:** The publisher is not necessarily an education provider under section Q.

<a id="subtype-06"></a>
### 06 — Clinical and practice-management software publishers

**Parent:** [5829](#class-5829) · D4 / A1-A3 / Estimate confidence: low · source p. 132.

- **Software:** Patient workflows, interoperability, appointments, billing and controlled software delivery.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Documentation, scheduling assistance and validated clinical modules.
- **Boundary / watchpoint:** A software vendor is distinct from the clinical service operator.

<a id="subtype-07"></a>
### 07 — Supply-chain and logistics software publishers

**Parent:** [5829](#class-5829) · D4 / A1-A3 / Estimate confidence: low · source p. 133.

- **Software:** Shipment, inventory and planning products, customer integrations and application operations.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Demand forecasts, exception detection and planning assistance.
- **Boundary / watchpoint:** Traditional optimization and rules are not automatically AI.

<a id="subtype-08"></a>
### 08 — Vertical business SaaS publishers

**Parent:** [5829](#class-5829) · D4 / A1-A3 / Estimate confidence: low · source p. 133.

- **Software:** Industry-specific records, workflow, billing, integrations and cloud application delivery.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Domain-document search and controlled workflow assistance.
- **Boundary / watchpoint:** Examples include software for salons, farms, clinics and property managers.

<a id="subtype-09"></a>
### 09 — Design, engineering and simulation software publishers

**Parent:** [5829](#class-5829) · D4 / A1-A3 / Estimate confidence: low · source p. 133.

- **Software:** Design engines, simulation tools, customer licensing, rendering and collaboration platforms.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Design assistance, surrogate modeling and document checking.
- **Boundary / watchpoint:** Engineering outputs still require qualified validation.

<a id="subtype-10"></a>
### 10 — AI assistant and agent software publishers

**Parent:** [5829](#class-5829) · D4 / A3-A4 / Estimate confidence: low · source p. 133.

- **Software:** Model access, orchestration, evaluations, permissions, usage billing and observability.
- **AI applications / candidates:** AI is part of the product being sold rather than merely an internal aid.
- **Boundary / watchpoint:** Agent permissions and verifiable task completion are central.

<a id="subtype-44"></a>
### 44 — No-code and low-code software publishers

**Parent:** [5829](#class-5829) · D4 / A1-A4 / Estimate confidence: low · source p. 138.

- **AI applications / candidates:** Natural-language application creation and workflow assistance.
- **Boundary / watchpoint:** Generated applications need testing and access control.
