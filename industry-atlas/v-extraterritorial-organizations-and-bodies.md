## Scope

International bodies carry out programmes, finance, research and coordination across jurisdictions. Institutional and field-office technology can differ substantially.

**Evidence scope in the atlas:** UNHCR provides a primary humanitarian example. It is not representative evidence for every international organization. [S29](references.md#s29) [S23](references.md#s23) [S30](references.md#s30)

##### 9900 — Extraterritorial organizations and bodies

**Maturity estimate:** D2-D4 / A0-A2; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** United Nations bodies; international financial institutions; regional intergovernmental organizations; qualifying international organizations.
- **Software:** Programme management, grants, procurement, records, logistics and multilingual information systems.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Multilingual document search and programme-data analysis.
- **Adoption context:** Large bodies may pilot or deploy assistants; field-office capability varies.
- **Watchpoint:** Human rights, sensitive data and humanitarian accountability matter.
