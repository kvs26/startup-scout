# O — Administrative and support services

[All sectors](index.md) · [Cross-sector ecosystems](ecosystems.md) · [Source PDF](Global_Industry_Business_Software_AI_Atlas_2026.pdf#page=101)

**Coverage:** 6 divisions / 18 groups / 25 classes / 93 business examples. Source pp. 101–107.

## Scope

Rental, recruitment, travel, facilities, cleaning, call centres and business outsourcing range from manual local services to fully digital platforms. The digital base should be assessed before AI ambition.

**Evidence scope in the atlas:** Research on SMEs and document-heavy services supports workflow analysis; many individual classes lack direct measured adoption evidence. [S07](references.md#s07) [S16](references.md#s16) [S22](references.md#s22) [S30](references.md#s30)

The D/A ranges and class-level AI examples below are analyst estimates or candidate applications. They are not measured adoption rates or proof of buyer demand.

### Division 77 — Rental and leasing

#### Group 771 — Motor-vehicle rental and leasing

<a id="class-7710"></a>
##### 7710 — Motor-vehicle rental and leasing

**Maturity estimate:** D1-D3 / A0-A2; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** car-rental firms; van rental businesses; truck lessors without drivers; long-term vehicle rental firms.
- **Software:** Asset inventory, reservations, contracts, inspections, maintenance and billing.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Fleet demand, damage review and maintenance forecasting.
- **Adoption context:** Fleet-scale operators can use predictive tools; small lessors may use simple booking software.
- **Watchpoint:** Verify damage findings and contract terms.
