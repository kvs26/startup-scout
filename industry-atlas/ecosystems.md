# Cross-sector ecosystems

An ecosystem is a set of related activities, not another industry classification level. The PDF’s Appendix A (pp. 129–131) supplies these **starting** class codes. Follow the linked class profiles to see who manufactures, operates, intermediates, researches or supplies software. The routes are illustrative and are not startup opportunities or verified customer problems.
