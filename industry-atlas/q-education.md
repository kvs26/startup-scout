# Q — Education

[All sectors](index.md) · [Cross-sector ecosystems](ecosystems.md) · [Source PDF](Global_Industry_Business_Software_AI_Atlas_2026.pdf#page=110)

**Coverage:** 1 division / 6 groups / 12 classes / 42 business examples. Source pp. 110–112.

## Scope

Education levels and educational support are separated. Teacher or student use of a chatbot is not equivalent to an institution operating an approved AI learning system.

**Evidence scope in the atlas:** UNESCO provides guidance rather than prevalence estimates. Funding, connectivity, language and learner age produce substantial variation. [S24](references.md#s24) [S06](references.md#s06) [S07](references.md#s07)

The D/A ranges and class-level AI examples below are analyst estimates or candidate applications. They are not measured adoption rates or proof of buyer demand.

### Division 85 — Education

#### Group 851 — Pre-primary education

<a id="class-8510"></a>
##### 8510 — Pre-primary education

**Maturity estimate:** D0-D3 / A0-A1; confidence: low.

- **Businesses:** preschools; kindergartens; nursery schools; early-learning centres.
- **Software:** Enrollment, attendance, parent communication, staff records and billing.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Teacher preparation and administrative assistance.
- **Adoption context:** AI is more appropriate for staff preparation than unsupervised use by young children.
- **Watchpoint:** Protect children, consent and developmental needs.

#### Group 852 — Primary education

<a id="class-8520"></a>
##### 8520 — Primary education

**Maturity estimate:** D1-D3 / A0-A2; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** primary schools; elementary schools; independent primary schools.
- **Software:** Student records, learning platforms, attendance, timetables and parent communication.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Lesson preparation and teacher-reviewed learning support.
- **Adoption context:** Resources differ widely; teacher AI use is not the same as school-wide deployment.
- **Watchpoint:** Use age-appropriate tools and teacher oversight.

#### Group 856 — Educational support

<a id="class-8561"></a>
##### 8561 — Course and tutor intermediation

**Maturity estimate:** D3-D4 / A1-A3; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** tutor marketplaces; course comparison services; learning-provider matching platforms.
- **Software:** Search, listings, matching, booking, payments, customer support and seller-management systems.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Learner-tutor matching and search assistance.
- **Adoption context:** Digital platforms may embed ranking and fraud models; offline agents can be much less digital.
- **Watchpoint:** Explain rankings; protect payments, identity and sensitive data.

<a id="class-8569"></a>
##### 8569 — Other educational support

**Maturity estimate:** D1-D3 / A0-A2; confidence: low.

- **Businesses:** education consultants; student guidance services; testing support providers; educational advisory firms.
- **Software:** Student cases, course information, application records, assessments and communications.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Guidance-document search and administrative triage.
- **Adoption context:** AI usually supports information retrieval and administration.
- **Watchpoint:** Check advice and protect learner records.
