# P — Public administration, defence and social security

[All sectors](index.md) · [Cross-sector ecosystems](ecosystems.md) · [Source PDF](Global_Industry_Business_Software_AI_Atlas_2026.pdf#page=108)

**Coverage:** 1 division / 3 groups / 8 classes / 28 business examples. Source pp. 108–109.

## Scope

These are public functions, not ordinary private businesses. Administration, regulation, defence, public safety and benefits have different accountability and confidentiality requirements.

**Evidence scope in the atlas:** OECD documents government use cases. This atlas intentionally limits defence and security AI examples to administrative, documentary and support functions. [S23](references.md#s23) [S30](references.md#s30)

The D/A ranges and class-level AI examples below are analyst estimates or candidate applications. They are not measured adoption rates or proof of buyer demand.

### Division 84 — Public administration, defence and social security

#### Group 841 — State administration and economic, social and environmental policy

<a id="class-8411"></a>
##### 8411 — General public administration

**Maturity estimate:** D1-D4 / A0-A2; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** national administrative departments; municipal governments; public treasury offices; general government agencies.
- **Software:** Case management, public finance, records, identity, portals and service workflows.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Document retrieval and citizen-service assistance.
- **Adoption context:** Digital maturity varies substantially by agency and country; AI is often limited to selected services.
- **Watchpoint:** Provide accountable decisions, access controls and appeal routes.

<a id="class-8412"></a>
##### 8412 — Social-sector regulation and administration

**Maturity estimate:** D2-D3 / A0-A2; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** education regulators; health regulators; cultural service regulators; social service administration agencies.
- **Software:** Licensing, case records, inspections, reporting portals and policy documents.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Submission triage and policy-document search.
- **Adoption context:** AI can assist triage and search; binding decisions should remain accountable.
- **Watchpoint:** Check legal basis, evidence and procedural fairness.

<a id="class-8413"></a>
##### 8413 — Environmental regulation

**Maturity estimate:** D2-D4 / A0-A2; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** environment agencies; pollution-control authorities; conservation regulators.
- **Software:** Monitoring networks, maps, permits, inspections, laboratory results and case systems.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Monitoring-data analysis and compliance case triage.
- **Adoption context:** AI can support remote sensing and anomaly review, not replace enforcement evidence.
- **Watchpoint:** Verify signals through approved investigation processes.

<a id="class-8414"></a>
##### 8414 — Economic and business regulation

<a id="class-8422"></a>
##### 8422 — Defence administration and services

**Maturity estimate:** D2-D4 / A0-A2; confidence: low.

- **Businesses:** armed forces administration; defence ministries; military support establishments.
- **Software:** Secure records, logistics, procurement, staffing and controlled case or asset systems.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Administrative records, procurement review and logistics documentation only.
- **Adoption context:** This report covers administrative AI only; operational deployment is highly sensitive and uneven.
- **Watchpoint:** Human rights, security and accountable oversight are essential.

#### Group 843 — Compulsory social security

<a id="class-8430"></a>
##### 8430 — Compulsory social security

**Maturity estimate:** D2-D4 / A0-A2; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** mandatory social insurance agencies; statutory pension agencies; compulsory benefit administrators.
- **Software:** Benefit cases, identity, contributions, payments, appeals and reporting systems.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Case-document extraction and supervised error checks.
- **Adoption context:** AI may assist document processing; erroneous decisions affect livelihoods.
- **Watchpoint:** Never make unreviewable automated benefit denials.
