# L — Finance and insurance

[All sectors](index.md) · [Cross-sector ecosystems](ecosystems.md) · [Source PDF](Global_Industry_Business_Software_AI_Atlas_2026.pdf#page=89)

**Coverage:** 3 divisions / 10 groups / 24 classes / 81 business examples. Source pp. 89–94.

## Scope

Banks, lenders, funds, brokers, insurers and administrators are separate activities. A fund vehicle can outsource technology to its manager; regulated software dependence is not the same as autonomous decision-making.

**Evidence scope in the atlas:** The UK financial survey provides a strong regional anchor, but individual class bands and global extrapolations remain estimates. [S21](references.md#s21) [S30](references.md#s30)

The D/A ranges and class-level AI examples below are analyst estimates or candidate applications. They are not measured adoption rates or proof of buyer demand.

### Division 64 — Financial services other than insurance and pensions

#### Group 641 — Monetary intermediation

<a id="class-6411"></a>
##### 6411 — Central banking

**Maturity estimate:** D3-D4 / A1-A2; confidence: low.

- **Businesses:** national central banks; regional monetary authorities; reserve banks.
- **Software:** Payments infrastructure, statistical systems, supervisory data, economic models and secure records.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Economic-text analysis and supervised data-quality review.
- **Adoption context:** AI use is typically supervised and specialized rather than autonomous policy-making.
- **Watchpoint:** Maintain confidentiality and accountable monetary decisions.

<a id="class-6419"></a>
##### 6419 — Other monetary intermediation

**Maturity estimate:** D3-D4 / A1-A3; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** commercial banks; savings banks; credit unions; cooperative banks.
- **Software:** Core banking, payments, customer records, risk, compliance and financial reporting.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Fraud alerts, service assistance and credit-risk support.
- **Adoption context:** Larger banks have established fraud and risk models; smaller institutions may use vendor modules.
- **Watchpoint:** Fairness, explainability, security and human escalation matter.

### Division 66 — Auxiliary financial and insurance services

#### Group 661 — Auxiliary financial services other than insurance and pensions

<a id="class-6611"></a>
##### 6611 — Financial market administration

**Maturity estimate:** D4 / A1-A3; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** stock exchanges; derivatives exchanges; securities market infrastructure operators.
- **Software:** Market operations, clearing or settlement interfaces, surveillance, risk and resilience systems.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Market-abuse alerts and operational monitoring.
- **Adoption context:** Operational software is core; AI usually augments monitoring and analysis.
- **Watchpoint:** Preserve market integrity and tested fallback operations.

<a id="class-6612"></a>
##### 6612 — Securities and commodity brokerage

**Maturity estimate:** D3-D4 / A1-A3; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** stockbrokers; commodity brokers; derivatives brokers; online securities brokers.
- **Software:** Client accounts, orders, market data, compliance, suitability and reporting tools.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Service assistance, trade surveillance and suitability support.
- **Adoption context:** Large and digital brokers embed more analytics than small advisory practices.
- **Watchpoint:** Do not let generated advice bypass suitability controls.

<a id="class-6619"></a>
##### 6619 — Other auxiliary financial activities

**Maturity estimate:** D2-D4 / A0-A3; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** payment processors; financial transaction support providers; financial advisers; mortgage advisory services.
- **Software:** Transaction processing, identity checks, customer records, risk and document systems.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Fraud alerts and financial-document extraction.
- **Adoption context:** Payments firms may have advanced fraud models; small advisers use lighter software.
- **Watchpoint:** Match controls to regulated activity and sensitive data.

#### Group 662 — Auxiliary insurance and pension services

<a id="class-6621"></a>
##### 6621 — Risk and damage evaluation

**Maturity estimate:** D1-D3 / A0-A2; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** insurance loss adjusters; claims assessors; risk surveyors; damage appraisal firms.
- **Software:** Claims files, field inspection, photos, estimating, document management and billing.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Report extraction and image-assisted damage review.
- **Adoption context:** AI can extract reports and assist image review; site-specific judgment remains important.
- **Watchpoint:** AI estimates are not proof of cause, coverage or fraud.

<a id="class-6622"></a>
##### 6622 — Insurance agents and brokers

**Maturity estimate:** D1-D3 / A0-A2; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** independent insurance agents; commercial insurance brokers; insurance comparison intermediaries.
- **Software:** Customer records, policy comparisons, submissions, renewals and commissions.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Policy comparison and application-document assistance.
- **Adoption context:** Larger brokers integrate document tools; small agencies often use carrier portals.
- **Watchpoint:** Validate policy terms and client suitability.

<a id="class-6629"></a>
##### 6629 — Other insurance and pension support

**Maturity estimate:** D2-D3 / A0-A2; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** insurance administration providers; pension administration firms; actuarial support businesses.
- **Software:** Administration platforms, member or policy records, calculations and document workflows.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Document extraction and administration exception checks.
- **Adoption context:** Repetitive administration supports AI assistance, subject to auditability.
- **Watchpoint:** Check calculations and retain source-document trails.

#### Group 663 — Fund management

<a id="class-6630"></a>
##### 6630 — Fund management

**Maturity estimate:** D3-D4 / A1-A3; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** asset managers; mutual fund managers; private-equity managers; investment portfolio managers.
- **Software:** Portfolio and order management, research, risk, compliance and investor reporting.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Research search, portfolio analytics and compliance review.
- **Adoption context:** AI can support research and surveillance; it does not guarantee investment outperformance.
- **Watchpoint:** Control conflicts, confidential data and model risk.
