# F — Construction

[All sectors](index.md) · [Cross-sector ecosystems](ecosystems.md) · [Source PDF](Global_Industry_Business_Software_AI_Atlas_2026.pdf#page=61)

**Coverage:** 3 divisions / 9 groups / 12 classes / 46 business examples. Source pp. 61–63.

## Scope

General builders, infrastructure contractors, specialist trades and referral platforms have different software needs. Digital drawings or building models do not imply AI is routinely integrated.

**Evidence scope in the atlas:** RICS gives current professional-sample evidence; Eurostat provides a separate EU enterprise measure. Neither establishes worldwide adoption by each trade. [S14](references.md#s14) [S04](references.md#s04) [S07](references.md#s07)

The D/A ranges and class-level AI examples below are analyst estimates or candidate applications. They are not measured adoption rates or proof of buyer demand.

### Division 41 — Residential and non-residential building construction

#### Group 410 — Residential and non-residential construction

<a id="class-4100"></a>
##### 4100 — Residential and non-residential construction

**Maturity estimate:** D1-D3 / A0-A2; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** homebuilders; apartment builders; commercial building contractors; industrial-building contractors.
- **Software:** Estimating, project schedules, building models, drawings, procurement and site records.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Document search, quantity checks and progress monitoring.
- **Adoption context:** Major projects are more digital than small contractors; AI is often pilot or limited workflow use.
- **Watchpoint:** Check designs, quantities and safety claims independently.
