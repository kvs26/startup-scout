# S — Arts, sports and recreation

[All sectors](index.md) · [Cross-sector ecosystems](ecosystems.md) · [Source PDF](Global_Industry_Business_Software_AI_Atlas_2026.pdf#page=116)

**Coverage:** 4 divisions / 10 groups / 19 classes / 72 business examples. Source pp. 116–120.

## Scope

Independent creators, cultural institutions, elite sports and local recreation occupy very different digital environments. AI should not erase distinctions between original evidence, creative work and synthetic output.

**Evidence scope in the atlas:** UNESCO-convened experts and the IOC provide selected sector context. Much local recreation and gambling-class maturity remains an estimate. [S18](references.md#s18) [S28](references.md#s28) [S30](references.md#s30)

The D/A ranges and class-level AI examples below are analyst estimates or candidate applications. They are not measured adoption rates or proof of buyer demand.

### Division 90 — Arts creation and performing arts

#### Group 901 — Arts creation

<a id="class-9011"></a>
##### 9011 — Literary creation and musical composition

**Maturity estimate:** D0-D3 / A0-A2; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** independent writers; composers; lyricists; freelance scriptwriters.
- **Software:** Writing or art tools, portfolios, rights records, online sales and project administration.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Draft ideation and research assistance.
- **Adoption context:** Some creators embrace AI; others deliberately avoid it. Handmade practice remains valid.
- **Watchpoint:** Respect creative ownership and client disclosure expectations.

#### Group 902 — Performing arts

<a id="class-9020"></a>
##### 9020 — Performing arts

**Maturity estimate:** D0-D3 / A0-A2; confidence: low.

- **Businesses:** theatre companies; musicians; dance companies; independent live performers.
- **Software:** Bookings, promotion, rehearsals, ticketing interfaces and rights records.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Promotion, rehearsal administration and captioning.
- **Adoption context:** Administrative AI is easier to adopt than replacing live performance.
- **Watchpoint:** Protect performer consent and creative rights.

#### Group 903 — Support to arts creation and performing arts

<a id="class-9031"></a>
##### 9031 — Arts facilities and sites

**Maturity estimate:** D1-D3 / A0-A2; confidence: low.

- **Businesses:** theatres; concert halls; performing arts venues; arts-centre operators.
- **Software:** Ticketing or reservations, payments, membership, staffing and event schedules.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Audience forecasts and venue-service assistance.
- **Adoption context:** Chains and online-first venues integrate more data than independent sites.
- **Watchpoint:** Keep customer and event information accurate.

<a id="class-9039"></a>
##### 9039 — Other support to arts creation and performance

**Maturity estimate:** D1-D3 / A0-A2; confidence: low.

- **Businesses:** stage production services; performing arts production firms; live show technical support businesses.
- **Software:** Production schedules, stage plans, crew rosters, equipment and supplier systems.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Production schedules and technical-document assistance.
- **Adoption context:** Technical production is increasingly digital; AI is usually a supporting tool.
- **Watchpoint:** On-site safety must remain independently supervised.

<a id="division-91"></a>
### Division 91 — Libraries, archives, museums and cultural activities

#### Group 911 — Libraries and archives

<a id="class-9111"></a>
##### 9111 — Libraries

**Maturity estimate:** D1-D3 / A0-A2; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** public libraries; academic libraries; specialist libraries; subscription libraries.
- **Software:** Catalogues, circulation, digital repositories, discovery and user-service systems.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Catalogue enrichment and discovery assistance.
- **Adoption context:** Large institutions can improve discovery with AI; small libraries may lack digitized collections.
- **Watchpoint:** Show source provenance and protect reader privacy.

<a id="class-9112"></a>
##### 9112 — Archives

**Maturity estimate:** D1-D3 / A0-A2; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** public archives; business archives; historical document archives; specialist record archives.
- **Software:** Archival catalogues, scanning, preservation storage, metadata and access systems.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Handwriting recognition, metadata and document discovery.
- **Adoption context:** AI is useful only where records can be lawfully digitized and checked.
- **Watchpoint:** Retain original records and label uncertain transcription.

#### Group 912 — Museums, collections, historical sites and monuments

<a id="class-9121"></a>
##### 9121 — Museums and collections

- **AI applications / candidates:** Collection tagging and visitor information support.

#### Group 913 — Cultural heritage conservation and restoration

<a id="class-9130"></a>
##### 9130 — Cultural heritage conservation and restoration

**Maturity estimate:** D1-D3 / A0-A2; confidence: low.

- **Businesses:** art conservators; heritage restoration studios; conservation laboratories; collection-preservation businesses.
- **Software:** Condition records, images, laboratory analysis, projects and collection documents.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Condition-image comparison and documentation.
- **Adoption context:** AI can compare images and organize records; physical restoration remains specialist work.
- **Watchpoint:** Do not treat generated reconstruction as historical evidence.

#### Group 914 — Botanical and zoological gardens and nature reserves

<a id="class-9141"></a>
##### 9141 — Botanical and zoological gardens

**Maturity estimate:** D1-D3 / A0-A2; confidence: low.

- **Businesses:** zoos; botanical gardens; aquariums; wildlife display parks.
- **Software:** Animal or plant records, care schedules, ticketing, research and maintenance tools.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Animal or plant monitoring and visitor forecasts.
- **Adoption context:** AI monitoring is selective and depends on suitable sensors and labeled data.
- **Watchpoint:** Keep animal welfare and expert review central.

<a id="class-9142"></a>
##### 9142 — Nature reserves

**Maturity estimate:** D1-D3 / A0-A2; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** nature reserve operators; conservation park managers; protected-area visitor operators.
- **Software:** Habitat maps, ranger records, visitor permits, ecological databases and remote sensing.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Habitat imagery and biodiversity monitoring.
- **Adoption context:** Larger programmes can use image analysis; local sites may rely on basic tools.
- **Watchpoint:** Protect sensitive species locations and community rights.

<a id="division-92"></a>
### Division 92 — Gambling and betting

#### Group 920 — Gambling and betting

<a id="class-9200"></a>
##### 9200 — Gambling and betting

**Maturity estimate:** D2-D4 / A1-A3; confidence: low.

- **Businesses:** licensed casinos; licensed betting operators; lottery operators; regulated online gambling businesses.
- **Software:** Account or venue systems, payments, risk, compliance and game operations.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Fraud detection and responsible-gambling risk review.
- **Adoption context:** Digital operators may use sophisticated models; small licensed venues vary.
- **Watchpoint:** Prioritize legal compliance and harm prevention, not exploitation.

<a id="division-93"></a>
### Division 93 — Sports, amusement and recreation

#### Group 931 — Sports

<a id="class-9311"></a>
##### 9311 — Sports facilities

**Maturity estimate:** D1-D3 / A0-A2; confidence: low.

- **Businesses:** gyms; sports stadium operators; swimming pool operators; golf-course operators.
- **Software:** Membership, bookings, access, payments, coaching and facility maintenance tools.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Demand forecasts and facility maintenance alerts.
- **Adoption context:** Chains are more integrated than independent facilities; AI often enters through vendor apps.
- **Watchpoint:** Health and injury advice requires qualified supervision.

<a id="class-9312"></a>
##### 9312 — Sports clubs

**Maturity estimate:** D1-D4 / A0-A3; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** professional sports clubs; amateur member clubs; team sports organizations; competitive sports clubs.
- **Software:** Membership, ticketing, coaching records, video analysis and athlete management.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Performance analysis and member-service assistance.
- **Adoption context:** Elite clubs can use advanced analytics; community clubs often use spreadsheets.
- **Watchpoint:** Respect athlete privacy and limits of performance prediction.

<a id="class-9319"></a>
##### 9319 — Other sports activities

**Maturity estimate:** D1-D3 / A0-A2; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** sports event promoters; independent sports officials; sports league services; other sports support businesses.
- **Software:** Event systems, video records, scheduling, scoring support and commercial administration.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Video tagging and event-operation assistance.
- **Adoption context:** AI differs sharply between elite competition and small local events.
- **Watchpoint:** Maintain review and appeal for consequential judgments.

#### Group 932 — Amusement and recreation

<a id="class-9321"></a>
##### 9321 — Amusement and theme parks

**Maturity estimate:** D2-D4 / A0-A3; confidence: low.

- **Businesses:** theme parks; amusement parks; water parks; attraction parks.
- **Software:** Ticketing, queue systems, rides maintenance, staffing, retail and visitor analytics.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Queue forecasts and ride-maintenance alerts.
- **Adoption context:** Large parks have connected assets; smaller attractions have simpler systems.
- **Watchpoint:** Keep ride safety independent of unvalidated AI.
