# U — Household employment and own-use production

[All sectors](index.md) · [Cross-sector ecosystems](ecosystems.md) · [Source PDF](Global_Industry_Business_Software_AI_Atlas_2026.pdf#page=126)

**Coverage:** 2 divisions / 3 groups / 3 classes / 11 business examples. Source pp. 126–127.

## Scope

Included for classification completeness. Households employing domestic workers and households producing for their own use are not ordinary commercial businesses.

**Evidence scope in the atlas:** These entries describe optional household tools. Their scores must not be included in an enterprise AI-adoption calculation. [S01](references.md#s01) [S06](references.md#s06)

The D/A ranges and class-level AI examples below are analyst estimates or candidate applications. They are not measured adoption rates or proof of buyer demand.

### Division 97 — Households employing domestic personnel

#### Group 970 — Households employing domestic personnel

<a id="class-9700"></a>
##### 9700 — Households employing domestic personnel

**Maturity estimate:** D0-D2 / A0-A1; confidence: low.

- **Businesses:** households employing cleaners; households employing nannies; households employing cooks; households employing gardeners.
- **Software:** Optional payroll, payment, calendar and household task tools.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Optional household administration assistance.
- **Adoption context:** A private household is not a business; tool use may be informal or absent.
- **Watchpoint:** Respect domestic workers' rights and personal privacy.

### Division 98 — Undifferentiated household production for own use

#### Group 981 — Household goods production for own use

<a id="class-9810"></a>
##### 9810 — Household goods production for own use

**Maturity estimate:** D0-D2 / A0-A1; confidence: low.

- **Businesses:** mixed subsistence household production; undifferentiated household goods-making; own-use production households.
- **Software:** Optional household budgeting, messaging, calendars and information apps.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Occasional information assistance, not a business deployment.
- **Adoption context:** These are own-use activities, not commercial businesses; no business adoption rate is implied.
- **Watchpoint:** Do not count personal AI use as enterprise adoption.
