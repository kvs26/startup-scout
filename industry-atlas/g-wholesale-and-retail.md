# G — Wholesale and retail

[All sectors](index.md) · [Cross-sector ecosystems](ecosystems.md) · [Source PDF](Global_Industry_Business_Software_AI_Atlas_2026.pdf#page=64)

**Coverage:** 2 divisions / 17 groups / 41 class / 154 business examples. Source pp. 64–72.

## Scope

Merchants own or trade goods; agents and marketplaces intermediate. Online versus offline selling is a channel distinction, not a complete industry hierarchy. Micro-retail and large chains vary sharply.

**Evidence scope in the atlas:** Retail industry cases illustrate workflows. Regional software surveys and SME research anchor only broad directional assessments. [S15](references.md#s15) [S04](references.md#s04) [S05](references.md#s05) [S07](references.md#s07)

The D/A ranges and class-level AI examples below are analyst estimates or candidate applications. They are not measured adoption rates or proof of buyer demand.

#### Group 461 — Wholesale on a fee or contract basis

<a id="class-4610"></a>
##### 4610 — Wholesale on a fee or contract basis

**Maturity estimate:** D1-D3 / A0-A2; confidence: low.

- **Businesses:** commission merchants; import-export agents; industrial sales agents; wholesale brokers.
- **Software:** Customer records, quotations, product catalogues, commissions and trade documents.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Lead matching and quotation-document extraction.
- **Adoption context:** AI commonly enters as document assistance or search rather than autonomous negotiation.
- **Watchpoint:** Confirm product specifications and counterparties.
