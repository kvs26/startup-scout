**Coverage:** 1 division / 4 groups / 6 classes / 21 business examples. Source pp. 56–57.

## Scope

Utilities can be software-critical without being AI-native. Plant controls, network operations, billing and maintenance form the base; forecasting and anomaly detection sit above it.

**Evidence scope in the atlas:** IEA evidence supports energy use cases. Local grid size, asset type, regulation and operating data determine actual deployment. [S12](references.md#s12) [S30](references.md#s30)

The D/A ranges and class-level AI examples below are analyst estimates or candidate applications. They are not measured adoption rates or proof of buyer demand.

<a id="division-35"></a>
### Division 35 — Electricity, gas, steam and air-conditioning supply

#### Group 351 — Electricity generation, transmission and distribution

<a id="class-3511"></a>
##### 3511 — Non-renewable electricity generation

**Maturity estimate:** D3-D4 / A1-A3; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** gas-fired power stations; coal power stations; nuclear power operators; oil-fired generators.
- **Software:** Plant controls, maintenance, asset histories, dispatch, market and compliance systems.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Asset anomalies and generation forecasting.
- **Adoption context:** Sensor-rich assets support analytics; level of integration differs by operator.
- **Watchpoint:** Protect safety systems and critical infrastructure.

<a id="class-3512"></a>
##### 3512 — Renewable electricity generation

**Maturity estimate:** D2-D4 / A1-A3; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** solar-farm operators; wind-farm operators; hydropower operators; geothermal power operators.
- **Software:** Plant monitoring, weather forecasts, asset maintenance, dispatch and energy trading software.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Weather-linked generation forecasts and inspection.
- **Adoption context:** Utility-scale assets often have remote monitoring; small installations use simpler vendor dashboards.
- **Watchpoint:** Forecasts need local weather and outage information.

<a id="class-3513"></a>
##### 3513 — Electricity transmission and distribution

**Maturity estimate:** D3-D4 / A1-A3; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** grid operators; electricity distributors; electricity trading operators; EV charging-station operators.
- **Software:** Network controls, outage management, grid maps, metering, billing and asset systems.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Load forecasts and network-condition alerts.
- **Adoption context:** AI can augment load and condition analysis; safe operation requires validated controls.
- **Watchpoint:** Cybersecurity and grid reliability are non-negotiable.

#### Group 352 — Gas manufacture and distribution through mains

<a id="class-3520"></a>
##### 3520 — Gas manufacture and distribution through mains

**Maturity estimate:** D2-D4 / A0-A3; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** gas distribution utilities; manufactured-gas suppliers; district gas network operators.
- **Software:** Network maps, supervisory controls, metering, billing, maintenance and customer-service systems.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Demand forecasting and leak-alert analysis.
- **Adoption context:** Larger utilities have richer operational data; legacy infrastructure limits integration.
- **Watchpoint:** Keep verified manual and safety fallbacks.

#### Group 353 — Steam and air-conditioning supply

<a id="class-3530"></a>
##### 3530 — Steam and air-conditioning supply

**Maturity estimate:** D2-D4 / A0-A3; confidence: medium.

- **Businesses:** district heating operators; district cooling operators; industrial steam suppliers.
- **Software:** Network maps, supervisory controls, metering, billing, maintenance and customer-service systems.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Heat-demand forecasts and equipment monitoring.
- **Adoption context:** Larger utilities have richer operational data; legacy infrastructure limits integration.
- **Watchpoint:** Keep verified manual and safety fallbacks.

#### Group 354 — Electricity and natural-gas brokers and agents

<a id="class-3540"></a>
##### 3540 — Electricity and natural-gas brokers and agents

**Maturity estimate:** D2-D3 / A0-A2; confidence: low.

- **Businesses:** energy brokers; electricity procurement agents; natural-gas brokers.
- **Software:** Customer records, energy contracts, tariffs, billing analysis and procurement tools.
- **AI applications / candidates:** Contract comparison and demand analysis.
- **Adoption context:** Digital comparison is common in sophisticated firms; AI adoption is task-specific.
- **Watchpoint:** Check tariff terms and disclose uncertainty.
