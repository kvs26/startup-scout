# Startup Opportunities

The language used to discuss software, AI, and similar technology opportunities, their evidence, and decisions about pursuing them.

## Glossary

**Opportunity**:
A possible business serving particular customers, whose demand and ability to make money may still be untested.

**Unresearched lead**:
An idea that has not yet received initial research.

**Candidate**:
An idea that has received initial research and can be considered for deeper investigation, without being proven to work.

**Claim**:
A specific statement about an idea, customer, market, or result that can be checked against evidence in its stated setting.

**Evidence**:
An observation or report with an identifiable source that helps assess a particular claim, within its limits.

**Assumption**:
Something we treat as true while exploring an idea, despite not yet having enough evidence.

**Confidence**:
How strongly evidence supports a particular claim or finding, including a negative one, rather than how appealing the idea is.

**Scouting**:
Finding, researching, and comparing ideas to help choose which to investigate further.

**Opportunity investigation**:
A deeper look at a chosen idea, repeated as earlier research and new customer results reveal what to learn or test next.

**Customer connection**:
Preparing to speak with relevant people about an opportunity, to learn from them or discuss a possible sale.

**Validation**:
Checking a specific claim against relevant evidence, which may support, challenge, or leave it unanswered without proving the whole business works.

**Selection**:
The user's choice to pursue, investigate, postpone, or decline an idea, which is not itself evidence of customer demand.

**Customer-test plan**:
A proposal for checking a claim with relevant customers, saying how to gather evidence and what would change a decision; it is not a completed test.
